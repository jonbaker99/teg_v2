| 13 | Home page (**will hit production on the day**) | After TEG 19 completed, the home page showed "TEG 19 — Final Results" with dashes for Trophy, Jacket and Spoon, and no final standings; `/leaderboard` was correct. Cause: the home page's honours line reads `data/teg_winners.csv` (`webapp/deps.py` `get_tournament_state`), and nothing writes TEG 19's row on completion. `calculate_and_save_missing_winners` (`teg_analysis/analysis/history.py`) has no callers; `load_cached_winners` fills gaps in memory only. Also check why the standings panel didn't render in the complete state; issue 15's crash may be part of it. | Fixed (Batch 1A) |
| 14 | Handicaps (**will hit production on the day**) | `/handicaps` errors once TEG 19 completes: "Cannot convert non-finite values (NA or inf) to integer". Cause: `handicaps.csv` has HM and GP blank for TEG 18 but `0.0` for TEG 19 (the TEG setup roster writes 0 for non-players), so they get a TEG 19 row but no TEG 18 row, the pivot gives NaN, and `.astype(int)` fails. SN sat out both TEG 18 and 19; the 36-point rule would handle that (a missed TEG counts as its handicap), but TEG setup saved 0 for SN's TEG 19 handicap, so the result is wrong. Past non-playing regulars kept a handicap. Fix: TEG setup keeps a handicap for regulars sitting out, repair SN's TEG 19 row, exclude players with no handicap in either TEG (HM, GP), and never let one player's gap break the whole page. The home page's Next TEG handicap tiles use the same path. | Fixed (Batch 1A) |
