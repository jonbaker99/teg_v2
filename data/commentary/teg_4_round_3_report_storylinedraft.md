# Crowborough Debut Brings Best And Worst

## Jon Baker follows his worst round of the TEG 4 Trophy so far with the best net score of the day at Crowborough, built around a four-hole purple patch either side of two closing quadruple bogeys.
<!-- storyline: round -->

Jon Baker's third round at Crowborough was a study in contrasts. Round 2 had left him nine over par net, but he turned that around emphatically, closing round three three under net for a swing of twelve shots — his best net score of the day at the TEG 4 Trophy, and the round-of-the-day figure came in at 13, so his effort was not far off that mark.

The turnaround was built through the middle of the round. From the 11th to the 14th he reeled off four straight pars, gaining five shots on net par along the way, with a par at the treacherous 2nd-index 12th standing out among them.

But the round did not finish quietly. His par run ended abruptly with a quadruple bogey at the 15th, and after steadying himself again over the closing holes, a second quadruple bogey struck at the 18th. Two blow-up holes either side of four flawless ones summed up a round that swung wildly between his worst form of the week and his best.

## Gregg Williams's outward nine at Crowborough contains four separate quadruple bogeys inside six holes, producing the third-worst gross round recorded in TEG history to date and his own second-worst round on both scales.
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Gregg Williams's third round at Crowborough Beacon unravelled in the space of six holes on the front nine. After an opening double bogey at the 1st, he ran up quadruple bogeys at the 2nd, 4th and 5th, sandwiched around a double bogey at the 3rd and another at the 6th — 18 shots dropped, and five holes without a net par along the way. The 2nd, one of the four quadruples, came at the hardest hole on the course.

Williams found some form on 16 and 17, playing both to gross par, but a fourth quadruple bogey at the 15th undid any repair work. The round also cost him ground in the Wooden Spoon race, dropping him from third to fifth in the field of five after the damage at the 4th.

The round stands as the third-worst gross score recorded in TEG history, and Williams's own second-worst on both gross and net scales.

## David Mullin's 17-gross round, the second-best gross round recorded in TEG history to date, blows the TEG 4 Green Jacket race wide open even as the TEG 4 Trophy tightens to a flat tie, while the TEG 4 Wooden Spoon changes hands from Stuart Neumann to Henry Meller.
<!-- storyline: race -->

David Mullin produced a gross 17 at Crowborough Beacon, the second-best gross round recorded in TEG history to date, and it did strange things to all three races at once.

On the Trophy, the lead changed hands on net score four times through the round: Mullin took it at the 10th, Jon Baker drew level at the 11th and again at the 2nd, before Mullin pulled back level at the 15th. By the finish the two were tied outright, gap zero, after Mullin had started the day two clear. The Green Jacket tells the opposite story — Mullin still leads, but the margin has stretched from seven to 16, blown open by the sheer scale of his gross score.

The Wooden Spoon changed hands too. Stuart Neumann had occupied the bottom spot before a double bogey at the 1st-stroke-index 2nd dropped him further back, only for Henry Meller, coming off a strong second round, to collapse with a triple bogey at the 14th and slide to last himself, taking over the Spoon by four shots.
