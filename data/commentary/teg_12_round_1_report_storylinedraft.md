# The Tour Course Picks Sides

## Gregg Williams opens TEG 12 with 46 points and an 84 gross, the lowest gross round of his 40-round TEG career and a personal best at PGA Catalunya - Tour
<!-- storyline: round -->

Gregg Williams began TEG 12 at PGA Catalunya - Tour, his third visit to the course, with a gross 84, twelve over par, the lowest gross round of his 40-round TEG career and eight strokes better than his previous best at the venue. It also earned him 46 Stableford points, the best score of the field on the day, with nobody else in the six-man group threatening the mark.

The round built steadily. He went two holes, 7 and 8, without losing a gross shot, then picked up nine points across holes 12 to 14, taking three points at the par-4 13th despite a bogey there on the hardest hole on the course. A par at the 14th took him outright into the lead for the TEG 12 Trophy, and a closing par at the 18th drew him level at the top of the TEG 12 Green Jacket standings as well. By the end of the round he led both competitions outright.

## Stuart Neumann's 105 gross is the highest round ever recorded at PGA Catalunya - Tour, with 22 shots coming at the 13th and 15th alone
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Stuart Neumann's opening round at PGA Catalunya - Tour produced the highest gross score ever recorded at the course: 105, beating the previous worst of 103 set across ten prior visits. Twenty-two of those shots came from just two holes. At the 13th, the hardest hole on the course, he ran up a sextuple-bogey 10, and at the par-five 15th he added a septuple-bogey 12.

The damage was not confined to those two holes, but the round was not without its moments. Neumann gained nine Stableford points across holes 10 to 12, and a level par on the 2nd was enough to draw him level for the lead in the 2019 Trophy. He also strung together pars on the 16th and 17th without dropping a shot.

It capped an unhappy return to the course for Neumann, whose previous best gross there was 98; this round finished seven shots worse than that mark.

## Alex Baker loses ten shots to par in the first three holes, adds a 10 at the 10th, and finishes the day bottom of the TEG 12 Wooden Spoon despite a tidy last three holes
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Alex Baker's opening round at PGA Catalunya - Tour came apart before it began. He dropped ten shots to par across the first three holes, a triple bogey at the 1st followed by a quadruple bogey at the short 2nd and another triple at the 3rd, and went those three holes without a single net par. The 13th, the hardest hole on the course, brought a quadruple bogey 8, part of a run through holes 13-15 that cost him nine shots. A quintuple bogey 10 at the 10th added to the damage. The round moved him to the bottom of the TEG 12 Wooden Spoon race after his double bogey at the 14th. There was a late reprieve: pars at the 16th and 18th either side of a bogey at the 17th brought nine points from his last three holes. It was not enough to lift him off the bottom of the Spoon table, and his gross score was ten shots worse than his best at the course a TEG earlier.

## John Patterson ends day one a single point off the TEG 12 Trophy lead despite a 12 at the 4th, while the TEG 12 Green Jacket sits level between Gregg Williams and Jon Baker and Alex Baker already props up the TEG 12 Wooden Spoon
<!-- storyline: race -->

John Patterson opened the 2019 TEG 12 Trophy with a round that swung wildly between disaster and control. At the par-4 4th, one of the harder holes on the PGA Catalunya Tour course, he took a 12, eight over par, easily his worst hole of the day. He recovered to string together pars from the 8th through the 11th, four holes without dropping a gross shot, part of a stretch from the 7th that brought him 19 points and lifted him into the outright Trophy lead at the 11th. A double bogey at the 12th ended that run, but a bogey at the 15th was still enough to put him back in front. He finished the day a single point off the lead, with his 96 gross also a personal-best score on the course.

Elsewhere, the 2019 TEG 12 Green Jacket honours were shared: Jon Baker birdied the 14th to draw level at the top of the gross standings, and Gregg Williams matched him with a closing par at the 18th. In the Wooden Spoon race, Alex Baker's quadruple bogey at the par-3 2nd, the easiest hole on the course, dropped him to last place before the day was even half done.
