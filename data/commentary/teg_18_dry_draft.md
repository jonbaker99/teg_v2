# TEG 18 — Camiral (PGA Catalunya), Girona, Catalonia — DRY STORYLINE DRAFT

## OVERVIEW

Alex Baker won the Trophy on 169 Stableford points, by 8 from John Patterson. Gregg Williams won the Green Jacket on +66 gross, by 14 from David Mullin. Jon Baker finished last on 126 points and took the Wooden Spoon. Rounds 1 and 2 were played on the Tour course (its 5th and 6th TEG rounds), rounds 3 and 4 on the Stadium course (its 6th and 7th).

---

## Round 1 — Forty-Six From a Man Who Usually Holds the Spoon

Saturday, PGA Catalunya – Tour.

Alex Baker took the outright Trophy lead at the 3rd, lost it to David Mullin at the 4th, and retook it outright at the 5th. He was not headed again for the remaining 67 holes of the tournament. (b07) From the 8th he made bogey at the par-3 8th, bogey at the par-4 9th, par at the par-5 10th, par at the par-3 11th and bogey at the par-4 12th — 3, 3, 4, 4, 3 for 17 points in five holes. (b14) He then took 13 points in three: par at the par-3 14th, birdie at the par-5 15th and par at the par-3 16th. (b39) The run ended with a double bogey 6 at the par-4 17th, SI 3. He signed for 46 points, a 12-point Trophy lead.

(b08) John Patterson dropped 22 shots in eight holes: a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 3rd, a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 4th (SI 2), a double-bogey 7 at the par-5 5th, a double-bogey 6 at the par-4 6th, a triple-bogey 8 at the par-5 7th, a triple-bogey 6 at the par-3 8th, a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 9th and a double-bogey 7 at the par-5 10th. He still posted 34 points.

(b10, b61) David Mullin ran up an 11 at the par-5 15th, a sextuple bogey and his worst score relative to par on any par 5. It dropped him from second-bottom to the bottom of the Spoon race.

(b35) Gregg Williams made bogey or worse at every hole from the 2nd to the 14th — 21 over par across those 13 holes, including a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 3rd, a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 4th (SI 2), a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 7th and a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 17th — before a birdie at the par-5 15th ended the streak. He was third in the Jacket at 27 over, three back.

Jacket lead changes in the round were routine: Mullin drew level at the 3rd, Jon Baker took the lead outright at the 5th, Mullin drew level again at the 10th, and at the 16th both Alex Baker and Williams drew level. Jon Baker ended the day bottom of the Spoon table on 30 points; he led the Jacket by 2.

---

## Round 2 — The Worst Round in the Tour Course's TEG History — and the Leader Shot It

Sunday, PGA Catalunya – Tour.

(cr02) Alex Baker shot 106 gross, the highest score in 23 TEG rounds on the Tour course, exceeding the previous worst of 105 across 22 prior visits. (b11) He took a quintuple-bogey 8 at the par-3 16th, one of the easiest holes on the course at SI 17. (b36) He then birdied the par-4 17th, SI 3, to end a run of bogey-or-worse. (b05) He closed with a sextuple-bogey 11 at the par-5 18th, his worst score relative to par on any par 5. The round was still worth 41 Stableford points and his Trophy lead fell by one, to 11.

(b03) Jon Baker's hold on the Jacket lead, which had run 20 holes from R1's 5th, ended with a bogey 6 at the par-5 7th; Williams took over. Jon Baker took the outright lead back with a par at the par-4 9th, and Williams drew level at the 10th and again at the 13th. Jon Baker finished the day still 2 clear in the Jacket. (b16) He closed with 16 points in five holes: birdie at the par-3 14th, then pars at the par-5 15th, the par-3 16th, the par-4 17th (SI 3) and the par-5 18th. His 39 points moved him off the bottom of the Spoon table to 4th; Mullin took the bottom place at the 18th.

(b12) Patterson took 16 points in five from the 11th: par at the par-3 11th, par at the par-4 12th, bogey at the par-4 13th (SI 1), par at the par-3 14th and bogey at the par-5 15th. His 94 was a personal best on the Tour course.

Williams stood second in the Jacket, two back, after a round of 14 over.

---

## Round 3 — Two Holes into the Stadium, Two Competitions Settled

Monday, PGA Catalunya – Stadium.

(b09) At the par-4 2nd, Williams made par and took the Green Jacket lead outright. He did not give it back. (b31) At the same hole, Jon Baker made a triple-bogey 7 and dropped to the bottom of the Spoon race. He did not climb out. (b17) Jon Baker later ran up a quintuple-bogey 9 at the par-4 4th. He scored 28 points.

(cr01) Mullin shot 84 gross, a new Stadium course record across 27 prior TEG visits, beating the previous mark of 89. It was his own personal best there by 7 shots and 8 better than his last visit.

(b33) Alex Baker dropped 12 shots in five holes: a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 6th, a double-bogey 7 at the par-5 7th, a double-bogey 5 at the par-3 8th, a double-bogey 6 at the par-4 9th (SI 1) and a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 10th. (b04) He then took 21 points in six: par at the par-3 11th (SI 16), bogey at the par-5 12th, pars at the par-4 13th and par-4 14th, bogey at the par-5 15th (SI 18, the easiest hole) and bogey at the par-3 16th. He posted 44 points and led the Trophy by 12.

Williams shot 13 over and led the Jacket by 8. Patterson's 93 was a personal best on the Stadium course.

---

## Round 4 — The Brothers Baker Finish First and Last

Tuesday, PGA Catalunya – Stadium.

(b01) Williams closed with an 84 gross — his Stadium personal best by 8 shots, and a match for the record Mullin had set the previous day — for a round of 12 over. He won the Green Jacket at +66, by 14 from Mullin.

(b02, b06, b22) Alex Baker took a sextuple-bogey 10 at the par-4 14th and a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 17th, scored 38 points, and won the Trophy on 169, by 8.

(b13, b46, b48) Jon Baker made double bogey at five consecutive holes from the 7th — 7 at the par-5 7th, 5 at the par-3 8th, 6 at the par-4 9th (SI 1), 6 at the par-4 10th and 5 at the par-3 11th — 10 shots dropped. He went 12 holes from the 2nd without a hole better than bogey (double at the par-4 2nd, bogey at the par-5 3rd, double at the par-4 4th, bogey at the par-3 5th, bogey at the par-4 6th, the five doubles from the 7th to the 11th, bogey at the par-5 12th) before a birdie at the par-4 13th. He scored 29 points and finished on 126.

(b41) Mullin dropped 9 shots in four holes: a double-bogey 6 at the par-4 14th, a triple-bogey 8 at the par-5 15th (SI 18, the easiest hole), a double-bogey 5 at the par-3 16th and a double-bogey 6 at the par-4 17th. (b42) Patterson had a triple-bogey 7 at the 14th and a double-bogey 7 at the 15th, then a bogey at the 16th and a birdie 3 at the par-4 17th.

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Alex Baker led after every round: by 12 after R1 (46 points), by 11 after R2 (41), by 12 after R3 (44) and by 8 at the finish (38). The three lead changes all came in the opening five holes of R1 — Alex Baker at the 3rd, Mullin at the 4th, Alex Baker again at the 5th — and were routine. From R1's 5th hole he was never headed. Final margin 8 over Patterson, 169 to 161.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Jon Baker led after R1 (by 2) and after R2 (by 2). Williams was third after R1, three back on 27 over, and second after R2, two back after a 14. He took the lead outright with a par at the 2nd hole of R3 and held it to the finish, with rounds of 13 and 12 over on the Stadium. He led by 8 after R3 and won by 14 at +66. There were 10 lead changes in all, three of them outright, none in R4.

**Wooden Spoon.** Jon Baker was bottom after R1 on 30 points, moved to 4th after a 39-point R2, and returned to the bottom with a triple bogey at the 2nd hole of R3. He stayed there. Rounds of 28 and 29 points closed it out at 126. Mullin was bottom after R2; the bottom place changed 10 times in total, all in rounds 1–3.

## PLAYERS

- **Alex Baker** — Three prior Wooden Spoons, two in the last three TEGs. Led the Trophy outright from R1's 5th hole to the end; shot 106 in R2, the worst gross round in the Tour course's TEG history; won by 8 on 169.
- **Jon Baker** — Defending Trophy champion. Led the Jacket for 20 holes from R1's 5th; dropped to bottom of the Spoon race at R3's 2nd; 28 and 29 points in the closing rounds; Wooden Spoon on 126. Eight shots worse than his last visit to the Stadium.
- **Gregg Williams** — Reigning Wooden Spoon holder from TEG 17. Opened with a quadruple-bogey 9 at the Tour course's 7th; took the Jacket lead outright at R3's 2nd; 84 in R4 for a Stadium personal best by 8 shots; Green Jacket by 14, his third.
- **David Mullin** — Nine prior Green Jackets. An 11 at the Tour course's 15th in R1 and two 8s in R2; then 84 in R3, a new Stadium course record, a personal best there by 7 and 8 better than his last visit. Second in the Jacket.
- **John Patterson** — Dropped 22 shots in eight holes in R1 and still finished Trophy runner-up on 161. Personal bests at both courses (94 on the Tour in R2, 93 on the Stadium in R3) and a birdie at R4's 17th.
