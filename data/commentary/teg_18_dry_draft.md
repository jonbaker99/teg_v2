# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 18, Camiral Golf & Wellness, Girona, Catalonia (October 2025)

## OVERVIEW

Alex Baker won the Trophy with 169 Stableford points, eight clear of John Patterson (b03). Gregg Williams won the Green Jacket on +66 gross, 14 clear of David Mullin (b02). Jon Baker took the Wooden Spoon on 126 points, 23 behind Mullin (b29). Rounds 1 and 2 were played on the Tour course (its 5th and 6th TEG rounds), rounds 3 and 4 on the Stadium (its 6th and 7th).

---

## Forty-Six on Saturday: The Trophy Effectively Signed For at the Tour Course

Round 1, Saturday, PGA Catalunya – Tour.

The Trophy lead changed hands three times in the opening holes: Alex Baker went ahead at the 3rd, Mullin at the 4th, Alex Baker again at the 5th. That third change was the last of the tournament — Alex Baker led for the remaining 67 holes.

John Patterson's round contained an eight-hole stretch from the 3rd to the 10th in which he dropped 22 shots (b27): a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 3rd (b31), a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 4th, a double at the par-5 5th, a double at the par-4 6th, triples at the par-5 7th, the par-3 8th and the par-4 9th, and a double at the par-5 10th. The triple at the 4th dropped him to the bottom of the Spoon race at that point. He recovered to 34 points, with the back nine 14 points better than the front (b32).

Gregg Williams made a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 7th (b30) and a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 17th (b28, SI 3, one of the hardest). Between them he stopped a long run of dropped shots with a birdie at the par-5 15th (b37). He signed for 31 points and +27 gross.

Mullin made an 11 at the par-5 15th (b46), a sextuple bogey and his worst score relative to par on record. It dropped him from second to bottom of the Spoon race at that hole (b68).

Alex Baker took 17 points from the 8th to the 12th (b47) — bogeys at the par-3 8th and par-4 9th for three points each, then par at the par-5 10th and the par-3 11th for four each, and a bogey at the par-4 12th. He added 13 points from the 14th to the 16th (b50): par at the par-3 14th for four, birdie at the par-5 15th for five, par at the par-3 16th for four. His 46 points was the best round in the field and gave him a 12-point lead. Jon Baker led the Jacket by two and finished bottom of the Spoon table after R1.

---

## A Hundred and Six, and Still Eleven Clear

Round 2, Sunday, PGA Catalunya – Tour.

Alex Baker signed for 106 gross, a new course worst for the Tour course, exceeding the prior worst of 105 across 22 prior visits (cr02). It included an 11 at the par-5 18th (b44), a sextuple bogey and his own worst hole relative to par. He still scored 41 points and his Trophy lead moved only from 12 to 11.

Williams posted 42 points, up 11 on his R1 total and the best round in the field (b41). He drew level for the Jacket lead at the 13th, having been level at the 6th and the 10th as well; Jon Baker had taken it back outright at the 9th.

Patterson made a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 4th (b24, SI 2, one of the hardest). From the 7th to the 18th he went 12 holes without dropping a net shot (b06), a run that included four consecutive double bogeys at the par-5 7th, par-3 8th, par-4 9th and par-5 10th, then pars at the par-3 11th, par-4 12th, par-3 14th and par-5 18th. His 94 gross was a personal best at the Tour course.

Jon Baker scored 39 points, closing with eight holes without dropping a net shot from the 11th (b13) and 16 points over the last five (b16): birdie at the par-3 14th, then pars at the par-5 15th, par-3 16th, par-4 17th and par-5 18th. He led the Jacket by two after R2, with Alex Baker still leading the Trophy (b38). Mullin was bottom of the Spoon table after R2.

---

## Eighty-Four at the Stadium: Mullin Breaks the Record, Williams Takes the Jacket

Round 3, Monday, PGA Catalunya – Stadium.

Mullin shot 84 gross, a new TEG course record at the Stadium, beating the prior record of 89 across 27 prior visits (cr01). It was also a personal best, seven better than his previous 91 (TEG 11). He went the first 10 holes without dropping a net shot (b12): pars at the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 5th, 6th and 8th, bogeys at the par-4 4th, par-5 7th and par-4 9th (SI 1), and a birdie at the par-4 10th.

Williams took the Green Jacket lead outright at the 2nd, a par at the par-4 2nd (b39). His run of five straight pars from the 2nd ended with a double bogey at the par-4 6th (b18). From the 8th to the 18th he went 11 holes without dropping a net shot (b04), including a double bogey at the par-4 9th (SI 1) and bogeys at the 12th, 14th, 15th, 16th, 17th and 18th. He led the Jacket by eight at the end of the round.

Jon Baker made a quintuple-bogey 9 at the par-4 4th (b49), scored 28 points and dropped to the bottom of the Spoon table at the 2nd hole, where he stayed for the rest of the tournament.

Alex Baker took 21 points from the 11th to the 16th (b10): par at the par-3 11th for four, bogey at the par-5 12th for three, pars at the par-4 13th and par-4 14th for four each, bogey at the par-5 15th (SI 18, the easiest hole) for three, and a bogey at the par-3 16th for three. He finished with 44 points, the best round in the field, and stretched the Trophy lead back to 12. Patterson shot 93, a personal best at the Stadium.

---

## A Ten at the Fourteenth, and the Trophy Anyway

Round 4, Tuesday, PGA Catalunya – Stadium.

Alex Baker made a sextuple-bogey 10 at the par-4 14th (b34). He scored 38 points and won the Trophy by eight (b03).

Williams went the first 13 holes without dropping a net shot (b01): bogeys at the par-4 1st (SI 3), par-4 2nd, par-4 4th, par-5 7th, par-4 9th (SI 1), par-3 11th and par-4 13th; pars at the par-5 3rd, par-4 6th, par-3 8th, par-4 10th and par-5 12th; and a birdie at the par-3 5th. He signed for 84 gross, a personal best at the Stadium by eight shots on his previous 92 (TEG 12), and won the Jacket by 14 (b02, b08).

Patterson made a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 14th and a double-bogey 7 at the par-5 15th, then a bogey at the par-3 16th and a birdie at the par-4 17th for five points (b05).

Mullin dropped nine shots in four holes from the 14th (b48): double bogey at the par-4 14th, triple bogey at the par-5 15th (SI 18, the easiest hole), double bogey at the par-3 16th, double bogey at the par-4 17th.

Jon Baker scored 29 points and finished last on 126 (b29).

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Alex Baker led after every round: by 12, 11, 12 and finally eight. The only lead changes came in the opening holes of R1 — Alex Baker at the 3rd, Mullin at the 4th, Alex Baker at the 5th — after which the lead was never surrendered. The decisive round was R1: a field-best 46 points opened a gap larger than the eventual margin. A second field-best round, 44 in R3, removed the only realistic challenge. Across R2–R4 Patterson outscored him by four. Final margin: eight points, 169 to 161.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Jon Baker led after R1 and R2, by two each time. Williams was third after R1, three back, and second after R2, two back. There were 10 lead changes in all, five of them in R1 and none in R4; Williams drew level at R2 H6, H10 and H13 without going ahead. The decisive moment was R3 H2, where Williams went outright ahead for the first time (b39). He led by eight after R3 and 14 after R4, with round-by-round gross figures of +27, +14, +13 and +12 for a total of +66. He beat Mullin by 14.

**Wooden Spoon.** Jon Baker was bottom after R1, Mullin after R2, then Jon Baker after R3 and R4. There were 10 changes at the bottom, five of them in R1. The decisive drop was R3 H2, after which Jon Baker was bottom for the remainder. His round scores were 30, 39, 28 and 29 — below the field median in all four. R3 was the decisive round: 28 points against Mullin's course-record 84. Final gap: 23 points behind Mullin.

## PLAYERS

- **Alex Baker** — Trophy winner on 169 points (46, 41, 44, 38), the best round in the field in R1 and R3; also set the Tour course gross worst with 106 in R2, including an 11 at the 18th, and made a 10 at the Stadium's 14th in R4. Three prior Wooden Spoons, including two of the last three TEGs. Second-best score of his TEG career.
- **Gregg Williams** — Green Jacket winner on +66 by 14. Reigning Wooden Spoon holder from TEG 17. Opened with 31 points and +27 in R1, including a 9 at the 7th and an 8 at the 17th; best round in the field in R2 with 42 points; took the gross lead outright at R3 H2; closed with 84 at the Stadium, a personal best by eight shots.
- **Jon Baker** — Defending Trophy and Jacket champion. Led the gross after R1 and R2, then scored 28 and 29 in the last two rounds, below the field median in all four. Made a 9 at the Stadium's 4th in R3. Wooden Spoon on 126 points.
- **John Patterson** — Runner-up in the Trophy on 161. Dropped 22 shots in eight holes in R1, then recorded personal bests at both courses: 94 at the Tour in R2 and 93 at the Stadium in R3. Outscored Alex Baker across R2–R4 by four points.
- **David Mullin** — Made an 11 at the Tour's 15th in R1 and fell to the bottom of the Spoon race; set a new Stadium TEG course record of 84 in R3, seven better than his previous personal best. Second in the Green Jacket, 14 back, and second on the Trophy metric ahead of Jon Baker by 23.
