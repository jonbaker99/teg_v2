# The Double at the Stadium

## Jon Baker overturns a two-point deficit on the final day to win the TEG 11 Trophy and the TEG 11 Green Jacket together, the third double in TEG history
<!-- storyline: race -->

Jon Baker completed the TEG 11 double on the final day, overturning a two-point deficit to win the 2018 TEG 11 Trophy while also taking the TEG 11 Green Jacket, whose lead he had held unchanged into the last round. It was only the third double in TEG history, following those in TEG 2 and TEG 4.

The turnaround was built on Alex Baker's collapse. He posted his worst round to date, 27 points, bleeding shots in three separate stretches — holes 4-6, 12-14 and 16-18 — and adding quadruple bogeys at the 9th, the hardest hole on the course, and the 17th. Jon Baker drew level for the Trophy lead at the 6th despite a double bogey of his own, before pulling away to win by six points. His own round was not spotless — a quadruple bogey at the 13th intervened — but a steady two-hole stretch of pars at the 9th and 10th kept him ahead when it mattered.

## David Mullin shoots his best ever round at the Stadium Course, matches Jon Baker's gross score for the day, and still collects the TEG 11 Wooden Spoon by ten
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

David Mullin produced his best-ever round on PGA Catalunya's Stadium Course in the final round of TEG 11, a gross 91 that shaved a shot off his previous best there and matched the lowest gross score of the day — a mark also returned by Jon Baker, who completed the double by taking both the TEG 11 Trophy and the TEG 11 Green Jacket. Mullin opened superbly, three straight pars through the first three holes worth nine Stableford points, briefly drawing level with Baker for the Green Jacket (Gross) lead. But the round then unravelled: a triple bogey on the 15th followed by doubles on the 16th and 17th bled seven shots in three holes, without a single net par among them. That collapse wiped out the good work, and despite matching the best gross score of the day, Mullin closed out TEG 11 with the Wooden Spoon, his margin to safety having stretched from five strokes to ten.

## Gregg Williams loses eleven shots in four holes around the turn and misses second place in the TEG 11 Trophy by a single point
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

On the final day of the 2018 TEG 11 Trophy, at PGA Catalunya's Stadium course, Gregg Williams put together the round's most damaging stretch and paid for it with second place in the Trophy, missing out by a single point.

The trouble came right around the turn. A double bogey at the par-3 8th was followed by another at the par-4 9th — the hardest hole on the course — before a quintuple bogey nine at the 10th wiped out any return, and a further double bogey at the 11th completed the sequence. Four holes, eleven shots lost, and just four stableford points recovered across the stretch.

The damage cost him second place by that single point, but the round was not without reward: Williams's gross score was still his best of the week on the Stadium course, and two shots better than his previous mark there, a course personal best for the TEG. Elsewhere, the round also saw Jon Baker move into the outright Trophy lead.

## John Patterson's 36 points and a course personal best of 99 at the Stadium, thirteen shots better than his only previous visit
<!-- storyline: round -->

John Patterson shot 36 Trophy points at PGA Catalunya's Stadium course on Sunday, matching the best score recorded in the field of five, and his gross 99 was a course personal best — thirteen shots better than his only previous visit to the Stadium, back in TEG 11, when he had returned 112.

The round was anything but smooth. Patterson bled seven shots over holes 7 to 9, a stretch that included a triple bogey on the first, the hardest hole on the course, and then lost a further eight shots over holes 12 to 14, with a triple bogey on the par-5 12th and another on the 14th. In between he found a rare pocket of stability, playing 16 and 17 in level par. Even with those collapses, Patterson closed to within eight shots of the TEG 20 Trophy lead having trailed by as much as sixteen earlier in the round.
