# No News From Boavista

## The round that produced no headline act
<!-- storyline: round -->

The dispatch from this round of TEG 2 in the Algarve is short, because there is nothing in it. No score from the day stands out. No streak, no collapse, nothing that ranks among the best or worst of the competition. Nobody broke new ground. No single act demanded attention above the rest of the scores returned.

The absence is itself a finding. The field moved through the course in an orderly fashion. Nobody seized control of the leaderboard. Nobody fell away from it. The holes were played in the order they were presented, which is the warmest thing that can be said about the afternoon.

It came later in the tournament rather than at the start, so it sat between the sharper moments elsewhere. A quiet stretch of golf in Portugal. It did its job. It left no mark on the story.

## The Trophy and Green Jacket races frozen in place
<!-- storyline: race -->

The round was played in the Algarve, Portugal, in 2009. For once it left both the 2009 Trophy and the 2009 Green Jacket races precisely where it found them.

The day arrived with the opening round already behind it. The leaderboard invited movement. Somebody closing a gap, somebody else opening one further. The invitation was declined. Not a single shift worth recording. No score arrived to disturb the order at the top of either competition. No collapse further down forced a change in the chasing pack.

So it matters for what it did not do. Neither the Trophy contenders nor the Green Jacket contenders could use it to alter their standing. The table at the close of play read exactly as it had at the start. Positions held, rather than won or lost. Both competitions were left like a hotel room the guests had been too polite to disturb.
