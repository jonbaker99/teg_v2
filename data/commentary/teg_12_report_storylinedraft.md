# Twelve, Ten, Ten — and the Trophy: John Patterson Survives Catalonia

## How John Patterson won the TEG 12 Trophy by ten with three blow-up holes, two best-in-field rounds and a personal-best 172 points
<!-- storyline: trophy -->

**Patterson takes the TEG 12 Trophy by ten**

John Patterson won the TEG 12 Trophy with 172 Stableford points, ten clear of Gregg Williams. It is the highest total he has posted in a TEG.

He got there the hard way. In the first round on the PGA Catalunya Tour Course he ran up a 12 at the 4th, a par 4 played off stroke index 2 — eight over on a single hole, the worst score relative to par he has recorded, and the worst on a par 4 in TEG history. He added a 10 at the 15th in round two and another 10 at the 3rd in round three, three holes that returned nothing at all.

The scoring around them was strong enough to absorb it. Patterson took 19 points from holes 7 to 11 in round one, four pars in a row after a bogey at the 7th, and 19 more from holes 12 to 17 on the Stadium Course in round three, including a birdie at the 15th. Both weeks' best gross rounds — 96 on the Tour Course and 95 on the Stadium — were course personal bests.

After three rounds the Trophy was level at the top, with Jon Baker leading the Green Jacket. Williams' round-three collapse at the Stadium — 12 shots dropped across holes 11 to 13, capped by an 11 at the 13th — settled the margin.

## Stuart Neumann's TEG 12: the worst round in the Tour course's history in Round 1, the only eagle of the tournament in Round 4
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

**Stuart Neumann: from the worst round in Tour course history to the tournament's only eagle**

Stuart Neumann's TEG 12 began with a round that now sits at the bottom of the record book. His 105 gross on the PGA Catalunya Tour course in Round 1 is the highest score recorded there in eleven visits, passing the previous course-worst of 103. Two holes did most of the damage: a 10 at the par-4 13th, and then a 12 at the par-5 15th — seven over on a single hole, the worst score against par of his career and the worst any player has posted in TEG history.

What followed was a steady climb. On the same course on Friday he took 13 points from holes 3 to 6, closing the run with a birdie at the 6th, and his 93 that day was a personal best on the Tour course, five strokes better than his previous mark of 98. Round 3 on the Stadium course brought another wobble — ten shots dropped across holes 14 to 17, including a 9 at the 15th.

The 15th settled the account in Round 4. After a double bogey at the 13th and a bogey at the 14th, Neumann holed out for a three on the par 5: an eagle, the only one recorded by anyone in the tournament, and the first eagle logged in any TEG. His 96 that day was a Stadium course best, four better than his last visit.

## Gregg Williams played the best gross golf of his TEG career and lost the Trophy by ten, undone by three holes in Round 3
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

**Williams' best gross week ends in the rough**

Gregg Williams arrived in Catalonia in 2019 and immediately produced the best gross round of his TEG career: +12 at PGA Catalunya's Tour Course in Round 1, an 84 that beat his previous best on the course by eight shots. Across the four rounds he finished at +76 gross, also a personal best, and his 92 on the Stadium Course in Round 4 was a course best too, seven strokes better than his last visit there.

The gross golf was good enough to put him in front. Jon Baker had led the TEG 12 Green Jacket from the 16th hole of Round 1, a run of 23 holes, and lost it to Williams at the 3rd in Round 3.

Round 3 on the Stadium Course was also where it went. Williams took nine at the par-four 4th, five over. Then, from the 11th, he played three holes in twelve over: a double bogey at the short 11th, a triple at the par-five 12th, and an eleven at the par-four 13th — seven over on one hole, the worst score he has recorded on a par four. No net par came in that stretch.

Three holes, then, cost him a week in which he otherwise played the best gross golf of his career.

## Alex Baker spent three rounds at the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race and escaped it, with his best-ever round at the Stadium
<!-- storyline: d2 -->

**Alex Baker: bottom of the pile, then a course best**

Alex Baker's week began falling apart on the second hole of the first round at PGA Catalunya's Tour course, where a 7 at the par-three — a quadruple bogey on the easiest hole on the card, stroke index 18 — dropped him to the bottom of the TEG 12 Wooden Spoon race. Worse followed on the same lap: a 10 at the par-five 10th and an 8 at the 13th, the hardest hole on the course.

The second round was a study in contrasts. Baker took 12 points from holes 2 to 5 and nine more from 13 to 15, but in between he bled 11 shots across holes 6 to 9, going double-triple-triple-triple, before a birdie at the 10th finally ended the run.

He had climbed back to fifth by the third round, on the Stadium course, only to undo it again: an 8 at the 2nd, nine shots dropped over holes 2 to 4, and then four straight holes without a net par from the 15th, the double bogey at the 17th putting him back on the bottom rung.

The Sunday reply was his best golf on the property. Baker's 93 at the Stadium was a course personal best, beating the 100 he had managed in TEG 11 and coming in 13 strokes better than his previous visit.

## Jon Baker's TEG 12 Green Jacket: twenty-three holes in front, one hole behind, then a course record 89 to finish
<!-- storyline: jacket -->

**Jon Baker's TEG 12 Green Jacket**

Jon Baker took the TEG 12 Green Jacket at +63, eight shots clear of David Mullin. It is the best gross total of his career and the third-lowest recorded in TEG history.

The lead was his from the 16th hole of the opening round at PGA Catalunya's Tour Course, and he held it for 23 holes across three rounds. It ended on the third hole of round three, the first day on the Stadium Course, where a par five at the 3rd was enough for Gregg Williams to move in front. Baker steadied on the back nine, birdieing the par-five 12th and parring the 13th, and went into the final round with the gap closed.

Sunday settled it. Baker went round the Stadium Course in 89 gross, a new course record: the previous mark was 90, set across 15 prior visits, and it was Baker's own, from TEG 11. He also went three holes without dropping a shot to par from the 14th to the 16th — par at the 14th, the par-five 15th and the short 16th — through the stretch where the round could have got away from him.

It was the fifth TEG visit to the Stadium Course and the third to Catalonia. Williams also posted a course best there, 92 in the final round.

## David Mullin, winner of eight Green Jackets, collects a second consecutive Wooden Spoon after losing ten shots in three holes
<!-- storyline: spoon -->

David Mullin finished the week with 139 points and the TEG 12 Wooden Spoon.

The tournament had not started that way. On the Tour course in round two he made four straight pars from the 5th through the 8th for 12 points, part of a 40-point round, and his 84 there was his best gross score on the Tour course in four visits. Round three, on the Stadium course, brought 30 points, a drop of ten on the previous day.

The damage that settled matters came early on Sunday. At the par-5 3rd David Mullin took nine strokes, a quadruple bogey worth no points, and followed it with an eight at the par-4 4th, another quadruple bogey and another blank. A double bogey at the short 5th made it ten shots lost in three holes and a single point from the stretch. The nine at the 3rd was the hole that moved him to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon standings, a position he did not leave.

He played the rest of the round well enough. From the 11th to the 14th he went par, birdie, par, birdie for 14 points without dropping a gross shot, and closed with a 92, his best Stadium round of the week and one stroke off the 91 he shot there in TEG 11. It was not enough to lift him off the bottom.
