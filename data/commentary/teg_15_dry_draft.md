# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 15, Lisbon Coast, Portugal, October 2022

## OVERVIEW

Gregg Williams won the Trophy on 157 Stableford points, 21 clear of Stuart Neumann. He also won the Green Jacket at +63 gross, 29 shots clear of Jon Baker — his first Green Jacket, in a competition previously won only by David Mullin (9) and Jon Baker (3). Alex Baker took the Wooden Spoon on 114 points, his second.

---

## Round 1 — Two Eights, Thirty-Nine Points: Williams Opens Five Clear

Saturday at Estoril, a new course for TEG: Portugal's first golf club, a short Mackenzie Ross parkland.

Williams opened with a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 1st (b25) and closed with another quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 18th, SI 16, one of the easiest holes on the course (b26). Between them he scored 39 points for +17 gross.

The lead exchanges were routine and early. Mullin drew level at the 2nd in both competitions. Williams drew level in the gross at the 4th and took the outright Trophy lead at the same hole. Jon Baker drew level on the Trophy at the 10th. Williams went outright top of the gross list at the 11th (b01 arc) and was not headed again.

Neumann ran up a sextuple-bogey 10 at the par-4 15th (b06) — his worst score to par to that point.

Williams ended the round leading the Trophy by 5 and the Green Jacket by 8. Alex Baker was last on the round-by-round table with 30 points.

---

## Round 2 — Three Elevens, Four Tens and One Eagle: West Cliffs Sorts the Field

Sunday at West Cliffs, also new for TEG: Cynthia Dye's dunescape, "World's Best New Course" in 2017.

The field produced three 11s and four 10s. Jon Baker took 11 at the par-4 17th (b04), his worst score to par recorded. Neumann took 11 at the par-5 13th (b07), 10 at the par-5 6th (b17). Alex Baker took 11 at the par-5 6th (b09) and 10 at the par-4 17th (b10). Mullin took 10 at the par-5 6th (b22). Williams took 10 at the par-5 15th (b13), SI 16.

The same par-5 15th produced the only eagle of the tournament: Mullin holed out in 3 for 5 points (b03, b24).

Alex Baker dropped 13 shots across holes 6-8: an 11 at the par-5 6th (+6), a 9 at the par-5 7th (+4) and a 7 at the par-4 8th (+3) (b62).

Patterson drew level at the top of the Trophy at the 3rd. The bottom of the field changed hands five times during the round: Mullin at the 2nd, Jon Baker at the 3rd, Jon Baker again at the 7th, Mullin at the 11th, Neumann at the 14th.

Williams scored 40 points for +16 gross, extending his leads to 9 on the Trophy and 14 on the Jacket. Alex Baker scored 27, again last for the round.

---

## Round 3 — Eighty-Two: Williams Rewrites Penha Longa

Monday at Penha Longa, the 2nd TEG round at this venue, the previous visit being TEG 8.

Williams shot 82 gross — a new Penha Longa course record, beating the prior record of 86 across 6 prior visits (cr01). It was a personal-best gross round of +10 (b05) and 14 shots better than the 96 he shot there in TEG 8. It was worth 44 points and pushed his Trophy lead from 9 to 18; his Jacket lead moved to 17.

Penha Longa yielded three other personal course bests: Jon Baker 85 (six better than TEG 8), Mullin 85 (by one shot), Neumann 92 (seven better).

Patterson scored 19 points across holes 3-8 (b08): par at the par-4 3rd (3 pts), bogey at the par-4 4th, SI 2, one of the hardest (3 pts), par at the par-3 5th (3 pts), par at the par-5 6th, SI 18, the easiest hole (3 pts), par at the par-3 7th (4 pts), bogey at the par-5 8th (3 pts).

Alex Baker made a double bogey at the par-4 1st (b38) and dropped to outright bottom of the Wooden Spoon table. He stayed there for the remaining 53 holes. He later took a sextuple-bogey 10 at the par-4 14th (b11) and finished the round on 29 points, last again.

---

## Round 4 — Twenty-One Clear: Williams Completes the Double at Oitavos

Tuesday at Oitavos Dunes, the 2nd TEG round at this venue after TEG 8.

Patterson took a sextuple-bogey 10 at the par-4 1st (b12) and dropped 10 shots across the first three holes: 10 at the par-4 1st (+6), 6 at the par-4 2nd (+2), 5 at the par-3 3rd (+2) (b61).

Mullin posted 22 points, the worst round of his TEG career (b28), and +33 gross, his worst gross round (b29). His 104 was 16 shots worse than the 88 he shot at Oitavos in TEG 8.

Williams shot 91 for 34 points and +20 gross — a personal best at Oitavos by two shots. Jon Baker's 94 was also a personal best by two, and Alex Baker's 103 a personal best by one. Neumann was four shots worse than his TEG 8 round here.

Final margins: Trophy by 21 (b02), Green Jacket by 29 (b01). Alex Baker scored 28, last for the fourth round in a row, and took the Spoon on 114 (b21).

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Williams led after every round: by 5, 9, 18 and 21. There were four lead changes in total, all routine and all in the first two rounds — Mullin drew level at R1 H2, Williams took the outright lead at R1 H4, Jon Baker drew level at R1 H10, Patterson drew level at R2 H3. After that Williams was never headed. Rounds of 39, 40, 44 and 34 made 157. The decisive round was R3 at Penha Longa: the record 82 for 44 points took the lead from 9 to 18. Neumann finished runner-up on 136 points equivalent margin of 21 — his best TEG finish, with no previous wins and two prior Spoons.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Three lead changes, all in R1: Mullin drew level at H2, Williams drew level at H4, Williams went outright top at H11. No lead change after that, across the remaining 61 holes. Round-end leads of 8, 14, 17 and 29. Round gross scores of +17, +16, +10, +20 for +63. Runner-up Jon Baker, 29 behind.

**Wooden Spoon.** Eight outright changes at the bottom, five of them in R2 alone (Mullin H2, Jon Baker H3, Jon Baker H7, Mullin H11, Neumann H14). Alex Baker was last on the round-by-round table from R1 onwards, and the decisive drop was the double bogey at Penha Longa's par-4 1st in R3 (b38), which put him outright bottom for the final 53 holes. Rounds of 30, 27, 29 and 28 for 114.

## PLAYERS

- **Gregg Williams** — Won both the Trophy (157) and the Green Jacket (+63), leading both from R1 to R4. Broke the Penha Longa course record with an 82 in R3, 14 shots better than his TEG 8 visit, and set an Oitavos personal best of 91 in R4. First Green Jacket; he had three prior Trophies and finishes of 3rd, 2nd, 2nd, 3rd in the previous four TEGs.
- **David Mullin** — Defending Trophy champion and holder of nine Green Jackets; won neither here. Made the tournament's only eagle at West Cliffs' par-5 15th and a 10 at the par-5 6th in the same round, a Penha Longa personal best of 85, and in R4 the worst round (22 points) and worst gross round (+33) of his TEG career, 16 shots worse than his TEG 8 round at Oitavos.
- **Stuart Neumann** — Trophy runner-up, the best finish of his TEG career, with two prior Spoons and no wins. Survived a 10 at Estoril's par-4 15th and an 11 at West Cliffs' par-5 13th; set a Penha Longa personal best of 92.
- **Jon Baker** — Reigning Spoon holder; outright bottom twice in R2 after an 11 at West Cliffs' par-4 17th, then finished Green Jacket runner-up on the back of an 85 at Penha Longa (six better than TEG 8) and a personal best 94 at Oitavos.
- **Alex Baker** — TEG 14 runner-up; last on every round-by-round table here, outright bottom from R3 H1 onwards, and Wooden Spoon on 114. Second career Spoon. Set an Oitavos personal best of 103 in R4.
- **John Patterson** — Drew level at the top of the Trophy at R2 H3; scored 19 points in six holes at Penha Longa in R3; opened R4 at Oitavos with a 10 at the par-4 1st and dropped 10 shots in three holes.
