# Won by One at Monte Rei

## Alex Baker wins the TEG 19 Trophy by one point with a 108 — the worst gross round ever recorded at Monte Rei — having lost the lead at the 17th and taken it back at the 18th
<!-- storyline: race -->

Alex Baker won the TEG 19 Trophy by a single point, 157 to Gregg Williams's 156, and he did it while posting the worst gross round ever recorded at Monte Rei: a 108, past the previous high of 106 set across ten prior visits.

He had led the Trophy since the ninth hole of Round 1 — 62 holes in front. The round itself was a procession of damage: an 8 at the 3rd, an 8 at the 7th, a 7 at the 9th, and a 10 at the 14th, his worst score on a par 3, on one of the easiest holes on the course. Holes 13 to 15 alone cost him 11 shots. A birdie at the 16th finally broke the run.

It was not enough to hold. Williams drew level at the 10th, and at the 17th, one of the hardest holes, Alex Baker made a quadruple-bogey 8 to Williams's par and lost the lead outright. A par at the last put it back. Williams took the TEG 19 Green Jacket; Alex Baker finished fifth in it.

## The par-3 14th at Monte Rei, which took 10 from Alex Baker, 7 from John Patterson and a triple bogey from Gregg Williams on the final afternoon
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

The 14th at Monte Rei is a short par 3, stroke index 17 — on paper one of the easiest holes on the course. In the fourth round it collected three of the field.

Alex Baker took 10 there, a septuple bogey and the worst score he has recorded on a par 3. It came in the middle of his worst stretch of the day: a double bogey at the 13th, the 10, then another double at the 15th, eleven shots dropped in three holes. John Patterson made 7 for a quadruple bogey, then followed it with a triple at the 15th, the hardest hole on the course. Gregg Williams escaped comparatively lightly with a triple-bogey 6, his only score worse than bogey in a run of five holes from the 11th.

Both Patterson and Williams answered immediately with a birdie at the par-5 16th. It mattered: Williams closed to within one of Alex Baker in the 2026 TEG Trophy by the finish.

## Jon Baker takes the TEG 19 Wooden Spoon by seven, finishing last in the field on the final day after leading the Green Jacket race in round one
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

**Jon Baker takes the Spoon by seven**

Jon Baker led the TEG 19 Green Jacket race for much of the opening two rounds — outright gross leader from the 5th hole of Round 1 — and finished the tournament last in the field, collecting the TEG 19 Wooden Spoon. He had held the Spoon position going into the final round with just two shots of cover; by the close the margin was seven, his 143 points leaving him adrift of David Mullin's 150 in the TEG 19 Trophy. He was second in the Trophy after Round 1 and fifth at the end.

The final round at Monte Rei did the damage. A quadruple bogey 7 at the 7th was followed by a worse spell: a triple at the 9th, the easiest hole on the course, then doubles at the 10th, 11th and 12th — nine shots gone in four holes, and four holes without a net par. A birdie at the 13th stopped it, and he closed with nine points across the 15th, 16th and 17th. His 97 was three better than his last visit here.

## Gregg Williams's 41-point, 84-gross final round at Monte Rei: the best round of the day, a course record equalled, his own course personal best, and still one point short of the Trophy
<!-- storyline: round -->

**Williams's 84 at Monte Rei: a course record matched, a Trophy missed by one**

Gregg Williams closed with 41 Stableford points and 84 gross at Monte Rei, the best round of the day in a field of five where the lowest return was 33. The 84 equalled the course record across ten prior visits, and bettered his own previous best there by a stroke.

It was not a tidy round. Bogeys at the 3rd and 4th were answered by a birdie at the 5th, another at the 6th, and an eagle at the par-5 8th. Then came seven dropped shots in five holes from the 11th — three bogeys, a triple at the par-3 14th, one of the easiest on the course, and a bogey at the 15th — before a birdie at the 16th steadied things again.

A par at the 3rd-hardest 17th put him outright top of the 2026 TEG Trophy, having trailed by as many as 11 points and sat fifth after Round 1. Alex Baker took it back at the last: 157 to 156. Williams took the Green Jacket by 14 strokes.
