# Royal Óbidos Draws Blood On Debut

## Gregg Williams survives two big numbers to post the best round of the day at Royal Óbidos
<!-- storyline: round -->

Gregg Williams produced the best round of the day at Royal Óbidos in the TEG 8 Trophy, despite two heavy blow-ups along the way. He opened with back-to-back bogeys before a par at the 3rd, then ran up a quadruple bogey at the 4th, the hardest hole on the course. He steadied with a birdie at the 8th to stop the bleeding, and from there put together the strongest form of his round, a stretch from the 7th to the 10th that gained six shots against par. A second disaster struck at the 14th, a quintuple bogey, but the back nine still finished ten shots stronger than the front. The overall score stood as the best round of the six-man field, though it did little to disturb the wider picture: Henry Meller extended his lead in the Trophy to 15, David Mullin took over the lead in the Green Jacket by 3 from Meller, and Alex Baker's gap at the foot of the Wooden Spoon widened to 7.

## Jon Baker's Trophy challenge collapses in three holes
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Jon Baker arrived at Royal Óbidos in R3 fresh from a strong second round of -7 net, but any hope of building a Trophy challenge unravelled almost immediately. At the par-5 2nd, one of the hardest holes on the course at stroke index 3, he ran up a quintuple bogey 10. He followed it straight away with a quadruple bogey 7 at the par-3 3rd. Two holes, nine shots over par between them, and the round was effectively over before it had begun.

There was a brief steadying stretch later on, two holes without dropping a gross shot at the 7th and 8th, but it came too late to repair the damage. Baker's score swung from -7 in Round 2 to +5 net in Round 3, a 12-shot reversal. In the 2014 TEG Trophy race, Henry Meller kept the overall lead, but his advantage stretched from 11 to 15 shots.

## David Mullin retakes the Green Jacket lead as Henry Meller's back nine unravels
<!-- storyline: race -->

Henry Meller went into the back nine at Royal Óbidos level with David Mullin for the TEG 16 Green Jacket lead, having drawn level on the 14th with a par on the sixth-hardest hole. But where Mullin had twice clawed back level - with a birdie on the 3rd and a par on the 16th - it was Meller who fell away. From the 15th he went three holes without a net par, a triple bogey on the par-3 15th followed by a double on the 16th and another triple on the 17th, then closed with a double bogey on the par-5 18th. Ten shots bled away in four holes, and with them the Jacket lead, which passed back to Mullin.

It capped a brutal reversal for Meller, who had shot 15-under net par in Round 2 and could only manage -1 in Round 3, a 14-shot swing. He still leads the TEG 16 Trophy, with his overnight cushion growing to 15, but the Jacket picture has flipped entirely in Mullin's favour. Stuart Neumann endured a similar collapse, following his own strong second round with a 17-shot swing the wrong way.
