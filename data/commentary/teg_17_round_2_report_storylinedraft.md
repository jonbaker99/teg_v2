# Records Fall Both Ways at West Cliffs

## David Mullin's course-record 82 headlines a stellar West Cliffs return
<!-- storyline: round -->

On Sunday 6 October, David Mullin returned to West Cliffs Golf Links for only the second time and shattered the course record, carding a gross 82 to beat the previous mark of 88, which had stood since across the six prior visits made to the course. It was a stroke fest of a turnaround from his own history there too: twelve shots better than the 94 he had shot on his only previous visit, back in TEG 15.

The round followed a poor opening effort in Round 1, where Mullin had managed just 33 Stableford points; in Round 2 he surged to 47, a swing of 14. The engine of the round was a run through holes 7 to 10, where he gained 14 points, including three straight holes — 8, 9 and 10 — without dropping a gross shot, birdieing both 9 and 10. Two more clean holes to close, 17 and 18, brought him level with Gregg Williams atop the TEG 16 Green Jacket standings.

## Alex Baker survives two quadruple bogeys to set a new West Cliffs personal best
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Alex Baker's second round at West Cliffs Golf Links swung wildly between recovery and collapse. A hot patch across holes 4-8 brought 16 stableford points, built on steady bogeys either side of a par at the 5th. That form did not last: a double bogey at the 9th opened a horror stretch through holes 10 and 11, where Baker carded quadruple bogeys at both — 8s on a pair of par 4s — bleeding 10 shots in three holes. A birdie at the 12th, the easiest hole on the course, finally broke the run of bogeys or worse, but the damage resumed at 13-15, another eight shots shed through a triple, a triple and a double bogey.

Yet the round still delivered a personal best. Baker's gross 105 beat his previous West Cliffs mark of 110 by five shots, a course personal best on only his second visit. It also lifted him clear of last place in the 2024 TEG Wooden Spoon race, handing the unwanted spot to John Patterson.

## John Patterson's collapse hands the Trophy lead to Jon Baker and drops him to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race
<!-- storyline: race -->

John Patterson's round at West Cliffs on Sunday unravelled from the 8th hole onward. Across holes 8 to 15 he dropped 27 shots without registering a single net par, a stretch that included a triple bogey at the 9th, 10th and 11th, a quintuple bogey 10 at the par-5 13th, and a sextuple bogey 11 at the par-5 15th — his worst hole relative to par of the day. Earlier he had also run up a quadruple bogey 8 at the 1st-stroke-index 3rd.

The damage added up to a gross 44, his worst round to date, and a new West Cliffs course-worst of 116, beating the previous mark of 115 set across six prior visits. It came a day after a strong opening round, a swing of 17 stableford points between rounds.

The collapse cost him the TEG 16 Trophy lead, which passes to Jon Baker after Baker moved ahead at the 1st hole and built on it with 14 points over the next four. Patterson also drops to last in the Wooden Spoon race.
