# Records Fall Both Ways at Oitavos Dunes

## Stuart Neumann's untroubled 39 points was the best round anyone posted all day, opening TEG 16 with the sort of total that wins by attrition rather than acclaim.
<!-- storyline: round -->

Stuart Neumann opened TEG 16 with 39 points at Oitavos Dunes, the best round anyone would post on the day, in a field of five where the worst score was 29. It was a quietly built total rather than a spectacular one. Neumann picked up 9 points across the first three holes, a bogey-par-bogey start on the links that still returned three points a hole, including a bogey at the par-3 3rd that was worth the same as a par elsewhere given its stroke index. That opening stretch set the tone for a round of steady accumulation rather than fireworks.

By the end of the round Neumann led the TEG 16 Trophy outright, with Gregg Williams topping the Green Jacket standings instead. It was Neumann's third visit to Oitavos Dunes, and while his gross score was two strokes better than his previous trip here, it fell short of his personal best at the course. The Stableford total, though, was enough to put him clear at the head of the field after round one.

## Alex Baker's 113 gross was not just his own worst outing at Oitavos Dunes but the worst round anyone has posted at the course across three TEG visits there.
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Alex Baker opened this year's TEG at Oitavos Dunes with a gross 113, the worst round anyone has posted at the course across three TEG visits there, beating the previous worst of 111 set over twelve prior rounds. It was also comfortably his own worst outing on the course, ten shots worse than his previous visit and thirty adrift of his own course-best 103.

The round had no single collapse but a string of them. Baker opened with a quintuple-bogey 9 at the 1st, added another quintuple at the 11th and a quadruple at the 7th, then closed with a sextuple-bogey 10 at the 17th. That finishing stretch was ruinous: 16 through 18 cost him eleven shots, with three holes yielding no net par and only two Stableford points between them. A separate three-hole bleed from the 3rd to the 5th, on a run that included the course's hardest hole, added a further seven gross shots. There was no recovery phase to speak of — just one blowup succeeding another for eighteen holes.

## Jon Baker banked a new personal best at Oitavos Dunes despite a double bogey at the 17th undoing some of his best golf of the day.
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Jon Baker put together a new personal best at Oitavos Dunes in round one of the 2023 TEG Trophy, his third visit to the course. His gross score of 92 beat the previous best of 94 he had set across two earlier visits, two shots better than his mark from his last time out here.

The improvement was built on a strong stretch through 14 to 16, where three straight pars brought nine stableford points and three holes without dropping a gross shot. That run included a par on the 15th, the easiest hole on the course, and a par-5 16th where the strokes came at no cost.

The good form did not survive the 17th, though. A double bogey there, on one of the harder holes on the course, ended the steady sequence and cost him ground he had just built. Even so, the damage was not enough to spoil the overall result: the course-best round stood.

## Gregg Williams ground out a new personal best at Oitavos Dunes to seize the early TEG 16 Green Jacket lead, with Jon Baker matching him almost shot for shot three behind.
<!-- storyline: race -->

Gregg Williams carved out a new course best at Oitavos Dunes on day one, carding 89 to beat the 91 he had managed across his two previous visits, and with it he grabbed the early lead in the TEG 16 Green Jacket. The round was built on tidy stretches of gross golf: he went two holes without dropping a shot through the 2nd and 3rd, and did it again through the short par-3 15th and the par-5 16th, leaving himself little to repair all day. His grip on the lead was briefly interrupted when David Mullin drew level for top spot after a bogey on the 6th, but Williams pulled clear again as the round progressed. Jon Baker kept himself in touch throughout, matching Williams almost shot for shot to sit just three back at the close, leaving an early two-way contest at the top of the Jacket standings heading into round two.
