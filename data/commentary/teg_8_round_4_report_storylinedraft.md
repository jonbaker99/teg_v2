# Last Day on the Dunes

## Jon Baker's final day: two shots clear in the Green Jacket overnight, passed at the second hole by David Mullin, and passed in the Trophy by Alex Baker
<!-- storyline: race -->

Jon Baker began the final round at Oitavos Dunes two shots clear in the TEG 8 Green Jacket, but the lead lasted only until the second hole, where David Mullin moved in front. Mullin did not have it easy from there — a quadruple bogey at the 11th, one of the round's harder holes, opened a run of bogeys from the 8th through the 12th before a birdie at the 13th arrested it. Two more bogeys at the 14th and 15th were answered by another birdie, at the 16th, to steady him again. Jon Baker held two clean pars at the 11th and 12th but could not find the shots to reclaim the Jacket, and Mullin's overnight lead of two swelled to six by the close.

In the TEG 8 Trophy, Alex Baker passed Jon Baker despite a torrid finish of his own — a quadruple bogey at the 15th and a quintuple bogey at the 18th, the hardest hole on the course. Gregg Williams still led the Trophy overall as the round ended, with David Mullin now on top of the Jacket.

## Henry Meller's first six holes at Oitavos Dunes: 22 gross shots dropped, a sextuple-bogey 10 at the 4th, and a finish that matched the start
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Henry Meller's fourth round at Oitavos Dunes fell apart before it had properly begun. A double bogey at the 1st was merely the mildest of six calamities: a quadruple bogey at the 2nd, a triple at the 3rd, and then a sextuple-bogey 10 at the 4th, followed immediately by a quintuple-bogey 9 at the 5th — the latter on the course's hardest hole, stroke index 1. A second double bogey at the 6th brought the carnage to a close, but by then Meller had shipped 22 gross shots in six holes without registering a single net par anywhere in the stretch.

There was a brief truce in the middle of the round — two pars in a row at the 14th and 15th — but the finish echoed the start. Holes 16 to 18 cost him nine more shots, including a quadruple bogey at the 18th, itself the second-hardest hole on the course. It capped a dramatic reversal from a strong third round, Meller's score falling by 13 stableford points from one day to the next, and left his stretch from 1-6 and his closing three holes as the round's bleakest bookends.

## Stuart Neumann's 32 points, his best round of TEG 8, arriving on the one day it could no longer save him from the Wooden Spoon
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Stuart Neumann closed out TEG 8 at Oitavos Dunes with a 32-point round, a 12-point improvement on his third-round 20 and comfortably his best score of the week. It came a day too late to matter. Neumann had already been cast as this TEG's Wooden Spoon holder, and even with the gap to the next-worst player having narrowed from 22 to 11, he still finished a full 11 points adrift heading into the final reckoning.

The round itself was proof of what he could do when it clicked. He played the 11th and 12th without dropping a shot, and there were signs of the same old trouble either side of that spell — quadruple bogeys at the 5th, the hardest hole on the course, and at the 17th cost him eight strokes to nothing in return. Strip those two blow-up holes out and the round looks like one of the best of his week. As it stood, it was simply the round that came too late.

## Gregg Williams's 41 points at Oitavos Dunes, the best round of the day, converting a five-point overnight lead into a twelve-point TEG 8 Trophy win
<!-- storyline: round -->

Gregg Williams closed out the TEG 8 Trophy in style, carding 41 points at Oitavos Dunes to post the best round of the day and turn a five-point overnight lead into a twelve-point winning margin. It was not a clean round. Early on, holes 5 to 8 cost him 11 gross shots, including a quadruple bogey on the 5th, the hardest hole on the course, followed by two double bogeys and a triple bogey that yielded just five stableford points across the stretch.

Williams steadied himself over the closing holes, taking 12 points from holes 12 to 15 with a run of pars either side of a single bogey at the par-5 13th, sealing the title from David Mullin, who had overtaken Jon Baker for the Green Jacket lead in the same round. The 41 was a marked turnaround from his 29 in Round 3, and came on his first visit to Oitavos Dunes, closing out the TEG with the round that mattered most.
