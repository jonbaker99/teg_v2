# Records at Both Ends

## Jon Baker's 84 gross at PGA Catalunya - Tour, a new course record that beats the standing mark of 89 by five and his own previous best there by six
<!-- storyline: round -->

Jon Baker set a new course record at PGA Catalunya - Tour with a gross 84 in round two, beating the previous mark of 89 by five shots and improving on his own previous best at the course, a 90 from TEG 6, by six.

The round was built on a stretch of pars from the 9th to the 11th, where three level pars — including one on the par-3 11th, one of the harder holes on the course — brought nine Stableford points without a dropped shot. He added another bogey-free two-hole stretch at the 6th and 7th earlier in the round.

The 84 kept Jon Baker's overall command intact. He remained the leader of both the 2018 TEG Trophy and the 2018 TEG Green Jacket, unchanged from before the round, with his Trophy lead down to two points and his Jacket lead out to three.

## John Patterson's 103, a new course worst at PGA Catalunya - Tour, including an eleven at the par-five 15th
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

John Patterson set a new course worst at PGA Catalunya - Tour, carding a 103 gross in round two of TEG that eclipsed the previous mark of 101, set across five prior visits to the course. The damage was done early and late. Holes 3 to 5 cost him eight shots without a single par - a double bogey, a triple bogey, and another triple bogey at the par-five 5th. He found a brief foothold with back-to-back pars at the 11th and 12th, but any recovery was undone at the par-five 15th, one of the tougher holes on the course, where he ran up an 11, a sextuple bogey and his worst hole against par of the round. The round left Patterson further adrift at the bottom of the Wooden Spoon standings, the gap to the next-worst player stretching from three shots to five, with Jon Baker still leading both the Trophy and Green Jacket races.

## Gregg Williams takes a ten at the 15th and still posts 92, equalling his best at the Tour course and his third-best gross round in 37 TEG rounds
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Round 2 at PGA Catalunya's Tour Course, on the club's back nine, brought the round's low point and its recovery in short order for Gregg Williams. At the par-5 15th, one of the course's harder holes, he ran up a quintuple bogey 10, following a bogey at the 14th. The damage continued with a double bogey at the 16th before he steadied himself with a birdie at the 17th, breaking a run of bogeys or worse, and closed with a par at the 18th to finish the back nine without dropping a shot over the last two holes. He had also managed a clean two-hole stretch through the 12th and 13th earlier in the round. Despite the disaster at the 15th, Williams still carded 92 for the round, matching the best gross score he has posted at the Tour Course, level with the mark he set on his only previous visit there.
