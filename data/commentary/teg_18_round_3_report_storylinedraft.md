# The Stadium Gives In

## David Mullin, bottom of the Trophy and holding the Wooden Spoon at breakfast, shoots 84 gross for a new PGA Catalunya Stadium course record and 44 Stableford points
<!-- storyline: round -->

**David Mullin**

David Mullin began Monday at the bottom of the 2025 TEG Trophy and holding the 2025 TEG Wooden Spoon, with only two points of daylight between him and the player above. By the end of the afternoon at PGA Catalunya's Stadium Course he had rewritten the course's record book.

His 84 gross is the lowest score any player has shot here. The previous mark was 89, set at some point across 27 prior visits. It is also comfortably Mullin's own best at the course — his previous best was 91 — and eight strokes better than he managed on his last visit.

The round was built on holes he simply refused to spoil. He opened with three straight pars, at the 1st, 2nd and 3rd, for nine points, including one of the hardest holes on the course at the opening tee. Pars at the 5th and 6th followed. The best sequence came around the turn: a par at the short 8th, a bogey at the 9th — the hardest hole on the course, and still worth three points off his handicap — and then a birdie at the 10th, ten points in three holes.

The 44 Stableford points were the best in a field of five, where the lowest return was 28. The Spoon changed hands: Jon Baker now holds it, 14 points adrift of the next man up.

## Alex Baker shoots 100 gross — seven worse than his last visit here — and still takes 44 points and extends his Trophy lead
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

**Alex Baker: a 100 that did the job**

Alex Baker went round PGA Catalunya's Stadium Course in 100 strokes on Monday, seven more than he took on his last visit here, and walked off with 44 Stableford points — the highest Trophy score returned by anyone in the five-man field in round three. The handicap strokes did a great deal of work.

The damage came in the middle of the round. Between the 6th and the 10th he dropped twelve shots in five holes: a triple bogey at the 6th, doubles at the 7th, 8th and 9th — the last of those the hardest hole on the course — and another triple at the 10th. Those five holes yielded eight points between them.

Then it turned. From the 11th to the 16th he collected 21 points in six holes, built on pars at the 11th, 13th and 14th and bogeys at the 12th, 15th and 16th. The 13th and 14th were the only back-to-back holes all day on which he failed to drop a gross shot.

The net effect was that the leader of the TEG 18 Trophy stayed exactly where he was, and then some: Alex Baker's advantage at the top grew from 11 points to 12.

## Europe's elite championship test hands out three personal bests and four scores of 43 points or more in a single afternoon
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

**Two course bests on the Stadium, built on the plainest golf imaginable**

The sixth visit to the Stadium Course at Camiral, on Monday 13 October, produced the lowest gross rounds either Gregg Williams or David Mullin has managed there. Williams went round in 85, seven strokes better than his previous visit and seven better than the 92 that had stood as his course best. Mullin's 84 was eight better than his last visit and seven inside his own previous mark of 91. In a field of five, the best return of the day in the 2025 TEG Trophy was 44 points, the worst 28.

Neither course best came from anything spectacular. Williams's afternoon was set up by four consecutive pars from the 2nd to the 5th, worth three points apiece and twelve in total, with not a gross shot dropped across the stretch. Mullin did the same thing over a shorter span, opening with pars at the 1st, 2nd and 3rd for nine points — the first of those on the 1st, one of the hardest holes on the course.

The day reshaped two of the three races. Williams took over the lead in the 2025 Green Jacket, now eight clear, while Jon Baker inherited the 2025 Wooden Spoon, 14 adrift of the next man.

## Jon Baker hands the TEG 18 Green Jacket lead to Gregg Williams at the 2nd and drops to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race on the same hole
<!-- storyline: race -->

**Two holes, two titles turned over**

Jon Baker arrived at PGA Catalunya's Stadium Course on Monday holding the TEG 18 Green Jacket lead by two, and lost it at the 2nd. A triple bogey 7 there returned no points. Gregg Williams made a routine par at the same hole, and that was enough: the gross lead changed hands outright, and by the end of the round Gregg Williams was eight clear.

The same hole cost Jon Baker at the other end of the table. He passed David Mullin to take up last place in the TEG 18 Wooden Spoon race — a position he had been two points clear of before the round, and which he ended the day holding by 14.

It got worse before it got better. At the 4th he ran up a 9, a quintuple bogey. The finish offered no relief: four double bogeys from the 15th to the 18th, eight shots dropped across the stretch and not a single net par, the last of them at the 18th, one of the hardest holes on the course.

The round totalled 28 points, down 11 from his 39 in round two, and the lowest score returned by any of the five players. His gross 100 was 11 strokes worse than his last visit here. Gregg Williams, meanwhile, went the other way: his 85 is a course best, beating his previous mark of 92.
