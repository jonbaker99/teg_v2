# History and Havoc at Quinta da Marinha

## Jon Baker's course-record 89 gross headlines the best round of the day at Quinta da Marinha
<!-- storyline: round -->

Jon Baker produced the best round of the day at Quinta da Marinha, carding a gross 89 in round 3 to set a new course record, beating the previous mark of 91 set across six prior visits to the venue. It was also his own personal best there, three shots inside his previous visit and comfortably his best gross score of the trip.

The round had a rocky patch in the middle: three holes without a net par from the 7th to the 9th, a bogey followed by doubles on the par-4 8th — the hardest hole on the course — and the par-3 9th. Baker steadied himself with back-to-back pars on the 12th and 13th, and the par at the 12th, against one of the toughest indexes on the course, drew him level for the lead in the 2015 Green Jacket (Gross). By the close of play he had gone further still, moving two clear of David Mullin, whose six-shot lead in that race had vanished entirely.

## Stuart Neumann's round disintegrates into the day's worst score, stretching his Wooden Spoon gap to 22 points
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Stuart Neumann's Round 3 at Quinta da Marinha unravelled early and never recovered. He opened with a 10 on the par-5 1st, a quintuple bogey, and followed it with a double bogey at the 2nd, a double at the 3rd, a quintuple bogey 9 on the par-4 4th and a triple bogey at the 5th — 17 shots dropped across the first five holes without a single net par among them.

The back nine offered no respite. A quintuple bogey 8 on the par-3 9th was followed by an 11 at the par-5 10th, a sextuple bogey on one of the course's harder holes, and a double bogey at the 11th, another stretch of three holes without a net par.

The round produced a Stableford score of 20, the worst of the field on the day, a sharp reversal from the 31 he had posted in Round 2. It leaves Neumann anchored to the bottom of the 2015 TEG Wooden Spoon standings, with his gap to the next-worst player stretching from 8 points to 22.

## Alex Baker survives a sextuple-bogey ten at the 8th to set a new personal best at Quinta da Marinha
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Alex Baker's third round at Quinta da Marinha turned into a survival exercise. Trouble began at the par-5 3rd, where triple bogeys at the 3rd, 4th and 5th bled nine shots in three holes, and the damage did not stop there. A double bogey at the 7th ran straight into a sextuple-bogey ten at the par-4 8th, the hardest hole on the course, before further double and triple bogeys at the 9th and 10th completed a 13-shot stretch across just four holes. A quadruple bogey at the 12th, the second-hardest hole, kept the bleeding going. Baker finally arrested it with a birdie at the 17th.

Remarkably, none of it derailed the bigger picture. Baker's gross score for the round beat his previous best at Quinta da Marinha, set on his only prior visit, by a single stroke — a new personal best at the course despite the carnage in the middle of the round.

## David Mullin's 36-hole Green Jacket lead ends at the very hole it began, while Gregg Williams' Trophy cushion is nearly halved
<!-- storyline: race -->

David Mullin's 36-hole lead in the 2015 Green Jacket (Gross), held since the 13th hole of the first round, ended at Quinta da Marinha — fittingly at the very same hole, the 13th, where Jon Baker moved ahead in the third round. Mullin had briefly drawn level again at the 15th, but the round fell apart from there: he closed with 26 points, his worst round of the tournament, a 10-shot drop from Round 2's 36. Baker's own 89 gross was a course best for him this TEG.

Gregg Williams held on to his 2015 Trophy lead but saw his cushion nearly halved, from 11 points to 5, after a torrid finish to his round. Williams shed 14 shots across holes 8 to 11, capped by a quintuple bogey 10 at the par-5 11th. His round of 29 points was a 12-shot fall from a strong Round 2, and left Baker out in front of the Green Jacket race heading into the weekend.
