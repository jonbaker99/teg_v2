# Twelve Shots in Four Holes {.round-report-title}

<p class="dateline">TEG 14 | Round 2 | 05/11/2021 | Littlestone</p>

<p class="round-scores"><span class="round-scores-header">Round Stableford:</span> DM 41 | AB 35 | JB 29 | GW 27</p>
<p class="round-scores"><span class="round-scores-header">Round Gross:</span> DM +14 | JB +21 | GW +26 | AB +29</p>

Littlestone Golf Club, Littlestone-on-Sea, Kent. Friday. The 2nd TEG round at this venue.

Twenty-four hours earlier, at Royal Cinque Ports, Alex Baker had posted a quadruple and a quintuple on the back nine. Those had felt, at the time, like a pair of isolated incidents — regrettable, certainly, but the kind of thing that happens when the concentration wanders on an exposed links.

At Littlestone, the front nine provided clarification.

---

**Alex Baker — 35 Stableford points**

Romney Marshes sits on the flat, remote fringes of Kent, and Littlestone Golf Club occupies it with the quiet confidence of a course that does not need to impress anyone. Its opening holes are not complicated. This made what happened at holes 2, 3, 4, and 5 harder to explain, and easier to observe.

Triple at the par-4 2nd. Double at the par-4 3rd. Triple at the par-4 4th — one of the hardest on the course, stroke index 2. Then, at the par-5 5th — one of the easiest, stroke index 16 — a 9: quadruple bogey, zero Stableford points. Twelve shots over par in four consecutive holes. Three Stableford points to show for them.

He continued to the par-4 8th and scored 8: another quadruple bogey, nothing.

At some stage in the back nine, Baker located a more cooperative version of the game. From the 13th to the 18th, he went six holes without dropping a net shot: par at H13, bogeys at H14 and H15, double at the par-4 16th — stroke index 3 — bogey at H17, par at H18. Fifteen Stableford points in six holes. A closing flourish that changed the look of the scorecard without materially changing the situation.

He finished on 35.

**David Mullin — 41 Stableford points**

Mullin arrived at Littlestone leading the Trophy by 3, and appeared to be in the process of surrendering it. Holes 4, 5, and 6: triple bogey at the par-4 4th (one of the hardest, stroke index 2), double bogey at the par-5 5th, bogey at the par-3 6th. Six shots over par in three holes, a mid-round patch that invited the realistic possibility of a tighter competition by the end of the day.

He answered at the par-5 7th with a birdie. Four Stableford points. The matter was, briefly, resolved.

His final stretch featured further turbulence — bogeys at H15 and H16, a double at the par-3 17th — before a birdie at the par-5 18th provided the day's last entry. Forty-one Stableford points in total: best in the field for the second consecutive round, on the second consecutive links course.

The overnight lead of 3 became a 9-point advantage. Mullin also extended his Green Jacket lead, posting the day's best gross performance.

**Jon Baker — 29 Stableford points**

Jon Baker scored 29 Stableford points — the same as his Round 1 total — and remained last in the Trophy standings. The Wooden Spoon gap to Williams narrowed from 4 to 2 as Williams had a worse round. This represented, for Jon Baker, the day's most positive development.

**Gregg Williams — 27 Stableford points**

Williams posted 27 Stableford points — the worst round of the day. He is third in the Trophy on 60 cumulative points, and his gap over Jon Baker in the Wooden Spoon standings is now 2. It is perhaps worth noting that 27 points on a day when Baker scored 35 and went through what he went through represents a certain achievement, in the wrong direction.

---

The Trophy gap has moved from 3 to 9. The Jacket gap has widened from 5 to 12. Baker's front-nine implosion at Littlestone — 12 shots over par in four holes, plus a further quad at the 8th — accounts for the difference between a race and a procession. Mullin did not need to be his best today. He needed to be better than the arithmetic, and the arithmetic cooperated.
