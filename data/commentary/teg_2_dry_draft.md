# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 2, Boavista, Algarve, May 2009

## OVERVIEW

David Mullin won the Trophy on net-vs-par at +6, seven clear of Jon Baker (b01). Mullin also won the Green Jacket on gross at +66, eleven clear of Stuart Neumann (b06). Henry Meller took the Wooden Spoon at +39 net VP (b19). Three rounds, all played at Boavista Golf & Spa Resort in Lagos — a new course for TEG, and TEG's first visit to the Algarve.

## ROUND 1 — Mullin's +0: The Best Round TEG Had Ever Seen (So Far)

Saturday 16 May. Five players, none of whom had seen the course before.

The early exchanges were routine. Gregg Williams drew level at the top on hole 2; Baker went outright ahead at the 4th; at the 5th both Mullin and Neumann drew level; Baker drew level again at the 8th. The same pattern applied to the gross competition — Williams level at the 2nd, Mullin and Baker level at the 4th, Neumann level at the 5th — and after hole 5 of round 1 the Jacket lead never changed hands again.

Mullin signed for a net score of +0, then the best round in TEG history (b02). The gross figure behind it was +20, also then the best gross round in TEG history (b03). It gave him a nine-shot lead in the Jacket and a four-shot lead in the Trophy after 18 holes.

Baker posted +4 net, then the second-best round in TEG history (b08), off a gross +32, then the third-best gross round (b14). Neumann posted +11 net, then the third-best round in TEG history (b15), off a gross +29, then the second-best gross round (b09).

The back nine did the damage elsewhere. Meller played holes 11 to 15 in 20 over: a triple bogey 6 at the par-3 11th, a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 12th, a quadruple bogey 9 at the par-5 13th, a quintuple bogey 8 at the par-3 14th, and a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 15th, the hardest hole on the course (b16). He finished the round at +24 net, +42 gross. He was outright bottom of the Trophy from the 3rd hole of the tournament.

Williams played holes 12 to 18 in 22 over: double bogey at the par-4 12th, double bogey at the par-5 13th, a sextuple bogey 9 at the par-3 14th, double bogey at the par-4 15th, triple bogey at the par-5 16th, a quintuple bogey 9 at the par-4 17th — one of the easiest holes on the course — and a double bogey at the par-4 18th (b20). He finished at +12 net, +40 gross.

Neumann's own worst hole of the tournament came at the same par-3 14th: an 8, a quintuple bogey (b17). Three 8s and a 9 were taken from that hole on day one.

## ROUND 2 — Ten Shots in Three Holes: Mullin Gift-Wraps the Lead

Sunday 17 May, same course.

The lead moved three times in the first six holes. Baker took the Trophy lead outright at the par-3 4th with a par for a net 2 under (b39). Mullin took it back outright at the par-4 5th with a bogey for a net par (b38). Baker drew level again at the 6th.

Baker then played holes 6, 7 and 8 in four net shots to par: a bogey at the par-3 6th (net −1), a par at the par-4 7th (net −2), and a bogey at the par-5 8th, the second-hardest hole on the course (net −1) (b41).

Mullin's gross round was +28, then the third-best gross round in TEG history (b12), and it kept him eight clear in the Jacket. His net round was +8, and the reason was the finish: holes 16, 17 and 18 in ten over — a triple bogey 8 at the par-5 16th, a triple bogey 7 at the par-4 17th, and a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 18th (b43).

Baker signed for +1 net, then the second-best round in TEG history (b10), off a gross +29 — his best gross score at Boavista. He went three shots clear in the Trophy. His own finish was 13 over across holes 15 to 18 (b37): triple bogey at the par-4 15th, double bogey at the par-5 16th, quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 17th and quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 18th.

Neumann posted a gross +27, then the second-best gross round in TEG history (b11). He also ran up a second quintuple bogey 8 at the par-3 14th (b18).

Meller played holes 13 to 16 in 11 over — double bogey at the par-5 13th, triple bogey 6 at the par-3 14th, triple bogey at the par-4 15th (the hardest hole), triple bogey at the par-5 16th (b48) — and improved his net round to +13.

## ROUND 3 — Baker Leads by Three, Trails by the Third

Monday 18 May, same course again.

Baker started the day three clear and played the first five holes in 12 over: a double bogey 6 at the par-4 1st, a double bogey 5 at the par-3 2nd, a triple bogey 8 at the par-5 3rd, a triple bogey 6 at the par-3 4th, and a double bogey 6 at the par-4 5th, the second-easiest hole on the course (b24).

At the 3rd, Mullin made a bogey 6 at the par-5 for a net par and moved from second to first outright in the Trophy (b07). He did not lose the lead again.

Mullin's round was −2 net, then the best round in TEG history (b04), off a gross +18, then the best gross round in TEG history (b05) and his best score at Boavista (89 gross). The three-shot overnight deficit became a seven-shot win (b01), and the Jacket margin went out to eleven (b06). After round 3 he led both competitions (b44).

Neumann's round included a run broken at the 11th: bogey at the par-5 8th, double bogey at the par-4 9th (the hardest hole), bogey at the par-4 10th, then a birdie 2 at the par-3 11th (b40). He finished on 92 gross, his best at the course.

Meller posted a gross +20 — 91 shots, then the second-best gross round in TEG history (b13) — and a net +2, his third straight improvement (+24, +13, +2). It did not affect the Wooden Spoon, which he had held from the 3rd hole of round 1 (b19). Williams shot 105, his best of the three rounds.

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Net VP).** Mullin led after round 1 by four (net +0, b02). Baker led after round 2 by three (net +1, b10), Mullin having dropped ten shots across holes 16-18 (b43). Mullin led after round 3 by seven. The decisive moment was round 3, hole 3: Mullin's bogey at the par-5 for a net par took the lead outright (b07) while Baker was in the middle of a 12-over opening five (b24). Nine lead changes in total, five of them in the first eight holes of round 1; four were outright. Final: Mullin +6, Baker +13 — margin 7 (b01).

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Mullin led after every round: by nine, then eight, then eleven. Four lead changes, all in the first five holes of round 1, none outright. The decisive round was the first: +20 gross (b03). His +28 in round 2 (b12) held the lead despite the closing three holes, and his +18 in round 3 (b05) stretched it. Neumann's +29 and +27 (b09, b11) secured second. Final: Mullin +66, Neumann +77 — margin 11 (b06).

**Wooden Spoon.** Meller went outright bottom at hole 3 of round 1 and was bottom after all three rounds. The single bottom change came on that hole; there were no others. The round-1 stretch from 11 to 15 cost 20 shots (b16) and produced a +24 round. He improved to +13 and then +2, and closed with a +20 gross round (b13), but finished on +39 net VP (b19).

## PLAYERS

- **David Mullin** — Trophy winner at +6 by 7 and Green Jacket winner at +66 by 11. Round scores: +0, +8, −2 net; +20, +28, +18 gross. Set the then-best TEG net round twice and the then-best TEG gross round twice, on a course he had not previously played. Lost the Trophy lead in round 2 after dropping ten shots over the last three holes; regained it outright at hole 3 of round 3.
- **Jon Baker** — Runner-up in the Trophy by seven. Net rounds of +4 and +1, then TEG's second-best twice; gross rounds of +32 and +29. Led by three after round 2, then played the first five holes of round 3 in 12 over.
- **Stuart Neumann** — Green Jacket runner-up, 11 behind. Gross rounds of +29 and +27, each then the second-best in TEG history; net +11 in round 1. Two quintuple bogey 8s at the par-3 14th, in rounds 1 and 2. Best Boavista score 92 (R3).
- **Henry Meller** — Wooden Spoon at +39 net VP. Bottom of the Trophy from hole 3 of round 1 and after every round. Improved each round: +24, +13, +2. Closed with 91 gross (+20), then the second-best gross round in TEG history.
- **Gregg Williams** — Drew level at the top on hole 2 of round 1. Lost 22 shots over holes 12-18 that round, including a 9 at the par-3 14th and a 9 at the par-4 17th. Best round was 105 in R3.
