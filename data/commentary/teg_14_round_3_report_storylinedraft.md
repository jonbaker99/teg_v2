# Stalemate at the Top, Drift at the Bottom

## Gregg Williams's 39 points and 86 gross at Prince's, a personal best on the course and a twelve-point answer to his Littlestone round
<!-- storyline: round -->

Gregg Williams's third round at Prince's Golf Club produced 39 points and a gross 86, the best Trophy score recorded that day from the four-man field in the 2021 TEG Trophy. It was also a personal best on the course, improving his previous visit there by three strokes.

The round was a sharp reversal from his second-round 27 at Littlestone, a twelve-point swing that ranks among the largest turnarounds of his week. The middle of the round carried it: three straight pars from the 9th to the 11th, including SI 3 at the 10th, one of the hardest holes on the course, brought nine points and three holes without dropping a shot. That run ended abruptly at the par-5 12th, where a triple bogey broke the streak. Williams recovered to add two more bogey-free stretches, at the 2nd and 3rd and again at the 15th and 16th, to close out the best round of his week at Prince's.

## David Mullin's lopsided 36 points at Prince's, a quadruple-bogey 9 at the 8th redeemed by thirteen points over the closing four holes
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

David Mullin's third round at Prince's summed to 36 points, but the shape of it was uneven. At the par-5 8th he ran up a quadruple-bogey 9 for no return, the kind of hole that could have derailed a card. Instead he finished the round strongly, closing with 13 points over the last four holes — pars at the 15th, 16th and 17th followed by a birdie on the 18th, four holes played without dropping a single gross shot.

The round left him leading both the Trophy and the Green Jacket races after the day's play, his Trophy lead trimmed slightly to eight points but his Jacket advantage holding at 12. It was also his second visit to Prince's, matching his prior best gross score of 92 from his first trip round. The blow-up at the 8th aside, the closing stretch did the damage-limitation job needed to keep both leads intact.

## Alex Baker's 37 points, assembled out of three opening double bogeys and a quadruple-bogey 8, worth one point off the lead
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Alex Baker's third round at Prince's Shore/Dunes started disastrously, with double bogeys at each of the first three holes bleeding six shots in as many holes for a mere four points. A quadruple-bogey 8 at the 10th, one of the harder holes on the course, threatened to derail things further. But Baker steadied himself with back-to-back pars at the 11th and 12th, and pieced together enough elsewhere to card 37 points for the round, only one point off the day's best score of 39 in a four-man field.

The recovery was all the more notable given the finish was eight shots worse than his prior visit to the course. Neither Trophy nor Green Jacket races moved at the top, with David Mullin holding both leads, though the gap in the Trophy narrowed slightly to eight. Baker's round did nothing to disturb any of that, but it showed real resilience after a horror start.

## Jon Baker's 28 points turn a two-point Wooden Spoon cushion into a thirteen-point gap while David Mullin's Trophy and Jacket leads survive intact
<!-- storyline: race -->

Jon Baker's Prince's - Shore / Dunes round produced the worst Trophy score of the day among the field, 28 points, and it turned a tight fight at the bottom of the 2021 Wooden Spoon table into a rout. Baker had started solidly, parring the 8th and 9th, and looked to be building on it with three straight pars from the 14th to the 16th. But sandwiched between those stretches came a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 13th, the hardest hole on the course, and his run of pars was then snapped by a triple bogey at the 17th. The damage pushed his Wooden Spoon gap to the next-worst player out from two points to thirteen. At the top, David Mullin's leads held: he remains clear in both the Trophy, where his advantage narrowed slightly to eight points, and the Green Jacket, where his twelve-point cushion was untouched.
