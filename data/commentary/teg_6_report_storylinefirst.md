**Meller's high and low arrive on consecutive days**

Henry Meller's trip to Catalonia contained both the best golf of his career and one of its more testing afternoons, 24 hours apart.

The high point came at Costa Brava on the Sunday, where Meller signed for a round of 90. On net terms it was a personal best at -5, and the gross figure of +17 was a personal best too, the first time he had assembled a full round of that quality in TEG competition. He had, briefly, the look of a man who had worked something out.

Round 3 at El Prat's Rosa course took it back with interest. Meller opened with a 10 at the 1st, a par 5 of stroke index 4, a quintuple bogey and the worst hole relative to par he had recorded. There is a certain economy in getting your worst hole out of the way before anyone has settled. Seven holes later he ran up a second 10, this time a sextuple bogey at the par-4 8th, the hardest hole on the card. His round came in at 109.

Meller finished the tournament on +30 net against par and collected the Wooden Spoon. The two 10s in Round 3 accounted for seven net shots between them, which was most of the gap he needed to close.
