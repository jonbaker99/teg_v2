# Record Falls, Mullin Doubles Up

## David Mullin completes the TEG 14 Trophy and Green Jacket double while posting the worst round in the field, as Alex Baker's charge dies with a 10 at the 16th
<!-- storyline: race -->

David Mullin completed the TEG 14 double, winning both the Trophy and the Green Jacket — his fifth double in TEG history, following previous ones in TEGs 2, 4, 11 and 13. He did it despite carding the worst round in the four-man field at Prince's - Shore/Dunes, just 37 stableford points, including a quadruple bogey 8 at the 10th, one of the hardest holes on the course. His Trophy lead was cut to two shots by the finish, but it held.

The closest challenge came from Alex Baker, who clawed nine shots back off the Trophy lead to trail by just two, riding two hot stretches — ten points over the first three holes and eleven more from the 13th to the 15th. Three consecutive pars set up what looked like a run at the top, but it ended abruptly at the 16th, one of the easiest holes on the course, where Baker carded a sextuple bogey 10. In the Green Jacket race, Gregg Williams and Jon Baker both closed sharply, from deficits of 18 and 15 down to eight and seven, but neither caught Mullin, who sealed the double regardless.

## Jon Baker takes the TEG 14 Wooden Spoon by twelve points on the same afternoon he sets the course record
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Jon Baker finished the 2021 TEG 14 Wooden Spoon in last place by twelve points, but the final afternoon at Prince's - Shore/Dunes produced the best golf of his week. Having carded just 28 stableford points in round three, he turned in 39 in round four, an eleven-point swing, to record his best round of the tournament. The round included an 8 (quadruple bogey) at the 18th, but it was still enough to set a personal best on the course this TEG, beating his previous best gross there by two shots and improving twelve strokes on his last visit to Prince's. The form also mattered further up the leaderboard: Baker's charge helped cut his deficit in the TEG 14 Green Jacket race from a high of fifteen points down to seven behind David Mullin, who remained in front throughout. None of it was enough to lift Baker off the bottom of the Wooden Spoon table, where he closed the tournament twelve points adrift of the next player.

## Jon Baker's 83 gross at Prince's - Shore / Dunes, a new course record, built on a five-hole run without a dropped shot from the 11th
<!-- storyline: round -->

Jon Baker set a new course record at Prince's – Shore / Dunes, going round in 83 gross to beat the previous mark of 85, a figure that had stood across nine prior visits to the layout. It was also his personal best there by two shots, improving on the 85 he had carded in TEG 13 and arriving twelve strokes better than his last visit to the course.

The record was built on a five-hole stretch from the 11th where he did not drop a gross shot, taking in birdies at the 12th and, notably, at the 13th — the hardest hole on the course. Across holes 12 to 15 he piled up 14 stableford points. The run ended at the 16th with a double bogey, and later, at the last, a quadruple-bogey 8 crept in. Neither dented the outcome: the 83 stood as the new benchmark for the course.
