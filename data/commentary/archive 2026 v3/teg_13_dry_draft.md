# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 13, Kent, England (2020)

## OVERVIEW

Jon Baker won the Trophy on 158 Stableford points (39-36-44-39), six clear of Gregg Williams. He also won the Green Jacket at +50 gross, eighteen shots clear of Williams — the best gross aggregate in TEG history, and his third Jacket in succession. David Mullin finished last on the Trophy metric with 135 points and took the Wooden Spoon.

Four rounds, four Kent links, none previously played by anyone in the field: Royal Cinque Ports at Deal, Prince's (Dunes/Himalayas), Littlestone, and Prince's again (Shore/Dunes).

---

## Round 1 — Deal or No Deal: Baker Nine Clear, Patterson Signs for 108

Thursday, Royal Cinque Ports, Deal — a championship links that has hosted two Open Championships, and a first visit for every player.

Jon Baker opened par-par at the 1st and 2nd and birdied the par-5 3rd: 10 points from three holes (b19). He signed for 85 gross (+13) and 39 points, and led the Green Jacket by nine and the Trophy by six at the close. He had held the Trophy lead since the 2nd hole.

John Patterson took 8 at the par-4 1st (b22), 9 at the par-4 7th (b13) and 9 at the par-5 16th (b24). His 108 was the highest gross round of the tournament. He was bottom of the Spoon race after the 2nd hole and bottom again at the end of the round.

Alex Baker ran up 8 at the par-4 11th, one of the hardest holes on the course at SI 3 (b21).

David Mullin started 7-5-6-5 — triple bogey, bogey, bogey, double bogey, seven over through four — then birdied the par-5 5th, SI 2 (b37).

---

## Round 2 — Baker Blinks at 16, Birdies the 18th

Friday, Prince's, Sandwich, in the Dunes/Himalayas configuration.

Jon Baker bogeyed the 4th and double-bogeyed the 5th before birdieing the par-5 6th (b31). Gregg Williams drew level with him on the 15th, and at the par-3 16th, where Baker made par for 3 points, Williams moved in front (b01). That ended a run of 32 consecutive holes in the lead for Baker, dating back to the 2nd hole of Round 1. Baker birdied the par-4 18th to retake the outright lead (b33). He posted 36 points and 88 gross (+16), leading the Trophy by one and the Jacket by seven.

John Patterson dropped eleven shots over the first four holes: 8 at the par-4 1st, 5 at the par-3 2nd, 8 at the par-5 3rd, 6 at the par-4 4th (b40). He added an 8 at the par-4 17th (b27). He was bottom of the Spoon race at the end of the round for the second day running.

David Mullin took 8 at the par-4 9th (b25).

---

## Round 3 — Littlestone, Little Resistance: 44 and 49 on the Marsh

Saturday, Littlestone on the Romney Marshes — laid out by William Laidlaw Purves, the architect of Royal St George's, and refined by Braid and MacKenzie.

Jon Baker shot 79, +8 gross, the second-best gross round in TEG history to date (b05) and the lowest gross round of the tournament. Within it, holes 8 to 11 produced 13 points: par at the 8th, birdie at the par-3 9th, par at the par-4 10th (SI 1, the hardest hole on the course) and par at the 11th (b12). He took 44 points and finished the day leading the Trophy by five and the Jacket by fourteen.

John Patterson scored 49 points, the third-best round in TEG history to date (b06), off a gross of 83 — +12, a personal best (b07). He also made 7 at the par-3 17th (b29).

Gregg Williams shot 86.

Alex Baker took 8 at the par-4 2nd (b28) and, after a double bogey at the par-3 9th, dropped to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race (b18), where he finished the round.

---

## Round 4 — Shore Thing: Baker Doubles Up, Mullin Spoons at the 10th

Sunday, Prince's again, this time the Shore/Dunes routing.

Jon Baker closed with 85 gross (+13) and 39 points. That completed the Trophy on 158 points by six (b03) and the Green Jacket at +50 by eighteen (b02). Late in the round he bogeyed the par-5 15th and double-bogeyed the 16th (SI 17, one of the easiest holes on the course) before birdieing the par-3 17th (b32). No lead change was recorded in the final round.

John Patterson made 3 at the par-5 2nd for an eagle (b04) — the only eagle recorded by anyone across the tournament (b15). He later took 9 at the par-5 15th (b30).

David Mullin took 9 at the par-4 10th, SI 3, one of the hardest holes on the course (b14). It dropped him to last in the Wooden Spoon race for the first time in the tournament, with eight holes to play (b10), and he stayed there. Final total 135 points (b11).

Alex Baker posted 89 gross, +17, a personal best (b08) and his best round of the tournament.

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Jon Baker led at the end of every round: by six after Round 1, one after Round 2, five after Round 3, six at the finish. He held the lead from the 2nd hole of Round 1. Three lead changes were recorded, all in the middle two rounds: Williams drew level at the 15th in Round 2 and went in front at the 16th (b01); Baker took the outright lead back with a birdie at the 18th two holes later (b33) — the decisive takeover; Williams drew level again at the 1st at Littlestone in Round 3 (b43), the easiest hole on that course at SI 18. Baker's 44 points at Littlestone then opened the gap to five, and 39 in Round 4 closed it out. Final margin: six points over Williams.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Baker led wire to wire with no lead changes at all: +13 at Royal Cinque Ports for a nine-shot lead, +16 at Prince's for seven, +8 at Littlestone for fourteen, +13 in Round 4 for eighteen. The Round 3 79 (b05) was the decisive round. Aggregate +50 (b02), the best gross total in TEG history, and Williams second.

**Wooden Spoon.** Nine bottom-place changes in all, six of them in the opening holes of Round 1 while the field was bunched. Patterson was bottom after Rounds 1 and 2; Alex Baker was bottom after Round 3 (b18). Mullin's own position went 3rd, 3rd, 4th, 5th. The decisive moment was the 9 at the par-4 10th at Prince's Shore in Round 4 (b14), which put him last for the first time (b10) with eight holes remaining. He finished on 135 points (b11) — a third consecutive Wooden Spoon and a fourth in total.

## PLAYERS

**Jon Baker** — Won the Trophy (158 points, by six) and the Green Jacket (+50, by eighteen, a third in succession and the best gross aggregate in TEG history). Led the Trophy from Round 1 hole 2 and the Jacket throughout; the only time he was headed was between the 15th/16th and the 18th of Round 2.

**Gregg Williams** — Runner-up in both the Trophy (by six) and the Green Jacket (by eighteen). The only player to hold or share the lead at any point: in front at Round 2 hole 16, level again at Round 3 hole 1. Shot 86 at Littlestone.

**John Patterson** — Posted the tournament's highest round (108 at Royal Cinque Ports) and its lowest points-scoring day (49 at Littlestone, third-best round in TEG history), plus a personal-best +12 gross and the only eagle of the tournament, at the par-5 2nd in Round 4. Bottom of the Spoon race after Rounds 1 and 2; did not finish last.

**David Mullin** — Finished last on 135 points for the Wooden Spoon: positions 3rd, 3rd, 4th, 5th across the four rounds. Round scores 31-37-32-35. A third consecutive Spoon and a fourth in total.

**Alex Baker** — Bottom of the Spoon race after Round 3 following an 8 at Littlestone's 2nd and a double bogey at the 9th; closed with 89 gross at Prince's Shore/Dunes, +17, a personal best.
