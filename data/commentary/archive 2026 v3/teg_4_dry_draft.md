# TEG 4 — East Sussex and Surrey, 29 August – 1 September 2011

## OVERVIEW

David Mullin won the Trophy on net versus par at +9, eight clear of Jon Baker (+17); +9 is the lowest winning Trophy total in TEG history to date. Mullin also won the Green Jacket on gross at +81, nineteen shots clear of Stuart Neumann (+100). Gregg Williams took the Wooden Spoon at +40 net versus par, five behind Henry Meller. All four rounds were played on courses new to TEG, so no player held prior course history anywhere.

---

## Round 1 — No Bunkers at Ashdown, and Meller Still Finds a 12

Monday at Royal Ashdown Forest, a heathland course with no bunkers. Baker led the Trophy after the round at +2, three clear of Mullin (+5). The opening exchanges were bunched: the Trophy lead changed hands ten times inside the round, with Neumann, Baker, Mullin and Williams all involved, most of them shared rather than outright.

Meller ran up a 12 at the par-3 11th (SI 3), nine over par on the hole and net eight — the worst single hole in TEG history to date. Baker took a 9 at the par-4 14th (SI 1), five over.

Williams dropped 22 shots to par across the closing nine, holes 10 to 18: double bogey at the par-4 10th, triple at the par-3 11th, doubles at the par-4 12th and par-4 13th, triple at the par-4 14th (SI 1), doubles at the par-4 15th and the par-3 16th (SI 17), and triples at the par-4 17th and par-4 18th.

Mullin posted 23 over gross, best in the field, and moved to the front of the Green Jacket at the 11th.

## Round 2 — Baker's Worst Round Yet Hands Mullin the Lead at Lingfield

Tuesday at Lingfield Park, parkland with mature trees and water. Baker posted +9 net (33 gross), his worst round to date at that point, including a 9 at the par-4 17th (SI 5), five over. Mullin returned +4 net (22 gross).

Mullin drew level on the Trophy at the 10th, Baker went back in front outright at the 12th, and Mullin drew level again at the 13th. Mullin led the Trophy at the end of the round at +9, two clear of Baker (+11).

Williams took a 9 at the par-4 3rd, five over. Meller took a 9 at the par-5 15th (SI 1), four over. Neumann was bottom of the Wooden Spoon standings at the end of the round, having gone to the bottom outright at the 2nd, the 5th and again at the 9th.

## Round 3 — Mullin's 88 on the Beacon — and Baker Draws Level Anyway

Wednesday at Crowborough Beacon, heathland with eight MacKenzie-redesigned holes. Mullin shot 88, 17 over gross — the second-best gross round in TEG history to date — for -1 net. Within it he went nine holes from the 9th to the 17th without dropping a net shot: pars at the par-4 9th, par-5 10th, par-3 13th (SI 18) and par-5 14th, bogeys at the 11th, 12th, 15th, 16th and the par-3 17th.

Baker returned -3 net, a twelve-shot improvement on his Round 2 score. He opened with nine holes without dropping a net shot, holes 1 to 9: bogeys at the 1st, 2nd (SI 1), 4th, 5th (SI 3), 6th and 8th, pars at the par-3 3rd and par-5 7th, and a double bogey at the par-4 9th. He then played the 11th to the 14th in four pars, gaining five net shots across the stretch. He took quadruple bogeys at the par-4 15th and the par-4 18th.

The Trophy was level after 54 holes, Mullin and Baker on +8 apiece, sixteen clear of the rest. The lead passed back and forth within the round: Baker drew level at the 2nd, Mullin went ahead outright at the 10th, Baker drew level at the 11th, Mullin drew level at the 15th.

Williams dropped 18 shots in the first six holes: double at the par-4 1st, quadruple at the par-4 2nd (SI 1), double at the par-3 3rd, quadruples at the par-4 4th and par-4 5th (SI 3), and a double at the par-3 6th. He fell to the bottom of the Spoon race outright at the 4th and finished the round fourth of five, with Meller bottom.

## Round 4 — Baker Eagles the 8th, Then Loses 27 Shots in Eight Holes

Thursday at Bletchingley, a modern parkland course advertised for its drainage and fast playing conditions.

Baker birdied the par-3 2nd (SI 17) for the outright Trophy lead; Mullin drew level at the 3rd. Baker then holed out for an eagle 2 at the par-4 8th — the only eagle recorded across the tournament — and stood seven clear.

From the 9th to the 16th Baker dropped 27 shots to par: double bogey (7) at the par-5 9th, triple (7) at the par-4 10th, triple (8) at the par-5 11th, sextuple (9) at the par-3 12th (SI 18, the easiest hole on the course), double (6) at the par-4 13th, triple (6) at the par-3 14th, sextuple (11) at the par-5 15th, and double (6) at the par-4 16th (SI 2). He finished the round at +9 net, 33 gross, twelve worse than his Round 3 score.

Mullin opened with six holes without dropping a net shot: bogeys at the par-4 1st, par-3 2nd and par-5 3rd, pars at the par-4 4th (SI 3), par-4 5th (SI 1) and par-5 6th. He took a 7 at the par-3 7th, four over. He closed at +1 net, 19 gross, the best round in the field, and took the Trophy lead outright at the 12th.

Behind them, the Spoon was settled. Williams dropped 13 shots from the 4th to the 7th — triple at the par-4 4th, quadruple at the par-4 5th (SI 1), quadruple (9) at the par-5 6th, double at the par-3 7th — and went to the bottom outright at the 6th. Meller's quintuple 10 at the par-5 11th, the last of four holes (8th to 11th) in which he dropped 11 shots, put him bottom outright instead. Williams returned to the bottom at the 13th and stayed there, dropping 16 shots over the closing six: triple at the par-4 13th, quadruple (7) at the par-3 14th, triple (8) at the par-5 15th, doubles at the par-4 16th (SI 2), par-3 17th and par-4 18th. Meller also took a 10 at the par-5 15th.

Williams finished the round at +15 net and 43 gross, both personal worsts to date. Four players posted personal-best gross totals across the four rounds: Neumann +100, Meller +107, Baker +113, Williams +152.

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (net vs par).** Baker led after Round 1 at +2, three clear. Mullin took the lead in Round 2 when Baker posted +9 to Mullin's +4, and led by two. Baker's -3 at Crowborough squared it: both men on +8 with 18 to play, and Mullin held the round-by-round lead only jointly. In Round 4 Baker led outright from the 2nd and by seven after his eagle at the 8th; Mullin took the lead outright at the 12th, as Baker made 9 on that par 3, and was not headed again. There were 20 lead changes in all, ten of them in Round 1. Final margin: Mullin +9, Baker +17.

**Green Jacket (gross).** Mullin led from the 11th hole of Round 1 and led at the end of every round — by 3, 7, 16 and finally 19. There were four lead changes, all in Round 1. He beat Neumann head-to-head in all four rounds and was best in the field in three, the best card being 17 over at Crowborough. Final: Mullin +81, Neumann +100.

**Wooden Spoon.** Williams was third of five after Rounds 1 and 2, fourth after Round 3 (having gone to the bottom outright at Crowborough's 4th before Meller ended the round last), and bottom after Round 4. Meller was bottom after Rounds 1 and 3, Neumann after Round 2. In the final round Williams went to the bottom outright at the 6th, was displaced by Meller's 10 at the 11th, and returned to the bottom for good at the 13th — the start of a closing six in which he shed 16 shots. Final: Williams +40, Meller +35.

## PLAYERS

**David Mullin** — Round scores +5, +4, -1, +1 for +9 net; gross 23, 22, 17, 19 for +81. Never finished a round worse than second of five. Won both the Trophy and the Green Jacket, his third Jacket in succession and his second Trophy.

**Jon Baker** — Defending Trophy champion. Round scores +2, +9, -3, +9 for +17. Led by seven after the 8th hole of the final round and lost by eight. Personal-best gross total, +113.

**Gregg Williams** — Below the field median in all four rounds; Trophy positions 3, 3, 4, 5. Wooden Spoon at +40. Personal-worst round (+15) and personal-worst gross round (+43) at Bletchingley, alongside a personal-best gross total of +152.

**Henry Meller** — Recorded the worst hole in TEG history, a 12 at Ashdown's par-3 11th; bottom of the Spoon race after Rounds 1 and 3 and again briefly in Round 4 after a 10 at Bletchingley's 11th. Finished fourth on the Trophy at +35, escaping the Spoon after two in succession, with a personal-best gross total of +107.

**Stuart Neumann** — Green Jacket runner-up at +100 gross, a personal best, beaten head-to-head by Mullin in all four rounds. Bottom of the Spoon standings at the end of Round 2 at Lingfield.
