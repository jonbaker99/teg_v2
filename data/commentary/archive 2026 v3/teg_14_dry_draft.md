## OVERVIEW

David Mullin won the Trophy (Stableford) with 154 points, 2 ahead of Alex Baker (152). Mullin also won the Green Jacket (gross) at +68 over par, 7 shots clear of runner-up Jon Baker (+75). Jon Baker took the Wooden Spoon with 125 Stableford points, 12 behind Gregg Williams (137). Gregg Williams posted a personal-best gross total for the tournament (+76).

## Championship Links, Championship Form — From the Last Man You'd Expect

Thursday. Royal Cinque Ports Golf Club, Deal, Kent. Second TEG round at this venue.

Mullin finished with 40 Stableford points — best in the field — and led the Trophy by 3 and the Jacket by 5 after R1.

On the Trophy, Mullin drew level with the field at the par-4 11th hole (par, 3 Stableford points) and took an outright lead at H16. On the Jacket, Alex Baker drew level with Mullin at H6; Mullin drew back level at H9. Both lead changes were routine early-round exchanges.

Mullin's most productive stretch was holes 9 to 12 (b37): par at the par-4 9th (3 pts), birdie at the par-4 10th (4 pts), par at the par-4 11th (3 pts), par at the par-4 12th (3 pts) — 13 Stableford points over four holes. His run of clean scoring ended at the 13th (b38), where he ran up a double bogey (6 gross) on the par-4.

Alex Baker suffered two blowups on the back nine at Royal Cinque Ports. At the par-4 11th he scored 8 gross — quadruple bogey (b31, SI3 — one of the hardest). At the par-5 16th he scored 10 gross — quintuple bogey (b23), 0 Stableford points.

Jon Baker moved to last place in the Wooden Spoon standings at H4, where he bogeyed the par-3 4th (SI18 — the easiest hole, sc 4, 1 Stableford point). He stayed in last place from that hole onwards.

## The Easiest Hole on the Course Does the Impossible

Sunday. Prince's Golf Club (Shore / Dunes), Sandwich, Kent. Third TEG round at this venue.

Mullin scored 37 Stableford points — last in the field that round. Alex Baker scored 43 — best in the field. The Trophy lead entering R4 was 8. The final Trophy margin was 2.

Mullin ran up a quadruple bogey at the par-4 10th (8 gross, SI3 — one of the hardest, b06, 0 Stableford points).

Jon Baker played his best round of the tournament — 39 Stableford points. He set a new Prince's - Shore / Dunes course record: 83 gross, beating the prior record of 85 set across 5 prior visits to the venue (cr01). He went 7 holes without dropping a net shot, H9 through H15 (b04): par at the par-4 9th (3 pts), bogey at the par-4 10th (SI3, 2 pts), par at the par-3 11th (2 pts), birdie at the par-5 12th (4 pts), birdie at the par-4 13th (SI1 — the hardest hole, 4 pts), par at the par-4 14th (3 pts), par at the par-5 15th (3 pts). Baker completed his record-setting round despite a quadruple bogey at the par-4 18th (8 gross, b35).

Alex Baker went par-par-par across H13, H14, H15 (b08): par at the par-4 13th (SI1, 4 Stableford points), par at the par-4 14th (SI5, 4 pts), par at the par-5 15th (SI9, 3 pts). ## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

### Trophy

Mullin led wire-to-wire. He drew level at H11 R1 and took an outright lead at H16 R1. Trophy lead after each round: 3 (R1), 9 (R2), 8 (R3), 2 (R4). The margin narrowed in R4 because Baker (Alex) outscored Mullin by 6 Stableford points across the round (43 to 37). Baker's sextuple bogey at the par-4 16th at Prince's in R4 (10 gross, SI17 — the easiest hole on the course, b01) yielded 0 Stableford points. Final: Mullin 154 points, Baker (Alex) 152, margin 2.

### Green Jacket

Mullin led from R1. After early exchanges (Baker drew level on gross at H6; Mullin drew level at H9), Mullin held the Jacket lead for the remaining 63 holes. Jacket lead after each round: 5 shots (R1), 12 shots (R2), 12 shots (R3), 7 shots (R4). Mullin's strongest gross rounds were R1 at Royal Cinque Ports (15 over par — a personal best at that venue, 7 shots better than his TEG 13 score) and R2 at Littlestone (14 over par — a personal best, 7 shots better than TEG 13). Gregg Williams posted a personal-best gross total for the tournament: +76 (b25). Final: Mullin +68, Jon Baker +75, margin 7.

### Wooden Spoon

Jon Baker moved to last place in the Trophy standings at H4 R1 (bogey on the par-3 4th, SI18) and held that position for all four rounds. Round-by-round Trophy scores: 29 (R1), 29 (R2), 28 (R3), 39 (R4) — total 125 points. Gregg Williams was next with 137 points — 12 ahead. Baker's R4 (39 pts) was his best round of the tournament; he set the Prince's course record in the same round (83 gross, cr01). Final: Baker 125 Stableford points — Wooden Spoon.

## PLAYERS

**David Mullin.** Won the Trophy (154 pts, 2 ahead of Alex Baker) and the Green Jacket (+68, 7 ahead of Jon Baker), leading wire-to-wire in both competitions. Had collected three consecutive Wooden Spoons (TEG 12, 13, and was reigning Spoon holder) entering TEG 14.

**Alex Baker.** Finished second in the Trophy with 152 Stableford points. Outscored Mullin in R4 (43 to 37) but scored 0 Stableford at the par-4 16th (SI17) in R4 (sextuple bogey, 10 gross, b01). Suffered blowups in all four rounds, including quintuple at Royal Cinque Ports H16 R1 (b23) and quadruples at Littlestone H5 and H8 in R2 (b27, b24) and Prince's H10 in R3 (b14).

**Jon Baker.** Finished last in the Trophy with 125 Stableford points (Wooden Spoon), 12 behind Gregg Williams. In last place from H4 R1 through the end of R4. Posted 39 Stableford in R4 — his best round of the tournament — and set the Prince's - Shore / Dunes course record (83 gross, cr01), beating the prior record of 85. Arrived at TEG 14 as defending Trophy champion and two-time defending Jacket champion.

**Gregg Williams.** Finished third in the Trophy with 137 Stableford points. Posted a personal-best gross total for the tournament (+76, b25).
