# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 11, Catalonia, Spain (October 2018)

## OVERVIEW

Jon Baker won the Trophy on 143 Stableford points, six clear of Alex Baker. He also won the Green Jacket on gross at +70, three clear of David Mullin. Mullin took the Wooden Spoon, last on the Trophy metric with 125 points.

---

## Round 1 — Jon Baker Rewrites the Stadium: 90, and the Record With It

Thursday, PGA Catalunya - Stadium, the 2nd TEG round at this venue.

Jon Baker shot 90 gross: a new PGA Catalunya - Stadium course record, beating the prior record of 94 (across 5 prior visits) [cr01]. It was worth 35 points and +18 gross. He took the lead in both the Trophy and the Green Jacket at the 4th hole and ended the round leading the Trophy by 3 and the Jacket by 2. Four routine lead changes occurred earlier in the round, at holes 3, 6 and 7, with David Mullin and John Patterson briefly level.

Gregg Williams made 8 at the par-3 5th, a quintuple bogey [b13]. Alex Baker made 8 at the par-4 13th, a quadruple bogey [b17]. Patterson made 9 at the same 13th, a quintuple bogey [b25], and dropped to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race at the 14th, a par 4, with a triple-bogey 7 [b44]. He finished the round last on points.

---

## Round 2 — Two Days, Two Course Records, and Carnage at the 15th

Friday, PGA Catalunya - Tour, the 2nd TEG round at this venue.

Jon Baker shot 84 gross: a new PGA Catalunya - Tour course record, beating the prior record of 89 (across 5 prior visits) [cr02]. The round was worth 41 Stableford points and +12 gross, his best of the tournament on both measures. He led the Trophy by 2 and the Jacket by 3 after two rounds.

The par-5 15th produced two of the round's largest numbers. John Patterson made 11, a sextuple bogey and his worst score relative to par [b12]. Gregg Williams made 10 at the same hole, a quintuple bogey [b14]. Alex Baker made 9 at the par-4 12th, a quintuple bogey [b07].

Patterson signed for 103: a new PGA Catalunya - Tour course-worst, exceeding the prior worst of 101 (across 5 prior visits) [cr03]. He was last on points again after two rounds. Mullin was 4th on points at this stage, on 36 for the round.

---

## Round 3 — Forty-Five Holes in Front, Then Along Came Alex

Saturday, El Prat - Azul, a new course for TEG.

Both leads changed hands. In the gross, Mullin drew level at the 3rd and then went ahead at the 5th, where Jon Baker made par at the par 4 — ending a 37-hole tenure at the top that had run from Round 1's 4th hole [b02]. Jon retook the gross lead outright at the next hole, the par-3 6th, with a bogey [b05]; Mullin made 7 there, a quadruple bogey [b18].

Jon then played holes 7 to 9 in 10 points: par at the par-4 7th, par at the par-4 8th (SI 1, the hardest hole on the course), and a birdie at the par-5 9th [b23]. He dropped 7 shots across holes 11 to 13: a double bogey at the par-4 11th, a double bogey at the par-3 12th (SI 18, the easiest hole on the course), and a triple bogey at the par-4 13th [b42].

Alex Baker had drawn level on the Trophy at the 1st with a bogey. At the 13th, a par 4, he moved outright ahead [b06], ending Jon's Trophy lead after 45 holes in front, held since Round 1's 4th hole [b01]. Jon made par at that hole [b01].

Mullin bogeyed the par-3 12th (SI 18, the easiest hole on the course) and dropped to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race [b21]. He scored 29 points for the round.

Round scores: Jon 32 points, and Alex led the Trophy by 2 overnight. Jon still led the Jacket by 3.

---

## Round 4 — Baker Beats Baker: Six Points, and the Jacket at Last

Sunday, PGA Catalunya - Stadium, the 3rd TEG round at this venue.

David Mullin opened with three pars for 9 points — the par-4 1st, the par-4 2nd, the par-5 3rd [b22] — and drew level for the Green Jacket lead at the 3rd [b10].

Alex Baker dropped 7 shots across holes 4 to 6: a double bogey at the par-4 4th, a double bogey at the par-3 5th, and a triple bogey at the par-4 6th [b38]. At the same 6th, Jon Baker made a double bogey and drew level for the Trophy lead [b09]. He did not trail again.

Alex went on to make 8 at the par-4 9th, a quadruple bogey at SI 1, the hardest hole on the course [b19], and 8 at the par-4 17th, another quadruple bogey [b20]. He finished with 27 points and +34 gross — his worst TEG round to date [b11].

Jon made 8 at the par-4 13th, a quadruple bogey [b16], and still returned 35 points and +19 gross.

Mullin dropped 7 shots across holes 15 to 17: a triple bogey at the par-5 15th (SI 18, the easiest hole on the course), a double bogey at the par-3 16th, and a double bogey at the par-4 17th [b39]. He scored 31 points and finished last on the Trophy metric with 125 [b08].

Gregg Williams made 9 at the par-4 10th, a quintuple bogey [b15].

Final positions: Jon Baker won the Trophy on 143 points by 6 [b03] and the Green Jacket at +70 by 3 [b04].

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Jon Baker led after Round 1 (by 3) and Round 2 (by 2). Alex Baker led after Round 3 (by 2). Jon led after Round 4 (by 6). There were seven lead changes: four routine ones in Round 1, Alex drawing level at Round 3's 1st, Alex going outright ahead at Round 3's 13th after Jon had led for 45 holes, and Jon drawing level again at Round 4's 6th. Decisive moment: Round 4, hole 6 at the Stadium, where Jon's double bogey drew him level while Alex was mid-way through dropping 7 shots in three holes. Final margin: 6 points, 143 to 137.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Jon Baker led after every round — by 2, 3, 3 and 3. Five lead changes: two routine ones in Round 1, Mullin drawing level at Round 3's 3rd, Jon retaking it outright at Round 3's 6th, and Mullin drawing level again at Round 4's 3rd. Decisive moment: Round 3, hole 6 at El Prat, where Jon bogeyed the par 3 and Mullin made 7. Final: +70, by 3 from Mullin.

**Wooden Spoon.** John Patterson was bottom after Rounds 1 and 2. David Mullin was bottom after Rounds 3 and 4. Three changes at the bottom: Williams at Round 1's 1st, Patterson at Round 1's 14th, and Mullin at Round 3's 12th. Decisive moment: Round 3, hole 12 at El Prat — a bogey at the easiest hole on the course dropped Mullin from 4th to last, and he never came back off the bottom. Final: 125 points, having scored 29, 36, 29 and 31.

## PLAYERS

- **Jon Baker.** Jacket runner-up in three of the last four TEGs and never better than third on the Trophy in that time; set new course records at both PGA Catalunya layouts (90 at the Stadium, 84 on the Tour), lost a 45-hole Trophy lead to his brother in Round 3, and won the Trophy, the Green Jacket and both course records.
- **Alex Baker.** Defending Trophy champion; the only player to take the lead off Jon all tournament, held it into Round 4 by two, then posted 27 points — his worst TEG round — to lose by six.
- **David Mullin.** Eight-time Jacket winner and back-to-back champion; new personal bests at both PGA Catalunya courses (85 on the Tour, 91 at the Stadium), gross runner-up by three, and last on points with the Wooden Spoon.
- **John Patterson.** Reigning Spoon holder; an 11 at the Tour's 15th, a course-worst 103, and three quintuple bogeys, but finished above Mullin on points.
- **Gregg Williams.** Three prior Trophy wins; a quintuple bogey in three of the four rounds, and a Round 4 99 at the Stadium, fourteen shots better than his last visit there (113, TEG 6).
