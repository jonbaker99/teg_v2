# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 17, Lisbon Coast, Portugal, October 2024

## OVERVIEW

Jon Baker won the Trophy on 166 Stableford points, 18 clear of David Mullin. He also won the Green Jacket at +67 gross, 13 clear of Mullin. Gregg Williams finished last on the Trophy metric with 123 points and took the Wooden Spoon.

## Praia D'El Rey Sees Something It Has Never Seen in 24 Visits

Round 1, Saturday 5 October, at Praia D'El Rey — the 5th TEG round at the venue. Alex Baker signed for 114 gross, a new course-worst there, exceeding the previous worst of 110 across 24 prior visits. It was built on a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 4th, a sextuple bogey 10 at the par-4 5th (SI 2, one of the hardest), and a quintuple bogey 9 at the par-4 18th. John Patterson also opened with a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 1st.

David Mullin took 16 points from holes 12 to 16 — par at the par-5 12th, birdie at the par-4 13th, par at the par-3 14th, bogey at the par-4 15th, par at the par-4 16th — and finished the round on 33 points, +24 gross.

The Trophy lead changed twice, routinely: Jon Baker took it outright at the 3rd, Patterson took it back outright at the 16th. Patterson led after the round by 1 from Jon Baker, who scored 38 points. In the gross, Jon Baker had led since the 2nd hole; Williams drew level at the 17th and led the Jacket after R1 by 3. At the bottom of the Trophy, the last place passed from Mullin (2nd hole) to Williams (15th) to Alex Baker (18th), who ended the day bottom.

## Eighty-Two and One-Sixteen: West Cliffs Breaks Both Ends in a Single Afternoon

Round 2, Sunday 6 October, at West Cliffs — only the 2nd TEG round at the venue, with 6 prior visits on record. Both ends of the course record fell on the same day.

David Mullin shot 82 gross, beating the prior course record of 88 and improving 12 shots on his only previous visit there.

John Patterson shot 116 gross, a new course-worst, exceeding the prior worst of 115, and his worst gross round to date at +44. The damage ran from the 8th to the 15th: double bogey at the par-4 8th, triple bogeys at the par-4 9th, 10th and 11th, double bogey at the par-3 12th, a quintuple bogey 10 at the par-5 13th, a triple bogey at the par-4 14th (SI 2) and a sextuple bogey 11 at the par-5 15th — 27 shots dropped across eight holes.

Jon Baker took the Trophy lead outright at the 1st with a bogey at the par-4 opening hole (SI 17, one of the easiest) and did not surrender it again. He added 14 points across holes 2 to 5 — par at the par-3 2nd, par at the par-4 3rd (SI 1, the hardest hole), birdie at the par-4 4th, par at the par-3 5th — and posted 44 points and 86 gross, 15 shots better than his previous West Cliffs mark from TEG 15. Alex Baker improved his own West Cliffs best by 5 shots, to 105.

Baker led the Trophy by 2 after the round. In the gross, Mullin drew level at the 18th and led the Jacket by 3. At the bottom, Williams dropped to last at the 2nd and Patterson took it over at the 10th; Patterson was bottom after R2, Williams 4th on 35 points.

## One Hole: Mullin's Green Jacket Reign in Full

Round 3, Monday 7 October, at Royal Óbidos — the 4th TEG round at Seve Ballesteros' final design.

Jon Baker had led the Green Jacket since the 2nd hole of Round 1, a run of 35 holes. A double bogey 6 at the par-4 1st ended it and handed the lead to Mullin. Baker drew level again at the 2nd. Mullin took the lead outright at the 6th with a par at the par-3 6th. Baker drew level at the 7th, one hole later.

Baker then took 20 points from holes 11 to 16: birdie at the par-5 11th, bogey at the par-4 12th (SI 2), birdie at the par-3 13th (SI 18, the easiest hole), and pars at the par-4 14th, par-3 15th and par-4 16th. He signed for 86 gross — a new Royal Óbidos course record, beating the prior best of 87 across 18 prior visits, and 3 shots better than his own TEG 9 mark — and 44 points. He led the Trophy by 11 and the Jacket by 8 overnight.

Gregg Williams double-bogeyed the par-4 14th and dropped to the bottom of the Trophy standings for the first time. He double-bogeyed every hole from the 12th to the 18th — par-4 12th, par-3 13th, par-4 14th, par-3 15th, par-4 16th, par-4 17th, par-5 18th — seven consecutive double bogeys and 14 shots dropped, for a round of 26 points.

Alex Baker dropped 15 shots from the 12th to the 16th: double bogey at the par-4 12th, double bogey at the par-3 13th, a sextuple bogey 10 at the par-4 14th, double bogey at the par-3 15th, triple bogey at the par-4 16th. John Patterson took 13 points from holes 12 to 15 — par at the par-4 12th (SI 2), par at the par-3 13th (SI 18), bogey at the par-4 14th, par at the par-3 15th — and posted 94 gross, a personal best at the course and 7 better than his previous visit.

## Baker Wins the Lot Despite Two Nines

Round 4, Tuesday 8 October, back at Praia D'El Rey — the 6th TEG round there.

Jon Baker opened with a double bogey at the par-4 1st, bogeys at the par-5 2nd and par-3 3rd, then a birdie at the par-4 4th. He made a quadruple bogey 9 at the par-5 10th and another quadruple bogey 9 at the par-5 17th (SI 1, the hardest hole), with 9 points from holes 14 to 16 in between — par at the par-3 14th, bogey at the par-4 15th, par at the par-4 16th. He scored 40 points, finished the Trophy on 166 points and the Green Jacket at +67, and led both at the close.

Alex Baker dropped 25 shots across holes 5 to 13: double bogeys at the par-4 5th (SI 2) and par-4 6th, triple bogeys at the par-5 7th and par-3 8th (SI 16, one of the easiest), double bogeys at the par-4 9th and par-5 10th, a triple bogey at the par-3 11th, and quadruple bogeys at the par-5 12th and par-4 13th.

John Patterson made a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 1st, a sextuple bogey 9 at the par-3 14th (SI 17, one of the easiest), a sextuple bogey 10 at the par-4 15th (SI 3, one of the hardest) and a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 18th.

Gregg Williams made a quintuple bogey 9 at the par-4 1st and scored 29 points.

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Round-by-round leaders: Patterson by 1 after R1; Jon Baker by 2 after R2; Baker by 11 after R3; Baker by 18 at the finish. Three lead changes in total, two of them routine early exchanges in R1 (Baker at the 3rd, Patterson at the 16th). The decisive change came at the 1st hole of R2 at West Cliffs, where Baker took the lead outright and held it for the remaining 71 holes. Back-to-back rounds of 44 in R2 and R3 built the margin; a 40 in R4 completed it. Final: Jon Baker 166, from Mullin by 18.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Round-by-round leaders: Williams by 3 after R1; Mullin by 3 after R2; Jon Baker by 8 after R3; Baker by 13 at the finish. Five lead changes; only one was outright — Mullin's at the 6th hole of R3 at Royal Óbidos, which lasted one hole before Baker drew level at the 7th. Baker's 86 course record that afternoon established the 8-shot overnight margin. Baker's gross rounds were +20, +14, +14, +19. Final: Jon Baker +67, from Mullin by 13.

**Wooden Spoon.** Bottom place by round: Alex Baker after R1, Patterson after R2, Williams after R3 and R4. Williams was 3rd after R1 (33 points) and 4th after R2 (35). The decisive drop came at the 14th at Royal Óbidos in R3, where his double bogey moved him from 4th to last for the first time; the seven straight double bogeys home produced a 26-point round. R4's 29 points, including the quintuple bogey at the 1st, left him on 123 points and last.

## PLAYERS

- **Jon Baker** — Fourth in the Trophy in each of the last three TEGs and without a Trophy since TEG 13. Took the Trophy lead on the first hole of R2 and held it to the end; rounds of 38, 44, 44 and 40 for 166 points; set the Royal Óbidos course record of 86 and personal course bests at both Royal Óbidos and West Cliffs; won the Trophy by 18 and the Green Jacket by 13.
- **David Mullin** — Nine-time Green Jacket holder. Shot 82 at West Cliffs in R2, a new course record and 12 shots better than his only previous visit there. Held the outright gross lead for one hole in R3, from the 6th to the 7th at Royal Óbidos. Runner-up in both the Trophy and the Green Jacket.
- **Gregg Williams** — Arrived as reigning back-to-back Green Jacket holder and TEG 15 Trophy champion. Led the Jacket after R1. Scored 26 points in R3, finishing with seven consecutive double bogeys, and took the second Wooden Spoon of his career on 123 points.
- **John Patterson** — Led the Trophy after R1, was bottom of it by the end of R2 after a West Cliffs 116, a course-worst and his worst gross round to date, built on a 10 at the 13th and an 11 at the 15th. Posted a personal-best 94 at Royal Óbidos in R3, then made a 9 at the 14th and a 10 at the 15th at Praia D'El Rey in R4.
- **Alex Baker** — Reigning and back-to-back Wooden Spoon holder. Opened with a 114 at Praia D'El Rey, a course-worst across 24 prior visits; improved his West Cliffs best by 5 shots to 105 in R2; dropped 15 shots in five holes in R3 and 25 shots in nine holes in R4; avoided a third straight Spoon.
