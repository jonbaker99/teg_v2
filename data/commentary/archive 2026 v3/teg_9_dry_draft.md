# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 9, Lisbon Coast, Portugal, October 2016

## OVERVIEW

John Patterson won the Trophy with 168 Stableford points, six clear of Alex Baker — the highest Trophy total in TEG history. David Mullin won the Green Jacket at +67 gross, by 10 from Jon Baker, the lowest gross total in TEG history and his seventh Jacket. Stuart Neumann took the Wooden Spoon on 127 points, his second in succession.

Four rounds, two courses, alternating: Royal Óbidos (R1, R3) and Praia D'El Rey (R2, R4). TEG's third visit to the Lisbon Coast.

---

## Round 1 — 87 and 115: Royal Óbidos Has Its Best and Worst Day at Once

Friday, Royal Óbidos, the second TEG round played there.

Mullin went round in 87 gross (+15). That is a new Royal Óbidos course record, beating the 90 that had stood across six prior visits (cr01), and also his personal best there — three better than TEG 7. On the same afternoon Alex Baker shot 115 gross (+43), a new Royal Óbidos course-worst, exceeding the prior worst of 108 across six prior visits (cr02). Baker's card included an 11 at the par-4 14th, a septuple bogey and his worst score to par on any hole (b04).

Patterson opened with 38 points despite a 10 at the par-4 4th — SI 1, the hardest hole on the course — a sextuple bogey worth no points (b05). He also made a 7 at the par-3 15th. That left him second, one point behind Jon Baker, whose 89 was nine shots better than his last visit to Royal Óbidos.

Gregg Williams made a 9 at the par-3 15th, a sextuple bogey and his worst hole relative to par to that point (b25).

Neumann scored 23 points. He made a 9 at the 4th and then dropped 16 shots over the closing five holes: triple bogey 7 at the par-4 14th, quintuple bogey 8 at the par-3 15th, triple bogey 7 at the par-4 16th, double bogey 6 at the par-4 17th, triple bogey 8 at the par-5 18th (b44). The 8 at the 15th (b29) put him last outright.

Trophy lead changes in the opening holes were routine: Jon Baker level at the 2nd, Mullin level at the 3rd, Williams level at the 4th, Patterson outright at the 5th, Jon Baker level again at the 6th. In the Gross, Mullin led outright from the 3rd, Jon Baker drew level at the 6th, Mullin drew level again at the 15th, and Mullin took the outright lead at the 18th — by two at the end of the round.

---

## Round 2 — Forty-Nine at Praia D'El Rey: Second-Best Round in TEG History

Saturday, Praia D'El Rey, the third TEG round there.

Patterson took the outright Trophy lead at the 1st hole. Mullin drew level at the 2nd; Patterson drew level again at the 11th. Patterson finished with 49 points (+14 gross), the second-best round in TEG history to that date (b03).

The scoring came in blocks. Holes 9 to 13 produced 17 points: bogey at the par-4 9th, par at the par-5 10th, par at the par-3 11th, birdie at the par-5 12th, par at the par-4 13th (b06). The last four holes produced 14 more: par at the par-4 15th (SI 3), bogey at the par-4 16th, par at the par-5 17th (SI 1, the hardest hole), bogey at the par-4 18th (b19).

That turned a one-point deficit into a 10-point lead.

Alex Baker posted a personal-best 44 points and a personal-best gross round of +27 (b07, b08) — 100 gross, nine better than his previous best at Praia D'El Rey in TEG 7.

Williams made a 10 at the par-4 9th, a sextuple bogey (b26).

Mullin shot +14 and led the Gross by seven.

---

## Round 3 — Ten Becomes Three: Óbidos Bites the Leader

Sunday, Royal Óbidos, the third TEG round there.

Patterson scored 34. He made a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 12th, SI 2, one of the hardest holes (b31), and a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 18th (b32). His lead fell from 10 points to three.

Mullin made a quintuple-bogey 9 at the par-4 10th, his worst hole relative to par (b16), and still shot +22 and held the Gross lead at seven.

Jon Baker took 16 points from the opening five holes: par at the par-4 1st, birdie at the par-5 2nd, par at the par-3 3rd, bogey at the par-4 4th (SI 1), par at the par-5 5th (b18). He also made a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 11th.

Alex Baker made a quintuple-bogey 9 at the par-4 14th (b21) but still returned 102 gross, a personal best at Royal Óbidos by six shots. Williams shot 97 there, three better than his previous best. Neumann shot 98, one better than his previous best.

---

## Round 4 — Level at the 14th, Eleven Shots Later Alex Baker Was Second

Monday, Praia D'El Rey, the fourth TEG round there.

Alex Baker drew level with Patterson at the top of the Trophy table at the par-3 14th, where he made a double-bogey 5 (b23). It was not an outright lead; the two were tied.

Over the next three holes Baker made a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 15th (SI 3), a triple-bogey 7 at the par-4 16th and a double-bogey 7 at the par-5 17th (SI 1). Including the double at the 14th, that is 11 shots dropped over four holes (b45). He also made a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 6th. He still equalled his personal-best round of 44 points and his personal-best gross round of +27 (b09, b10).

Patterson made a quintuple-bogey 9 at the par-4 5th, SI 2, one of the hardest holes (b17), and closed with 47 points for a total of 168 — the highest Trophy total in TEG history — winning by six (b01). His gross total of +88 was his personal best (b15).

Mullin shot +16 to finish on +67, by 10 (b02). His round included a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 1st.

Williams made an 11 at the par-5 7th, a sextuple bogey and his worst hole relative to par (b27). His gross total of +115 was a personal best (b13).

Jon Baker made a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 17th (SI 1) and finished on +77 gross, a personal best (b14).

Neumann scored 37, matching his R3 score. He took 16 points from holes 11 to 15: par at the par-3 11th, par at the par-5 12th, birdie at the par-4 13th, par at the par-3 14th (SI 17, one of the easiest), bogey at the par-4 15th (b11). He finished bottom on 127 points (b22).

Alex Baker's gross total of +127 was a personal best (b12).

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Jon Baker led after R1 by one, with Patterson second. Patterson took the outright lead at the 1st hole of R2 and led after every subsequent round: by 10 after R2, by three after R3, by six at the finish. The R2 49 (b03) was the round that built the margin; the R3 34, with quadruple bogeys at the 12th and 18th at Royal Óbidos (b31, b32), cut it to three. The only R4 lead change came at the 14th at Praia D'El Rey, where Alex Baker drew level (b23). Baker then dropped 11 shots over holes 14 to 17 (b45) while Patterson scored 47. Final: Patterson 168, Alex Baker 162, margin six (b01).

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Mullin led after every round: by two, seven, seven and 10. The lead changed four times, all in R1 — Mullin outright at the 3rd, Jon Baker level at the 6th, Mullin level at the 15th, Mullin outright at the 18th, completing the course-record 87 (cr01). He was not headed across the remaining 54 holes. Round gross scores: +15, +14, +22, +16. The quintuple-bogey 9 at the 10th in R3 (b16) cost him nothing. Final: +67 to Jon Baker's +77, margin 10 (b02, b14).

**Wooden Spoon.** Neumann was bottom after all four rounds. Position at the bottom changed three times in R1 — Williams after the 1st, Alex Baker after the 2nd, Neumann from the 11th — and Neumann's quintuple-bogey 8 at the par-3 15th (b29), inside a stretch that cost 16 shots from the 14th (b44), left him last outright for the rest of the tournament. His round scores improved every day: 23, 30, 37, 37. Final total 127 points; back-to-back Spoons after TEG 8 (b22).

## PLAYERS

**John Patterson** — No previous TEG honours and first visit to both courses. Rounds of 38, 49, 34, 47 for 168 points, the highest Trophy total in TEG history, won by six (b01); gross total +88, a personal best (b15). Made a 10 at the SI-1 4th in R1 (b05) and a 9 at the SI-2 5th in R4 (b17).

**Alex Baker** — Opened with 115 gross at Royal Óbidos, the worst round recorded there (cr02), including an 11 at the 14th (b04). Personal-best 44-point rounds in R2 and R4 (b07, b09), personal-best gross rounds of +27 in both (b08, b10), personal bests at both courses (100 and 102) and a personal-best gross total of +127 (b12). Drew level for the Trophy lead at the R4 14th (b23), then lost 11 shots over holes 14 to 17 (b45). Second, six back.

**David Mullin** — Course-record 87 at Royal Óbidos in R1 (cr01), also his personal best there. Led the Gross from the 18th hole of R1 to the finish. Gross rounds +15, +14, +22, +16 for +67, the lowest gross total in TEG history and a seventh Green Jacket (b02). Quintuple-bogey 9 at the 10th in R3 (b16).

**Jon Baker** — Led the Trophy after R1 on the back of an 89 at Royal Óbidos, nine better than his previous visit. Took 16 points from the first five holes in R3 (b18). Gross runner-up at +77, a personal best (b14), and second in the Jacket for the fourth time in five TEGs.

**Gregg Williams** — Personal-best gross total of +115 (b13) and a personal-best 97 at Royal Óbidos in R3, but seven worse than TEG 7 at Praia D'El Rey. One large hole per round: 9 at the 15th in R1 (b25), 10 at the 9th in R2 (b26), 11 at the 7th in R4 (b27).

**Stuart Neumann** — Reigning Spoon holder. Last outright from the 15th hole of R1 (b29) after dropping 16 shots over the closing five (b44) and stayed bottom for all four rounds. Round scores 23, 30, 37, 37, including 16 points over holes 11 to 15 in R4 (b11) and a personal-best 98 at Royal Óbidos in R3; 14 shots worse than his TEG 7 round at Praia D'El Rey. Total 127 points and a second consecutive Wooden Spoon (b22).
