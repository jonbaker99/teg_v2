# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 3, Boavista Golf & Spa Resort, Lagos, Algarve, 5–8 October 2010

## OVERVIEW

Jon Baker won the Trophy on net vs par at −18, 18 clear of runner-up David Mullin — the 9th-best Trophy score in TEG history to date and Baker's first TEG title. Mullin won the Green Jacket on gross at +72, 12 clear of Stuart Neumann. Henry Meller took the Wooden Spoon at +16 net vs par, his second in succession. All four rounds were played at Boavista, the 4th to 7th TEG rounds at the venue and TEG's 2nd visit to the Algarve.

## ROUND 1 — Baker Plunders, Mullin Purrs, Meller Finds the 17th

Tuesday, the 4th TEG round at Boavista.

Baker signed for 90 gross, +19 to par and −9 net (b03) — the best round in TEG history to date on the Trophy metric, and the 3rd-best gross round in TEG history to date (b10). The 90 beat his previous Boavista best of 100, set in TEG 2, by 10 shots, and was 17 better than his last visit here.

Mullin went round in 88 gross, +17 (b02), the best gross round in TEG history to date, and −1 net, the 3rd-best round in TEG history to date on the Trophy metric (b09). He drew level on gross at the 4th; Meller also drew level on gross at the 4th. Neither took the lead outright. On the Trophy, Mullin drew level with Baker at the 6th — the only lead change of the entire Trophy, and routine, with the field bunched.

The Spoon race changed hands five times: Neumann to the bottom at the 3rd, Meller at the 6th, Neumann again at the 13th and the 15th, and Meller at the 17th. That last one came via a 12 at the par-4 17th, +8 on the hole and 7 over net (b06) — the worst hole relative to par in TEG history to date, on stroke index 17, the second-easiest hole on the card. It dropped Meller from 3rd to 5th and last (b61).

Gregg Williams played the closing four holes in 13 over: a triple bogey at the par-4 15th (stroke index 1), a triple at the par-5 16th, a triple at the 17th and a quadruple bogey — an 8 — at the par-4 18th (b45, b31).

Baker finished the round leading the Trophy by 8. Mullin led the Jacket by 2. Meller was last.

## ROUND 2 — Meller's Finest Hour, Baker's Quiet Larceny

Wednesday, the 5th TEG round at Boavista.

Meller shot 89 gross, +18 (b13), the 2nd-best gross round in TEG history to date, for −2 net (b12), also the 2nd-best round in TEG history to date on the Trophy metric. It was a personal Boavista best and lifted him from last to 3rd.

Baker also returned −2 net (b07), from 97 gross, +26, level with Meller as the 2nd-best round in TEG history to date. His round included five consecutive double bogeys — the 10th (par 4), 11th (par 3), 12th (par 4), 13th (par 5) and 14th (par 3) — ten shots dropped across the stretch.

Mullin took a 9 at the par-4 17th, a quintuple bogey, +5 gross and +4 net (b18). Williams made a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 10th, stroke index 3 (b32).

Neumann dropped to the bottom of the Spoon race at the 8th and finished the round there. Baker's Trophy lead grew to 13; Mullin's Jacket lead grew to 5.

## ROUND 3 — Eighty-Five: Mullin Takes Boavista Apart

Thursday, the 6th TEG round at Boavista.

Mullin shot 85 gross — a new Boavista course record, beating the 89 that had stood across 15 prior visits, and 4 better than his own previous best here (cr01). At +14 it was the best gross round in TEG history to date (b04), superseding his own R1 mark, and at −4 net it was the 3rd-best round in TEG history to date on the Trophy metric (b11).

Neumann shot 89 gross, +18, the 3rd-best gross round in TEG history to date (b14). Williams shot 97 gross, +26, for −4 net (b15), the 3rd-best round in TEG history to date on the Trophy metric; the 97 was 8 shots better than his previous Boavista best of 105, set in TEG 2.

Baker returned 92 gross, +21, for −7 net (b08), the 2nd-best round in TEG history to date on the Trophy metric. It included a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 5th and a quintuple bogey 9 at the par-4 18th (b19).

The Spoon changed hands three times in three holes. Meller took a triple bogey 7 at the par-4 1st and fell from 3rd to last (b27). Williams then made a quadruple bogey 7 at the par-3 2nd, stroke index 16, and dropped to last himself (b28, b33). At the par-5 3rd Meller made a 10, a quintuple bogey, +5 gross and +4 net (b22), and returned to the bottom (b29), where he stayed.

Baker's Trophy lead went to 16. Mullin's Jacket lead went to 9.

## ROUND 4 — The 17th Claims One Last Victim

Friday, the 7th TEG round at Boavista.

Baker played the round in level net par, which was enough to close out the Trophy at −18 and an 18-shot margin (b01).

Mullin shot +20 gross, taking his total to +72 and the Green Jacket by 12 from Neumann (b05).

Neumann made a 10 at the par-4 17th, a sextuple bogey, +6 gross and +5 net (b17) — the third large number that hole took across the four days, after Meller's 12 in R1 and Mullin's 9 in R2.

Williams made three more quadruple bogeys: a 9 at the par-5 3rd (b35), an 8 at the par-4 7th (b36) and an 8 at the par-4 12th (b37). Meller also made a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 7th (b38) and signed for +4 on the round, completing a +16 total and the Wooden Spoon (b20).

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (net vs par).** Baker led after every round: by 8 after R1, 13 after R2, 16 after R3, 18 at the finish. The only lead change in the competition came at the 6th hole of R1, when Mullin drew level — not outright, and routine at that stage. Baker was not headed across the remaining 66 holes. The decisive round was R1's −9 (b03), built on a 90 gross that was 10 better than any previous Boavista round of his. Final margin: 18 over Mullin, at −18.

**Green Jacket (gross).** Mullin led wire to wire: 2 clear after R1, 5 after R2, 9 after R3, 12 at the finish. The two lead changes both came at the 4th hole of R1, when Mullin and then Meller drew level; neither was outright. Mullin's four rounds were +17, +21, +14 and +20. The course-record 85 in R3 (cr01, b04) turned a 5-shot cushion into 9 and left R4 with nothing to decide. Final: +72, by 12 from Neumann.

**Wooden Spoon.** Meller was bottom after R1, Neumann after R2, Meller after R3 and R4. There were nine changes at the bottom, five of them in R1. Meller's 12 at the R1 17th (b06) put him last for the first time (b61). His R2 89 lifted him to 3rd. R3 reversed it inside three holes: the triple at the 1st (b27), Williams's quadruple at the 2nd taking it off him briefly (b28), and his own 10 at the 3rd (b22) returning him to last for good (b29) — the decisive moment. A +4 in R4 confirmed it. Final: +16 net vs par.

## PLAYERS

- **Jon Baker** — Runner-up in TEG 2 and previously without a title. Opened with −9, the best round in TEG history to date, led the Trophy from the 6th hole of R1 to the finish, and won by 18. His R1 90 gross was 17 shots better than his last visit to Boavista.
- **David Mullin** — Defending Trophy champion. Posted two TEG-record gross rounds (+17 in R1, +14 in R3) and the Boavista course record of 85, won the Green Jacket by 12, and finished second on the Trophy by 18.
- **Henry Meller** — Reigning Wooden Spoon holder. Set a personal Boavista best of 89 in R2 (−2 net, 2nd-best round in TEG history to date), then dropped to last in R3 and took a second consecutive Spoon at +16.
- **Stuart Neumann** — Second on gross at +84, second-best gross player of the tournament, including an +18 89 in R3. Was bottom of the Spoon race at the end of R2, and made a 10 at the 17th in R4.
- **Gregg Williams** — Improved his Boavista best by eight shots with a 97 in R3 (−4 net), while recording quadruple bogeys at the 6th and 18th in R1, the 10th in R2, the 2nd in R3 and the 3rd, 7th and 12th in R4. Held the bottom Spoon place for two holes of R3.
