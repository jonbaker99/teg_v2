# The Family Business: Alex Baker Takes the Trophy, Jon Baker Takes the Spoon

## Alex Baker leads from the 5th hole of R1 to the last, winning the Trophy on 169 points by 8 — while his gross game falls apart around him

Alex Baker won the Trophy with 169 points, finishing eight clear of runner-up John Patterson. It stands as the seventh-highest Stableford total on record and Baker's own second-best.

The points came in bursts. In the first round at PGA Catalunya's Tour course he took 17 points from holes 8 to 12, a run of three bogeys and two pars that his handicap turned into a profit, then added 13 more across holes 14 to 16, including a birdie at the par-5 15th. His most productive stretch came in round three on the Stadium course, where holes 11 to 16 yielded 21 points from three pars and three bogeys. He led the Trophy after round two, again after round three, and still after round four.

The gross scoring told a different story. His second round of 106 at the Tour course set a new course-worst there, beating the previous mark of 105 across 22 prior visits. That round included an eight at the par-3 16th and an eleven at the par-5 18th, both zero-point holes and both his worst on those pars. Round four brought more of the same: a ten at the 14th and an eight at the 17th, again for no points.

The gross damage cost him elsewhere. Second in the Green Jacket after round one, Baker finished the week fifth in that competition, with Gregg Williams leading it from round three onwards.

## David Mullin's 84 at the Stadium in R3 — a new all-time TEG course record — bookended by an 11 and a collapse

David Mullin's week ran to extremes at both ends of the scale.

It began badly. On the 15th at PGA Catalunya's Tour course in Round 1 — a par 5, stroke index 5 — he ran up an 11, a sextuple bogey worth no Stableford points. It was the worst score he has recorded on a par 5. The damage was immediate in the season-long picture too: the hole dropped him from second to fifth in the Wooden Spoon race, taking him to the bottom of that particular table.

Two rounds later he produced the outstanding round of the tournament. Mullin shot 84 gross at the Stadium course in Round 3, a new all-time TEG course record there. The previous best was 89, set across 27 prior visits to the course, so the mark fell by five shots.

The final round returned him to earth on the same course. Beginning at the 14th, Mullin played four consecutive holes without a net par: a double bogey at the par-4 14th, a triple bogey at the par-5 15th, a double at the par-3 16th and another double at the 17th. Across that stretch he dropped nine shots to par and collected just three Stableford points from a possible eight.

An 11 at one end, a course record in the middle, and a closing collapse — the full range in a single tournament.

## John Patterson finishes second having thrown the Trophy away in the first eight holes he played

**Patterson's tournament turned on eight holes at Catalunya**

John Patterson's opening round came apart almost immediately. From the 3rd to the 10th on the Tour course he dropped 22 gross shots in eight holes: a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 3rd, triple bogeys at the 4th, 7th, 8th and 9th, and double bogeys at the 5th, 6th and 10th. The stretch returned eight Stableford points in total, and from the 3rd through the 9th he went seven consecutive holes without a net par, finishing that run eight points short of the expected return. The damage was immediate enough that the triple bogey at the 4th moved him from fourth to fifth in the Wooden Spoon standings.

What followed made the start harder to accept. Patterson played the back nine of that round 14 points better than the front, including 13 points across holes 13 to 16 — par, par, bogey, par — on the way to 34 points for the day at 30 over gross.

The pattern repeated in round two: a quadruple-bogey 8 at the 4th and 11 shots dropped between the 6th and 10th, then 16 points in five holes from the 11th to the 15th. In round four on the Stadium course he took nine points from holes 4 to 6, and after a triple at the 14th and a double at the 15th, a birdie at the 17th ended the run.

## Gregg Williams, the reigning Wooden Spoon holder, wins the Green Jacket at +66 by 14

**Williams takes the Green Jacket**

Gregg Williams won the Green Jacket with a gross total of +66, finishing 14 clear of runner-up David Mullin. The score ranks sixth on the all-time list.

The win did not begin smoothly. Williams opened at PGA Catalunya's Tour course with a run of dropped shots through the front nine, including triple bogeys at the 3rd and 4th and a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-five 7th. A birdie at the 15th ended a long sequence of bogeys or worse, but another quadruple bogey followed at the 17th, an 8 on the par four. He signed for 31 points.

Round two was a different story. Williams scored 42 points, an eleven-point improvement, and took the gross lead from Jon Baker at the 7th hole, ending Baker's 20-hole tenure in front. His best work came at the 13th to 16th, four holes played in level par for 13 points.

He moved to the Stadium course and took outright command of the gross competition with a par at the 2nd in round three, part of another four-hole run without a dropped shot from the 2nd to the 5th worth 12 points. A double bogey at the 6th ended that streak.

In round four he birdied the 5th and picked up nine points across the 8th to 10th. In the Trophy competition he closed to nine off the lead having trailed by as many as 15.

## Jon Baker, defending Trophy champion, collects the Wooden Spoon on 126 points, 23 adrift of the next worst

**Jon Baker: the Wooden Spoon**

Jon Baker's tournament ended with the Wooden Spoon, his 126 Stableford points leaving him at the foot of the field.

The week was not without its better moments. Baker held the Green Jacket gross lead from the fifth hole of round one, a run of 20 holes across two rounds, before a bogey at the par-five seventh at PGA Catalunya's Tour course handed the lead to Gregg Williams. He recovered it by the end of the day. His closing five holes in round two were the best golf he played: a birdie at the par-three 14th followed by four straight pars, five holes without dropping a gross shot and 16 Stableford points in total. That left him leading the Green Jacket after 36 holes, with Alex Baker top of the Trophy standings.

The third round at the Stadium course undid much of it. At the par-four fourth Baker ran up a nine, a quintuple bogey worth no points at all, and the damage to his Stableford total was permanent.

By the finish he was well clear at the bottom of the Trophy table. For a player who had spent part of the week in front of one competition, ending the tournament with the Wooden Spoon on 126 points was an unusual combination of highs and lows.
