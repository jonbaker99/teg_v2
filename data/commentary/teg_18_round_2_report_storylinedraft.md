# The Course Record Nobody Wanted

## Gregg Williams and John Patterson share the day's best Stableford return
<!-- storyline: round -->

Gregg Williams and John Patterson both carded 42 points, the best Stableford return of the day in the 2025 TEG Trophy, though they arrived there by very different routes.

Patterson's round swung wildly. A quadruple bogey at the 4th, one of the hardest holes on the course, was followed by a horror stretch from the 6th to the 10th in which he shed 11 shots. He recovered with his best spell of the round, taking 16 points across holes 11 to 15, a run built on three pars sandwiching two bogeys. It was enough to set a course personal best for this TEG, improving his best gross score here to 94.

Williams built his 42 more steadily. Having opened with just 31 points in round one, he turned in 11 shots better on the same course, gathering nine points across holes 3 to 5 and closing strongly with a bogey-free stretch from 13 to 16 worth 13 points, four holes that included the course's hardest hole without dropping a shot.

## Alex Baker sets an unwanted course record at PGA Catalunya
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Round 2 at PGA Catalunya's Tour Course produced the worst round the venue has seen in six TEG visits. Alex Baker carded a gross 106, breaking the previous course-worst of 105 set across 27 prior visits.

The damage came in clusters. An early cold stretch on holes 3 to 5 bled seven shots, with a triple bogey followed by two double bogeys. The round unravelled further on the back nine: a quintuple bogey 8 on the par-3 16th, one of the easiest holes on the course, was followed immediately by a birdie on the 17th to stop the bleeding — only for a sextuple bogey 11 on the par-5 18th to close things out.

Remarkably, none of it cost Alex Baker his position at the top of the 2025 TEG Trophy standings. He remained the leader after the round, though his cushion shrank from twelve shots to eleven.

## Green Jacket lead survives a five-swing day, Wooden Spoon changes hands
<!-- storyline: race -->

At PGA Catalunya - Tour, the Sunday second round turned the 2025 Green Jacket (Gross) into a shuttle service that finished exactly where it started. Jon Baker had held the gross lead since the 5th hole of round one, a stretch of 20 holes, before a bogey at the par-5 7th handed it to Gregg Williams. The lead then swapped hands repeatedly through the middle of the round — Williams levelling at the 6th, Jon Baker retaking it outright at the 9th, Williams squaring again at the 10th and once more at the 13th, five swings in all. By the close, Jon Baker was back in front, the overall gap unchanged at two.

David Mullin's round collapsed in the same stretch: three holes from the 4th — one of the hardest on the course — cost him 11 shots, two quadruple bogeys either side of a triple. A double bogey at the last confirmed the damage, dropping him to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon standings in place of Jon Baker, the gap to safety now two shots.

Elsewhere, Alex Baker's card tied the round's best Trophy score, keeping him out front for the 2025 Trophy.
