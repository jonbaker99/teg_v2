# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 10, Algarve, Portugal, 2017

## OVERVIEW

Alex Baker won the Trophy on 184 Stableford points, 11 clear of Gregg Williams (b02). David Mullin won the Green Jacket at +51 gross, 12 clear of Jon Baker (b03). John Patterson took the Wooden Spoon on 112 points (b28). Four rounds: Boavista, two Palmares combinations, then Boavista again.

---

## Round 1 — Mullin Rewrites Boavista at the Eleventh Attempt

Saturday at Boavista, the 11th TEG round at the venue.

David Mullin shot 75 gross, +4. That is a new Boavista course record, beating the prior record of 82 set across 50 prior visits (cr01), and the best gross round in TEG history to date (b05). It was also worth 49 Stableford points, the second-best round in TEG history to date (b07).

The Trophy lead moved five times inside the round, with the field bunched: Alex Baker and Mullin both drew level at the 6th, Jon Baker led outright at the 9th, Mullin drew level at the 10th, Alex Baker drew level at the 11th. Mullin then took the lead at the 12th and kept it for the next 30 holes (b01 context). He finished the round 8 points clear on the Trophy and 14 shots clear on the Jacket.

The closing hole took two scorecards apart. Stuart Neumann made 10 at the par-4 18th, a sextuple bogey (b31). Gregg Williams made 9 there, a quintuple bogey (b27).

John Patterson made 10 at the par-5 8th, SI 2, one of the hardest holes on the course (b33). He had dropped to the bottom of the field at the 4th and did not leave it again.

Alex Baker made 7 at the par-3 2nd, SI 16, one of the easiest holes (b37), and 9 at the par-5 8th (b38). He signed for 41 points, second, 8 back.

---

## Round 2 — New Ground, New Personal Bests — and a 10 at the First

Sunday at Palmares, the Praia and Alvor nines combined — a new course for TEG, seaside links spliced onto parkland.

Three career numbers. Gregg Williams posted a personal-best round of 48 points (b15) and a personal-best gross round of +14 (b16). Alex Baker posted a personal-best 45 points (b09) and a personal-best gross round of +23 (b10). Jon Baker went round in +10 gross, the third-best gross round in TEG history to date (b08).

John Patterson opened with 10 at the par-4 1st, a sextuple bogey (b24).

Stuart Neumann dropped 13 shots across holes 13 to 15: 10 at the par-5 13th (b52), 9 at the par-4 14th, 7 at the par-4 15th — +5, +5 and +3 (b88). He then made 10 at the par-4 18th, a sextuple bogey and his second 10 at a closing hole in two days (b32).

Mullin went round in +11 gross. His Trophy lead shrank from 8 to 5; his Jacket lead stood at 13.

---

## Round 3 — Thirty Holes in Front, Undone by a Par

Monday at Palmares, the Alvor and Lagos nines — parkland, again new ground for TEG.

The lead changed hands repeatedly. Alex Baker drew level at the 4th; Gregg Williams drew level at the 4th. At the 6th, a par-4 of SI 11, Alex Baker made par for 4 points and took the lead outright (b14), ending Mullin's run of 30 holes in front, held since the 12th hole of Round 1 (b01). Mullin drew level again at the 7th.

At the par-5 13th, SI 4, Williams made bogey 6 for 3 points and took the lead outright (b19). At the same hole, Alex Baker made 10, a quintuple bogey (b26). Baker drew level again at the 15th.

Despite the 10, Baker posted a personal-best 47 points (b11) and a personal-best gross round of +22 (b12). He ended the round leading by 11.

John Patterson made 10 at the par-5 8th, a quintuple bogey (b34). Across holes 11 to 15 he dropped 19 shots: 7 at the par-4 11th (SI 2, one of the hardest), 8 at the par-3 12th (SI 18, the easiest hole on the course), 8 at the par-5 13th, 7 at the par-3 14th and 9 at the par-5 15th — +3, +5, +3, +4, +4 (b29, b35). He scored 27 points.

David Mullin made 8 at the par-4 18th, a quadruple bogey (b39). He went round in +23 gross and led the Jacket by 10.

---

## Round 4 — Fifty-One: The Best Round Ever Played in TEG

Tuesday at Boavista, the 12th TEG round at the venue.

Gregg Williams birdied the par-4 5th and drew level with Alex Baker at the top of the Trophy (b30). That was the closest he got.

Baker then took 21 points from holes 5 to 10: bogey at the par-4 5th, par at the par-3 6th, par at the par-4 7th, bogey at the par-5 8th (SI 2), par at the par-4 9th (SI 18, the easiest hole on the course) and par at the par-4 10th (SI 3) — 3, 4, 4, 3, 3, 4 points (b04).

Baker finished with 51 points, the best round in TEG history to date (b06), at +17 gross, another personal best (b13). That gave him 184 points and the Trophy by 11 (b02), plus a personal-best gross total of +90 (b20).

Williams made 7 at the par-3 6th (b44) and 8 at the par-4 18th (b45). He finished second on the Trophy and set a personal-best gross total of +76 (b21). He also recorded a new Boavista personal best of 93 gross.

Mullin went round in +13 to finish at +51 and win the Green Jacket by 12 (b03). Jon Baker was runner-up with a personal-best gross total of +63 (b22) and a new Boavista personal best of 86 gross.

John Patterson made 11 at the par-5 8th, SI 2, a sextuple bogey and his worst score at a par 5 to date (b25), and 9 at the par-4 18th (b36). He signed for 21 points, his worst round to date (b40), and finished on 112 points (b28).

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Mullin led after Round 1 by 8 and after Round 2 by 5. Alex Baker was second in both, on 41 and 45 points. The decisive change came at the 6th hole of Round 3 at Palmares Alvor/Lagos: Baker's par ended Mullin's 30-hole reign and gave Baker the outright lead (b01, b14). Williams took it back outright at the 13th in the same round (b19) and drew level again at the 5th in Round 4 (b30), but never led outright thereafter. Baker's 21 points across holes 5 to 10 in Round 4 (b04) and his 51-point round (b06) settled it. Final: 184 points, winning by 11 (b02). Round scores 41, 45, 47, 51.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Mullin led after every round — by 14, 13, 10 and 12. The only two lead changes came inside Round 1, at the 6th and the 9th, neither outright. His +4 at Boavista in Round 1 (cr01, b05) established the cushion; rounds of +11, +23 and +13 followed. Final: +51, winning by 12 from Jon Baker (b03).

**Wooden Spoon.** Neumann was bottom of the field at the 1st hole of Round 1; Patterson dropped below him at the 4th and stayed bottom for the remaining 68 holes. Round scores of 29, 35, 27 and 21. The 19 shots dropped over holes 11 to 15 in Round 3 (b29) and the 21-point closing round (b40) made it arithmetic. Final: 112 points (b28).

## PLAYERS

**Alex Baker** — Arrived with two runner-up Trophy finishes, three straight last-place Jacket finishes and a prior Wooden Spoon. Improved every round: 41, 45, 47, 51. Took the lead outright with a par at the 6th in Round 3, made 10 at the 13th the same day, and closed with the best round in TEG history. First Trophy, by 11; personal-best gross total of +90.

**David Mullin** — Set a new Boavista course record of 75 on his 11th visit there, seven better than the prior record of 82, and the best gross round in TEG history. Led the Trophy for 30 holes before losing it to a par at the 6th in Round 3. Won the Green Jacket at +51 by 12.

**Gregg Williams** — Personal bests at Palmares in Round 2 (48 points, +14) and a new Boavista best of 93 in Round 4. Led outright from the 13th in Round 3 and drew level at the 5th in Round 4. A 7 at the 6th and an 8 at the 18th in the final round; second on the Trophy by 11, personal-best gross total of +76.

**Jon Baker** — Led the Jacket briefly at the 9th in Round 1. Posted +10 at Palmares, the third-best gross round in TEG history, a Boavista personal best of 86 in Round 4, and a personal-best gross total of +63. Jacket runner-up.

**John Patterson** — Defending Trophy champion. Bottom of the field from the 4th hole of Round 1 to the final green. A 10 at the par-5 8th in Round 1, a 10 at the 1st in Round 2, a 10 at the 8th and 19 shots dropped over holes 11–15 in Round 3, an 11 at the 8th and a 9 at the 18th in Round 4. Closing round of 21 points, his worst to date; 112 points and the Wooden Spoon.

**Stuart Neumann** — Sextuple-bogey 10s at the 18th in Rounds 1 and 2, plus a 10 at the 13th at Palmares in Round 2 within a three-hole stretch costing 13 shots. On his 11th visit to Boavista he matched his previous visit exactly. Avoided the Spoon by out-pointing Patterson.
