# Level at Palmares

## Alex Baker and Gregg Williams both post 47 points on TEG's first visit to Palmares - Alvor / Lagos, arriving at the same number by conspicuously different routes
<!-- storyline: round -->

Alex Baker and Gregg Williams both signed for 47 points on Round 3 at Palmares - Alvor / Lagos, TEG's first visit to the course, but they got there by entirely different paths. Baker's was a personal best, both on the Trophy (Stableford) card and in gross terms at +22, despite a horror show at the 13th, where a quintuple bogey 10 wrecked what had been a tidy run. He built his round in bursts, banking 10 points across holes 2-4 and another nine across holes 9-11, and used the momentum to seize the outright lead at the 6th before Williams briefly took it back at the 13th itself. Baker regained a share of it again at the 15th. Williams, meanwhile, survived his own wobble — four straight bogeys from 13 to 16 — before a birdie at the 17th halted the slide, having earlier strung together nine points of his own across holes 11-13. The identical total sent Baker into the lead of the 2017 TEG Trophy outright, closing a five-point gap on David Mullin entirely.

## John Patterson's round falls apart over holes 11 to 15 and pushes him 14 points clear at the foot of the TEG 10 Wooden Spoon
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

John Patterson's third round at Palmares started promisingly enough, with three straight pars and bogeys either side to open with 9 points from his first three holes. Even a quintuple bogey 10 on the par-5 8th, one of the harder holes on the course, was survivable at that stage.

Then came the stretch that undid him. From the 11th to the 15th, Patterson dropped 19 shots without a single net par, managing just one stableford point across the five holes. A triple bogey on the 11th, one of the toughest holes at Palmares, was followed by a quintuple bogey 8 on the short 12th, a triple bogey on the par-5 13th, and quadruple bogeys on both the 14th and the 15th.

The collapse left him with the worst Trophy score of the day, 27, and pushed him further adrift at the bottom of the TEG 10 Wooden Spoon standings. Having trailed the next-worst player by 5 points before the round, Patterson now sits 14 points clear at the foot of the table.

## David Mullin's 31-point round ends 30 holes of Trophy leadership and drops him 11 behind, even as he retains the TEG 10 Green Jacket lead
<!-- storyline: race -->

David Mullin's hold on the TEG 10 Trophy (Stableford) lead, in place since hole 12 of Round 1, ended after 30 holes when Alex Baker moved in front at the sixth hole of Round 3. Mullin briefly drew level again at the 7th, but his back nine turned choppy: a triple bogey at the 13th and a double bogey at the 14th were followed by a three-hole burst of 11 points, birdies at the 15th and 17th sandwiching a par at the 16th. That recovery unravelled at the last, where a quadruple bogey 8 left him tied with Baker on points, Baker taking the lead. Mullin's round of 31 was down 11 on his second-round total of 42, the sharpest swing of the day - Jon Baker also fell 10 points short of his previous round. Mullin's Trophy lead is gone, but he still leads the TEG 10 Green Jacket, the gap now down to 10 strokes from 13.
