# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 17, Lisbon Coast, Portugal, 2024

## OVERVIEW

Jon Baker won the Trophy on 166 Stableford points, 18 clear of David Mullin — a personal best and the 9th best total in TEG history. Baker also won the Green Jacket at +67 gross, 13 clear of Mullin. Gregg Williams took the Wooden Spoon on 123 points.

Four rounds, four course records: two lows (Mullin's 82 at West Cliffs, Baker's 86 at Royal Óbidos), two highs (Alex Baker's 114 at Praia D'El Rey, John Patterson's 116 at West Cliffs). This was TEG's 6th visit to the Lisbon Coast.

---

## ROUND 1 — Twenty-Four Visits, One New Worst
*Saturday, Praia D'El Rey (5th TEG round at the venue)*

Alex Baker signed for 114 gross. That is the worst gross round in 24 prior TEG visits to Praia D'El Rey, exceeding the previous worst of 110 (cr01). The damage was concentrated: a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 4th (b27), a sextuple-bogey 10 at the par-4 5th, SI 2, one of the hardest holes on the course (b08), and a quintuple-bogey 9 at the par-4 18th (b18) — 15 over par across those three holes alone.

Others contributed to the same stretch. Gregg Williams made a quadruple-bogey 8 at the same par-4 5th (b28). John Patterson opened with a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 1st (b29).

David Mullin scored 33 points, with a 13-point split between his nines in favour of the back (b44). His run from the 12th to the 16th brought 16 points: par at the par-5 12th, birdie at the par-4 13th, par at the par-3 14th, bogey at the par-4 15th, par at the par-4 16th (b13).

Trophy lead changes were routine with the field bunched: Jon Baker went ahead at the 3rd, Patterson took it back at the 16th. Patterson finished the day leading the Trophy by one from Jon Baker, who scored 38. In the gross, Jon Baker led from the 2nd hole; Williams drew level at the 17th.

---

## ROUND 2 — Mullin's 82, Patterson's 116
*Sunday, West Cliffs (2nd TEG round at the venue)*

Both ends of the West Cliffs book fell on the same day. David Mullin shot 82 gross, lowering the course record from 88 across six prior visits, and 12 shots better than his own last visit there (cr03). John Patterson shot 116 gross, the highest the course has recorded, exceeding the prior worst of 115 (cr04) and 19 shots worse than his TEG 15 visit. It was also Patterson's worst gross round to date at +44 (b23). Jon Baker improved on his 2015 West Cliffs mark by 15 shots (cr03).

Jon Baker took the outright Trophy lead at the 1st hole with a bogey at the par-4 opener, SI 17 (b46), and did not give it back for the remaining 71 holes. He then made 14 points in four holes: par at the par-3 2nd, par at the par-4 3rd (SI 1, the hardest hole on the course), birdie at the par-4 4th, par at the par-3 5th (b14). He added nine more from the 14th to the 16th — bogey at the par-4 14th (SI 2), par at the par-5 15th, par at the par-3 16th (b47) — for a round of 44.

Patterson's round turned on eight holes from the 8th to the 15th, in which he dropped 27 shots: double bogey at the par-4 8th, triple bogeys at the par-4 9th, 10th and 11th, double bogey at the par-3 12th, a quintuple-bogey 10 at the par-5 13th (b19), a triple bogey at the par-4 14th (SI 2), and a sextuple-bogey 11 at the par-5 15th, SI 16, one of the easiest holes on the course (b06, b09). The 11 was his personal worst relative to par. He had already made a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 3rd, SI 1 (b33).

Mullin's own good stretch came from the 7th to the 10th: bogey, par, birdie, birdie for 14 points (b17). He then bogeyed the 11th and 12th and double-bogeyed the par-5 13th before a bogey at the 14th and a birdie at the par-5 15th ended the run (b50).

Alex Baker set a personal best at West Cliffs, helped by 16 points from the 4th to the 8th — bogey, par, bogey, bogey, bogey (b16) — though he took quadruple-bogey 8s at the par-4 10th and the par-4 11th (b31, b32).

After R2 Jon Baker led the Trophy by two. Mullin drew level with Baker in the gross at the 18th.

---

## ROUND 3 — Lost at the 1st, Won by the 16th
*Monday, Royal Óbidos (4th TEG round at the venue)*

Jon Baker had led the Green Jacket since the 2nd hole of Round 1 — 35 holes in front. He double-bogeyed the par-4 1st and lost the lead to David Mullin (b01). Baker drew level again at the 2nd. Mullin went outright ahead at the par-3 6th with a par (b07). Baker drew level again at the 7th and stayed in front from there.

Baker then made 20 points in six holes: birdie at the par-5 11th, bogey at the par-4 12th (SI 2), birdie at the par-3 13th (SI 18, the easiest hole on the course), par at the par-4 14th, par at the par-3 15th, par at the par-4 16th (b05). He signed for 86 gross — a new Royal Óbidos course record, beating the prior mark of 87 set across 18 prior visits, his own personal best there (previous 89, TEG 9) and eight shots better than his last visit (cr02). His round was 44 points. He led the Trophy by 11 and the Jacket by eight.

Gregg Williams double-bogeyed seven consecutive holes from the 12th to the 18th — the par-4 12th (SI 2), par-3 13th (SI 18), par-4 14th, par-3 15th, par-4 16th, par-4 17th and par-5 18th — dropping 14 shots (b49). The double at the 14th dropped him to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race for the first time (b25). He also made a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 5th (b37). His round was 26 points.

Alex Baker had four separate blow-ups of 8 or more: a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 4th (SI 1, the hardest hole), a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 5th, a sextuple-bogey 10 at the par-4 14th, and a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 18th (b34, b35, b10, b36). From the 12th to the 16th he dropped 15 shots: double, double, the 10 at the 14th, double, triple (b43).

John Patterson made a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 11th (b38) but took 13 points from the 12th to the 15th — par at the par-4 12th (SI 2), par at the par-3 13th, bogey at the par-4 14th, par at the par-3 15th (b26) — and set a personal best of 94 at Royal Óbidos.

---

## ROUND 4 — Nine, Nine, and Still Eighteen Clear
*Tuesday, Praia D'El Rey (6th TEG round at the venue)*

Jon Baker began with a double bogey at the par-4 1st, bogeys at the par-5 2nd and par-3 3rd, then a birdie at the par-4 4th (b45). He made a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 10th (b21) and another quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 17th, SI 1, the hardest hole on the course (b22). In between he took nine points from the 14th to the 16th: par at the par-3 14th, bogey at the par-4 15th, par at the par-4 16th (b24). He scored 40 points and +19 gross, and finished the round leading both the Trophy and the Jacket (b48).

Alex Baker dropped 25 shots in nine holes from the 5th to the 13th: double bogey at the par-4 5th (SI 2), double at the par-4 6th, triple at the par-5 7th, triple at the par-3 8th, double at the par-4 9th, double at the par-5 10th, triple at the par-3 11th, a quadruple-bogey 9 at the par-5 12th and a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 13th (b04, b39, b40).

Gregg Williams opened with a quintuple-bogey 9 at the par-4 1st (b20) and scored 29 points, confirming the Wooden Spoon on 123 (b15).

John Patterson made a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 1st (b41), a sextuple-bogey 9 at the par-3 14th, SI 17, one of the easiest holes (b11), a sextuple-bogey 10 at the par-4 15th, SI 3 (b12), and a quadruple-bogey 8 at the par-4 18th (b42). The 9 at the 14th was his personal worst relative to par.

Final: Jon Baker 166 points, Trophy by 18 (b02); +67 gross, Green Jacket by 13 (b03).

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Leader by round: Patterson after R1 (by 1), Jon Baker after R2 (by 2), R3 (by 11) and R4 (by 18). Baker opened with 38 and sat one behind Patterson. There were three lead changes, two of them routine early exchanges in R1 (Baker at the 3rd, Patterson at the 16th). The decisive one came at the 1st hole of R2 at West Cliffs, where Baker took the outright lead with a bogey (b46) and held it for the remaining 71 holes. Back-to-back 44s at West Cliffs and Royal Óbidos turned a 2-point lead into 11; a 40 in R4 made it 18. Final: 166 points, runner-up David Mullin.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Leader by round: Williams level with Baker after R1, Mullin level after R2, Jon Baker after R3 (by 8) and R4 (by 13). Baker led from the 2nd hole of R1. Williams drew level at the 17th of R1; Mullin drew level at the 18th of R2. Baker lost the lead outright for the first time at the R3 1st at Royal Óbidos after 35 holes in front, double-bogeying the par-4 opener (b01). Mullin was outright ahead by the par-3 6th (b07). Baker drew level again at the 7th and led from there, signing the course-record 86 (cr02) for an 8-shot cushion after R3, extended to 13 despite two quadruple-bogey 9s in R4. Final: +67, runner-up David Mullin.

**Wooden Spoon.** Bottom by round: Alex Baker after R1, Patterson after R2, Williams after R3 and R4. Williams was third after R1 on 33 points, slipped to fourth after R2 on 35, and dropped to last at the 14th hole of R3 at Royal Óbidos — a double bogey mid-way through seven consecutive doubles from the 12th (b25, b49) — for a round of 26. He never climbed out. A 29 in R4, opened with a quintuple-bogey 9 at the par-4 1st (b20), left him on 123 points. It is his second career Spoon, and his first since winning the TEG 15 Trophy.

## PLAYERS

- **Jon Baker** — Won both. Personal-best 166 points (9th best in TEG history), the Jacket at +67, a new Royal Óbidos course record of 86, and a 15-shot improvement on his last West Cliffs visit. Had finished 4th on the Trophy in each of TEGs 14, 15 and 16.
- **David Mullin** — Runner-up in both competitions. Nine prior Jackets. Set the West Cliffs course record with an 82, a 12-shot personal best there, and held the outright gross lead for one hole at Royal Óbidos.
- **Gregg Williams** — Reigning Jacket holder and TEG 15 Trophy winner. Third after R1, slid to the Wooden Spoon on 123 points via seven consecutive doubles at Royal Óbidos and a 9 to open R4.
- **John Patterson** — Led the Trophy after R1 and set a personal best of 94 at Royal Óbidos, but posted a worst-ever +44 at West Cliffs, dropping 27 shots in eight holes for a 116, the highest gross the course has recorded.
- **Alex Baker** — Back-to-back Spoon holder who avoided a third only because Williams fell further. New Praia D'El Rey course-worst of 114 in R1, four blow-ups of 8 or more at Royal Óbidos, 25 shots dropped in nine holes in R4, and a personal best at West Cliffs.
