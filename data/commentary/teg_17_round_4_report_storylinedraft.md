# Baker's Double Crowns TEG 17

## David Mullin's tidy final round wasn't enough to stop Jon Baker turning healthy leads into a seventh TEG double, while Gregg Williams's closing 29 points confirmed the Wooden Spoon.
<!-- storyline: race -->

Jon Baker closed out the final round at Praia D'El Rey exactly as he had led it, taking both the TEG Trophy and the Green Jacket to complete a double - his seventh in TEG history, following those in TEGs 2, 4, 11, 13, 14 and 15. Neither lead ever changed hands in round four: Baker's cushion actually grew, from 11 to 18 shots in the Trophy race and from 8 to 13 in the Jacket. David Mullin's tidy closing round gave the field something to chase, but it never threatened the gap.

At the other end, Gregg Williams's TEG was effectively sealed before it began, and his final round did nothing to change that. A quintuple-bogey 9 at the 1st set the tone, and though he rallied to close with 29 points, the damage to the Wooden Spoon race had long been done - his gap to safety stretched from 8 to 12 shots by day's end, confirming the Spoon as his.

## Alex Baker and John Patterson both endured chaotic closing rounds thick with blow-up holes, even as the destiny at the top was already settled.
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

By the time the field reached Praia D'El Rey for round 4, the destination of both the 2024 TEG Trophy and Green Jacket had already been settled, with the overall picture unchanged from the day before. But the round itself produced two of the most turbulent scorecards of the week, from Alex Baker and John Patterson, both playing out rounds with nothing left to affect.

Alex's card fell apart over a nine-hole stretch from the 5th to the 13th, bleeding 25 shots through a run of doubles, triples and quadruple bogeys at the 12th and 13th back to back. Within that stretch he went three holes, from the 11th to the 13th, without registering a single net par. Earlier in the round he had gone the other way entirely, picking up 9 stableford points across three holes from the 2nd to the 4th.

Patterson's damage came in isolated blows: a sextuple bogey 10 at the 15th, one of the hardest holes on the course, followed by a sextuple bogey 9 at the 14th, his worst hole of the tournament relative to par. Quadruple bogeys at the 1st and 18th bookended a chaotic day.

## Jon Baker's final round paired two calamitous quadruple bogeys with the best Stableford and gross scores of the day.
<!-- storyline: round -->

Jon Baker completed the double at Praia D'El Rey, taking both the 2024 TEG Trophy and the 2024 TEG Green Jacket — the seventh time in TEG history that one player has landed both titles in the same edition. He did it despite two quadruple bogeys, a 9 at the 10th and another at the 17th, the latter on the hardest hole on the course. Yet the same round produced the best Stableford score and the best gross score of the day, in a field that ranged from 40 points down to 29, and from 19 over to 38 over. Baker's day was a round of extremes: a double bogey and two early bogeys were answered by back-to-back birdies at the 4th and 5th, and after the disaster at the 10th he found nine points across the 14th to 16th. His lead grew rather than shrank, with the Trophy gap stretching to 18 and the Jacket gap to 13.
