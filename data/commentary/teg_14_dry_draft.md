# DRY STORYLINE DRAFT — TEG 14, Kent, England (November 2021)

## OVERVIEW

David Mullin won the Trophy with 154 Stableford points, two clear of Alex Baker. He also won the Green Jacket on a Gross total of +68, seven clear of Jon Baker. Jon Baker finished last on the Trophy metric with 125 points and took the Wooden Spoon.

---

## Deal, and the Reigning Spoon Holder Deals Himself In

Round 1, Thursday 4 November, Royal Cinque Ports at Deal — the second TEG round at the venue.

Gregg Williams made a quadruple bogey 9 at the par-5 3rd. Alex Baker drew level at the top of the Gross board with a par at the 6th. Jon Baker bogeyed the par-3 4th, stroke index 18, the easiest hole on the course, and dropped to the bottom of the Trophy board.

Mullin drew level for the Gross lead with a par at the 9th, then took 13 points from holes 9 to 12: par at the 9th, birdie at the 10th, pars at the 11th and 12th. The par at the 11th drew him level on the Trophy board. His run of par-or-better ended with a double bogey at the par-4 13th. He then took the outright Trophy lead with a par at the par-5 16th.

Alex Baker made a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 11th, stroke index 3, and a 10 — quintuple bogey, 0 points — at the par-5 16th.

Mullin signed for 40 points and 87 gross (+15), a personal best at Royal Cinque Ports by seven shots. Alex Baker's 98 was also a course personal best, four better than TEG 13. Jon Baker scored 29 points and was seven shots worse than his last visit here. Mullin led the Trophy by 3 and the Jacket by 5.

---

## Littlestone, Big Lead: Mullin Doubles Down on the Marsh

Round 2, Friday 5 November, Littlestone Championship Links on the Romney Marshes — the second TEG round at the venue.

Alex Baker dropped 12 shots across holes 2 to 5: triple bogey 7 at the 2nd, double bogey 6 at the 3rd, triple bogey 7 at the par-4 4th, quadruple bogey 9 at the par-5 5th. He added a second quadruple bogey, an 8 at the par-4 8th.

Mullin also lost ground in that stretch — triple bogey at the par-4 4th (stroke index 2), double bogey at the 5th, bogey at the par-3 6th — before a birdie at the par-5 7th ended the run. He closed the same way: bogey at the 15th, bogey at the 16th, double bogey at the par-3 17th, then a birdie at the par-5 18th.

Mullin posted 41 points and 85 gross (+14), a second course personal best in two days, again by seven shots. The Trophy lead went to 9 and the Jacket lead to 12. Jon Baker scored 29 points, thirteen shots worse than his 79 here in TEG 13. Williams scored 27 points and was eleven shots worse than his last visit to Littlestone.

---

## Nine at the Eighth, and Still Eight Clear

Round 3, Saturday 6 November, Prince's Shore/Dunes at Sandwich — the second TEG round at the venue.

Mullin made a quadruple bogey 9 at the par-5 8th, worth 0 points. It was his worst round of the tournament: 36 points and +20 gross, with a back nine ten points better than his front nine. He finished with 13 points across holes 15 to 18 — pars at the 15th, 16th and 17th, birdie at the par-4 18th.

Jon Baker made a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 13th, stroke index 1, the hardest hole on the course. He then played the 14th, 15th and 16th in par before a triple bogey 6 at the par-3 17th. He scored 28 points.

Alex Baker made a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 10th, stroke index 3.

Williams parred the 9th, 10th and 11th before a triple bogey 8 at the par-5 12th. He scored 39 points, up twelve on his round 2 total of 27, for 86 gross — a personal best at Prince's.

Mullin's Trophy lead was 8 after the round; his Jacket lead remained 12.

---

## Ten at the Sixteenth, Two Points Short

Round 4, Sunday 7 November, Prince's Shore/Dunes — the third TEG round at the venue.

Alex Baker opened with 10 points in three holes: par at the par-4 1st, par at the par-5 2nd, par at the par-3 3rd. Mullin made a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 10th, stroke index 3, for 0 points. Alex Baker then took 11 points from holes 13 to 15: par at the 13th (stroke index 1, the hardest hole), par at the 14th, par at the par-5 15th. At the par-4 16th, stroke index 17, the second-easiest hole on the course, he made a 10 — sextuple bogey, 0 points.

Jon Baker played the 11th in par, then birdied the par-5 12th and the par-4 13th and parred the 14th and 15th: 14 points across holes 12 to 15. He then double-bogeyed the 16th for 0 points and made a quadruple bogey 8 at the par-4 18th. He signed for 39 points, eleven better than his round 3 total, and 83 gross — a new Prince's Shore/Dunes course record, beating the prior record of 85 set across five previous visits.

Mullin scored 37 points and +19 for 91 gross at Prince's, one better than his previous best there. He finished on 154 points and +68.

---

## HOW THE COMPETITIONS WERE DECIDED

**Trophy (Stableford).** Mullin led after every round: by 3 after round 1, 9 after round 2, 8 after round 3, and 2 at the finish. There were two lead changes, both in round 1 — he drew level at the 11th at Royal Cinque Ports and went outright ahead at the 16th — and none thereafter across the remaining 56 holes. His round scores were 40, 41, 36 and 37. The decisive moment came at the par-4 16th at Prince's in round 4, where Alex Baker's 10 returned 0 points on stroke index 17. Final margin: 2 points, 154 to 152.

**Green Jacket (Gross).** Two lead changes, both in round 1 and both level rather than outright: Alex Baker at the 6th, Mullin at the 9th. Mullin led by 5 after round 1, 12 after round 2 and 12 after round 3. His round Gross figures were +15, +14, +20 and +19. Final margin: 7 shots over Jon Baker, +68 to +75. Gregg Williams's +76 was his own personal-best Gross total.

**Wooden Spoon.** Jon Baker dropped to the bottom of the Trophy board at the 4th hole of round 1 — a bogey at the par-3 4th at Royal Cinque Ports, stroke index 18 — and was bottom after every round thereafter. There was one bottom-place change in the whole tournament. His round scores were 29, 29, 28 and 39, for 125 points.

---

## PLAYERS

**David Mullin** — Won the Trophy on 154 points (third career Trophy) and the Green Jacket at +68 (ninth). Led both boards from round 1. Set personal bests at all three Kent courses: 87 at Royal Cinque Ports, 85 at Littlestone, 91 at Prince's. Made a quadruple bogey at the 8th in round 3 and another at the 10th in round 4.

**Alex Baker** — Second on the Trophy, two points back. Course personal best of 98 at Royal Cinque Ports in round 1, which included a 10 at the 16th. Dropped 12 shots in four holes at Littlestone; quadruple bogeys at the 11th (R1), 5th and 8th (R2) and 10th (R3). Scored 21 points across two stretches in round 4, then made 10 at the 16th.

**Jon Baker** — Defending Trophy champion. Bottom of the Trophy board from the 4th hole of round 1 onwards and last after every round. Finished on 125 points for the Wooden Spoon, and second in the Jacket at +75. Set the Prince's Shore/Dunes course record with 83 gross in round 4.

**Gregg Williams** — Posted a personal-best Gross total of +76, including a Prince's personal best of 86 in round 3 and a twelve-point improvement on his round 2 Stableford. Did not contend for either prize.
