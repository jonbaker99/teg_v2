# Penha Longa Draws First Blood

## Gregg Williams's round of the day: a stellar 41 points bookended by two separate horror stretches
<!-- storyline: round -->

Gregg Williams produced the round of the day at Penha Longa in TEG 2's second round, posting 41 Stableford points despite two separate collapses either side of a steady middle stretch. The damage came in two chunks: a nine-shot bleed across holes 11 to 13, including a double bogey at the 11th, a quintuple bogey 10 at the par-5 12th, and another double at the 13th; then a second slide from 15 to 17, three more dropped shots capped by a triple bogey at the 16th, the hardest hole on the course. Sandwiched between the carnage was a brief run of calm, back-to-back pars at the 6th and 7th. None of it stopped Williams posting the best score of the day in a six-man field, and it extended his lead atop the TEG Trophy standings from four points to eleven. David Mullin remained clear at the top of the Green Jacket race after the round.

## Penha Longa's par-4 16th, stroke index one, claims three separate blow-ups in the same round
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

The 16th at Penha Longa, the toughest hole on the Atlantic Course and stroke index one, took its toll on three players in the same round of TEG 2.

Henry Meller made a quintuple bogey nine there, and Stuart Neumann matched him hole for hole with a nine of his own. Alex Baker escaped slightly more lightly with a quadruple bogey eight, but all three carded blanks on their Stableford returns for the hole.

Neumann's day was the most costly. He arrived at Penha Longa already anchored to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon standings, and while the gap to the next-worst player did narrow from 12 shots to 8 by round's end, a nine at the hardest hole on the course did nothing to help his cause. Meller and Baker had rather more room to absorb the damage, but for one round, the 16th proved unforgiving to anyone who came through it.

## Stuart Neumann's recovery narrows the Wooden Spoon gap, with Henry Meller's rough finish closing fast
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Stuart Neumann arrived at Penha Longa firmly last in the race for the TEG 2015 Wooden Spoon, and a wretched opening round of 17 Stableford points had only tightened his grip on the booby prize, with the gap to safety out to 12. Round two told a different story. Neumann jumped to 31 points, a swing of 14 on his first-round total, to post his best score of the week on the course. It was not enough to shift him off the bottom of the Spoon standings, but it did cut the gap to 8.

Henry Meller made sure the gap did not close further still. Having played his way to a share of the best round of the day on this course through the front nine, he then lost the plot for four holes in the middle of the back nine, taking a double bogey, two triple bogeys and another double across holes 11 to 14 — ten shots dropped, with only two Stableford points salvaged from the stretch. It kept Neumann's rescue act from becoming a genuine escape.

## David Mullin stretches his Green Jacket lead as Alex Baker's back-nine implosion drags him to the rear of the pack
<!-- storyline: race -->

David Mullin extended his TEG 16 Green Jacket lead at Penha Longa on Thursday, though the gap in the Trophy race behind Gregg Williams stretched to 11 as scoring proved brutal for most of the field. Mullin was steady rather than spectacular, going two holes without dropping a shot around the turn with a birdie on the par-5 6th and a par on the 7th, and finished with a round of 86, his best gross score at the course through the event.

Alex Baker's day collapsed at the other end of the card. A run of four straight double bogeys from the 11th through the 14th bled eight shots, and worse followed at the finish: a double bogey on the 17th sandwiched between quadruple bogeys on the 16th and 18th cost him ten shots in the space of three holes. The 18th alone, a par-5, produced a 9. Baker's Green Jacket gap to Mullin now stands at six, with his Wooden Spoon prospects looking increasingly real.
