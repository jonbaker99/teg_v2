# Gregg Williams Rises as Jon Baker's Charge Crumbles

## Gregg Williams's recovery from a rocky start into five untouched holes produced the best gross round of the day at Penha Longa
<!-- storyline: round -->

Gregg Williams opened with contented pars at the 1st and 2nd before his card blew up at the 3rd and 4th, a triple bogey followed by a double bogey on the 4th, one of Penha Longa's hardest holes. A birdie at the par-3 5th halted the slide, but the recovery stalled again from the 6th to the 8th, three holes without a net par that included another triple bogey at the 7th and cost him four points against par. From there he settled completely, playing the 11th through the 15th without dropping a single gross shot, and picking up nine stableford points across the 13th, 14th and 15th alone. That closing stretch produced the best gross round of the day, in his third visit to Penha Longa, though it finished five shots shy of the 82 he carded there in TEG 15. The round kept him top of the TEG 16 Green Jacket standings, his lead swelling from 4 strokes to 14.

## Alex Baker suffered two separate multi-hole collapses in the same round, on the front nine and the back nine, and stayed rooted to the bottom of the TEG 16 Wooden Spoon
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Round 3 at Penha Longa brought no relief for Alex Baker, who opened the day already anchored to the bottom of the TEG 16 Wooden Spoon and proceeded to suffer two separate collapses in the same round. The first came early: from the 2nd to the 6th he shed 11 shots, including a triple bogey at the 4th, one of the hardest holes on the course, and four holes running without a net par.

The back nine was worse. Between the 11th and 14th he bled 12 shots, headlined by a 9 at the 12th — a quadruple bogey on one of the easiest holes on the course, and the low point of another three-hole stretch without a net par.

Baker finished the round still rooted to the bottom of the Spoon standings, the gap to the next-worst player unchanged at three points, both collapses cancelling out any chance of closing the distance.

## Jon Baker's three-hole implosion on the back nine handed Gregg Williams total control of the TEG 16 Green Jacket race
<!-- storyline: race -->

On the back nine at Penha Longa in the TEG 16 Green Jacket race, Jon Baker's round collapsed in three holes. At the 11th he took a double bogey, then followed it with a quintuple bogey 10 at the par-5 12th — a hole ranked as the easiest on the course by stroke index. The 13th brought a quadruple bogey 8. In all, Baker bled 11 shots over those three holes and went the entire stretch without recording a single net par.

The timing could hardly have hurt more. Gregg Williams had started the day leading the Jacket race by just 4 shots; by the end of the round that lead had stretched to 14, with Williams still out front and Baker's collapse doing much of the damage. It was also a painful return to a course where Baker had previously gone well — his gross score here came in 12 strokes worse than on his last visit to Penha Longa.
