# No Bargains at Deal

## Jon Baker's 39-point opening round at Royal Cinque Ports, built on a flawless first five holes and closed out with three straight pars, six clear of the field
<!-- storyline: round -->

Jon Baker opened the 2020 TEG at Royal Cinque Ports Golf Club with a 39-point round, the best score of the day, with the field's total spread running from 28 up to that 39. It was built on a clean start: pars at the first, second and fourth, then a birdie at the par-5 3rd, one of the harder holes on the card, before a par at the 5th rounded out five holes without a dropped shot. That opening burst alone was worth 10 stableford points across its first three holes. Baker closed with three more pars from the 16th through the 18th to bring the round home without further alarm. It was enough to put him on top of both the TEG 2020 Trophy and the TEG 2020 Jacket after the first round, on his first visit to the course, with a gross score of 85 setting the mark he would need to chase across the rest of the week.

## Alex Baker loses eighteen gross shots across two three- and four-hole stretches, the 4th to the 7th and the 10th to the 12th, on the way to 102
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Alex Baker's opening round at Royal Cinque Ports never got going. After a double bogey at the 4th, he dropped shots in bunches across the next three holes, running triple bogey-double bogey-double bogey through the 5th, 6th and 7th to lose nine shots in that four-hole stretch alone. The par-5 5th, the second-hardest hole on the course, cost him a triple bogey in the middle of the slide.

The back nine brought no relief. A double bogey at the 10th was followed by a quadruple bogey at the 11th, one of the harder holes on the course, and a triple bogey at the 12th — another nine shots gone in three holes, and three straight holes without a net par. That second collapse also dropped him to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race, from third to fifth.

Between the two stretches, Baker lost eighteen gross shots in just seven holes, finishing round one on 102.

## Gregg Williams and David Mullin both post 94 gross and both stop the rot with a single birdie, Mullin at the 5th and Williams at the 1st-stroke-index 15th
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Round one at Royal Cinque Ports Golf Club was a grind for both Gregg Williams and David Mullin, who each carded 94 gross, but each found exactly one hole to briefly halt the damage.

Mullin's round began in trouble: a triple bogey at the 1st, then bogeys at the 2nd and 3rd, and a double bogey at the 4th. That run of four straight holes without a par ended at the par-5 5th, one of the harder holes on the course by stroke index, where he made birdie.

Williams had his own version of the same story, only worse. After a double bogey at the 13th and a bogey at the 14th, he birdied the 15th — Royal Cinque Ports' hardest hole by stroke index. It was scant relief: earlier in the round he had already dropped six shots across three holes, from the 5th to the 7th, three double bogeys in a row without a single net par among them. For both men, one birdie apiece was the only thing separating a bad round from something worse.

## John Patterson's 28 points and 36-over gross round leave him three adrift at the foot of the TEG 13 Wooden Spoon race, while bottom place changed hands repeatedly before settling on him
<!-- storyline: race -->

John Patterson had the worst round of the field at Royal Cinque Ports in the opening round of TEG 13, carding 28 points on 36-over gross - the lowest Trophy score and highest gross of the day. The damage came in bursts: quadruple bogeys at the 1st and 10th, then a quintuple bogey nine at the 7th, before a run from the 9th to the 11th bled eight shots in three holes. A second quadruple bogey at the 16th, a par-5 with stroke index 7, only added to the total.

The bottom of the TEG 13 Wooden Spoon race changed hands four times over the round. Gregg Williams dropped there twice, at the 3rd and again at the 5th, and David Mullin took the spot after a double bogey at the 4th. But it was Patterson's double bogey at the 2nd, followed by the quadruple at the 16th, that left him anchored last. He finished the round three points adrift at the foot of the field, with Jon Baker leading both the Trophy and the TEG 13 Green Jacket after the opening 18.
