# Mullin Rewrites the Boavista Record Book

## David Mullin's record-breaking opening round at Boavista
<!-- storyline: round -->

On Saturday 30 September 2017, David Mullin shot a gross 75 at Boavista Golf & Spa Resort — four over par — the best gross round in TEG history to date. His 49 Stableford points made it the second-best round in TEG history to date, and the score also broke the Boavista course record, beating the previous mark of 82 set across 50 prior visits to the course. It was personal transformation as much as record-breaking: 23 shots better than his last visit there, and comfortably his best gross score at the venue this TEG.

Mullin built the round in bursts. He picked up 11 points across holes 12-14, birdieing the par-5 13th and the par-3 14th, then added 10 more from 16-18 with a birdie on the par-5 16th. An earlier run through holes 6-8, capped by a par on the brutal par-5 8th (stroke index 2), brought nine points. Across all three stretches he did not drop a single gross shot.

## John Patterson's introduction to Boavista, and to the TEG 10 Wooden Spoon race
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

John Patterson's first look at Boavista, on the opening day of TEG 10, did not go well. Playing his maiden round on the Howard Swan parkland course, he ran up a quintuple bogey 10 at the 8th, the hardest hole on the course, and never recovered a foothold. Two more blow-ups followed: a quadruple bogey 7 at the short 6th and a quadruple bogey 8 at the 18th.

The closing stretch was the real damage. From the 15th to the 18th he bled 11 shots, and from the 16th home he went three straight holes without a single net par. It added up to a round of 102, and by the 4th hole he had already slipped to the bottom of the TEG 10 Wooden Spoon race, a position that round one did nothing to shake loose.

It was not the introduction to Boavista, or to the Spoon race, that Patterson would have wanted.

## Stuart Neumann's collapse at the course where he once shared the record he watched fall
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Stuart Neumann returned to Boavista Golf & Spa Resort for the eleventh time in his TEG career on Saturday, arriving with a course best gross of 82, set back in TEG 5. That history counted for nothing in round one of TEG 16. Neumann carded a quadruple bogey 9 at the par-5 3rd, then fell apart entirely down the stretch: a quadruple bogey at the 15th, one of the course's hardest holes, a double bogey at the 16th, another quadruple at the 17th, and a sextuple bogey 10 at the 18th, his worst score against par ever recorded at Boavista. Holes 15 through 18 alone cost him 16 shots. The finished gross of 107 left him nine shots worse off than his previous visit and 25 shots adrift of the 82 he once held as his best round on the course — a personal record now buried under a Saturday collapse he will want to forget.

## How the early running for the TEG 10 Trophy and Green Jacket was contested, then settled
<!-- storyline: race -->

Round one of the TEG 10 Trophy and Green Jacket, played at Boavista Golf & Spa Resort, saw the lead change hands repeatedly before settling. At the par-3 6th, David Mullin drew level for both the Trophy and the Jacket, only for Alex Baker to bogey the same hole and join the tie at the top of the Trophy standings. Jon Baker then went ahead outright for the Trophy at the 9th, and level for the Jacket, before Mullin hit back at the 10th to share the Trophy lead again. Alex Baker rejoined the tie at the 11th. The round was finally settled late: Mullin birdied the par-5 16th and played the last three holes without dropping a gross shot, taking 10 Stableford points from that stretch. It carried him to the top of both leaderboards after round one, level at the head of the Trophy and outright ahead in the Jacket — a strong start built on a career-best score at Boavista, 23 shots better than his last visit there.
