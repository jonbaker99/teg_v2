# The Record Falls at Boavista

## David Mullin's 75 gross at Boavista — a new course record, the best gross round in TEG history to date and 49 Stableford points
<!-- storyline: round -->

David Mullin produced the best round of golf in TEG history at Boavista Golf & Spa Resort in Lagos on Saturday, shooting 75 gross to smash the course record of 82, a mark that had stood across 50 prior visits to the course. It was also a personal best there by seven shots, and 23 shots better than his previous visit. The round yielded 49 Stableford points, the second-best score recorded in TEG history to date, in the opening round of the 2017 TEG Trophy and Green Jacket, and the best score by 20 points in a field of six.

Mullin's card showed few flaws. He parred his way through holes 6 to 8, including the second-hardest hole on the course, before catching fire from 12 to 14, birdieing the par-5 13th and the par-3 14th for 11 points in three holes. He closed with another purple patch from 16 to 18, a birdie at the par-5 16th sandwiched by two pars for 10 more points, finishing four over gross for the round.

## Boavista's closing four holes, and the 18th in particular, dismantling most of the field
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

The closing stretch at Boavista did most of the damage in round one, with the 18th - one of the tougher finishing holes at SI 5 - proving especially unforgiving.

Stuart Neumann bore the brunt of it. Already toiling, he ran up a quadruple bogey 8 at the 15th, another quadruple at the 17th, then closed with a sextuple bogey 10 at the 18th - a sequence that saw him bleed 16 shots over his last four holes and post the worst score of his day at the very end of it. It left him well adrift of his best-ever total on the course.

He was not alone. John Patterson, playing Boavista for the first time, dropped 11 shots over the same closing four holes and went the last three without a single net par. Gregg Williams added a quintuple bogey 9 at the 18th of his own.

Amid the wreckage, David Mullin found the opposite: birdie, par, par down the last three for 10 points, a finish that helped him to a personal-best round on the course.

## The chase after round one of TEG 10 — Jon Baker's brief lead, Alex Baker's 41 points, and John Patterson's immediate residency at the foot of the Wooden Spoon
<!-- storyline: race -->

Round one of the 2017 TEG 10 Trophy (Stableford) at Boavista Golf & Spa Resort produced a fluid battle for both the Trophy and the Green Jacket (Gross) leads. Jon Baker moved to the outright Trophy lead with a par at the 9th, and the same hole brought him level at the top of the Jacket standings. David Mullin then drew level for the Trophy lead at the 10th, and Alex Baker joined the tie at the 11th, having already shared it once before at the 6th. Alex Baker's round was as messy as it was competitive: a quadruple bogey at the 2nd and another at the 8th were offset by a run of three bogeys across holes 4 to 6 that still yielded nine Stableford points. By the close, David Mullin had emerged with a share of both the Trophy and the Jacket lead. At the other end, John Patterson dropped to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon table after a bogey at the 4th, then a quadruple bogey at the 6th and a quintuple bogey at the 8th left him anchored there after just one round.
