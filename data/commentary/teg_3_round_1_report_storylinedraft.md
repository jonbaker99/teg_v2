# Baker and Mullin Rewrite TEG History at Boavista

## Jon Baker's net -9 stands as the best round recorded in TEG history to date
<!-- storyline: round -->

On Tuesday 5 October 2010, at Boavista Golf & Spa Resort in Lagos, Algarve, Jon Baker produced a round that stands as the best in TEG history: a net -9 in the first round of the 2010 TEG Trophy. His gross score of 19 over par also ranks as the third-best gross round recorded in TEG history to date.

The round was built on sustained accuracy rather than one explosive run. Between the 7th and 9th, Baker gained three shots to par, a stretch that included a bogey on the par-5 8th, one of the hardest holes on the course. Later, over the 13th and 14th, he went two holes without dropping a single gross shot, closing out with back-to-back pars.

The round also marked a personal course best for Baker at Boavista. His gross score of 90 improved on the 100 he had carded there previously, a swing of 17 strokes from his last visit to the course.

## Gregg Williams's personal-best net round is undone by a catastrophic finish
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Gregg Williams's opening round at Boavista Golf & Spa Resort in the 2010 TEG had already come under pressure by the turn. Between the 8th and the 10th he dropped seven shots in three holes: a double bogey on the par-5 8th, another double on the 9th — the easiest hole on the course — and a triple bogey on the 10th. A quadruple-bogey 7 followed at the short 6th. But the round unravelled completely at the finish. From the 15th, the hardest hole on the course, through to the 18th, Williams ran off four bogeys of escalating severity — triple, triple, triple and a closing quadruple — bleeding 13 shots in four holes and going the entire stretch without a single net par. What had been a promising round through the middle holes was buried by that finish, leaving him short of his own best score on the course and with little to show for the work done earlier in the day.

## David Mullin's course-record round grabs the Green Jacket lead as Henry Meller sinks to the bottom of the Wooden Spoon race
<!-- storyline: race -->

Round one at Boavista Golf & Spa Resort, on the group's fourth visit to the course, produced a new course record. David Mullin carded a gross 88, beating the previous mark of 89 and also improving on his own prior best at the course. His round of +17 gross stands as the best gross round in TEG history to date, and his net score of -1 ranks as the third-best round in TEG history to date. It put him in front for the 2010 TEG Green Jacket after the first round, while Jon Baker took the lead in the 2010 TEG Trophy, level with the field on net score.

At the other end, Henry Meller unravelled. A run of bogeys from the 9th through the 13th was followed by a double bogey at the 14th, before a birdie at the 16th briefly halted the slide. It didn't last: a 12 at the par-4 17th, one of the easiest holes on the course, was his and the tournament's worst hole relative to par, and it dropped him to the bottom of the 2010 TEG Wooden Spoon race.
