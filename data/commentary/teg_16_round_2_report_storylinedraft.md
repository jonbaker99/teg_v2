# Troia Bares Its Teeth

## Stuart Neumann's rollercoaster 35 points — the best round of the day, salvaged from two separate collapses
<!-- storyline: round -->

Stuart Neumann's round 2 at Troia opened as a disaster. Through nine holes he failed to make anything better than bogey, dropping shots in bunches — double bogeys at the 1st, 3rd, 6th and 9th, a triple at the 4th and another at the 8th — before finally halting the slide with a birdie at the 10th.

That birdie sparked the best spell of his day. Neumann reeled off 11 points in three holes, adding a par at the 11th and another at the 12th to complete a run in which he did not drop a single gross shot. The recovery did not last: a triple bogey at the 13th ended the steady stretch as suddenly as it had begun, and further down the course he added a quadruple bogey at the 16th.

Even with two separate collapses either side of his hot spell, Neumann still posted 35 points, the best score of the day in the field of five. It kept him on top of the 2023 TEG Trophy standings, though his lead over the field widened only from 5 to 7.

## Gregg Williams's horror round nearly hands Jon Baker the Green Jacket lead
<!-- storyline: d0 -->

Gregg Williams took a three-stroke lead into the second round of the 2023 TEG Green Jacket, played at Troia Golf Championship Course, and proceeded to fall apart. From the 8th, he went four holes without a net par: a bogey on the 8th followed by double bogeys on the 9th, 10th and 11th, the last of those on the easiest hole on the course. After a brief respite, the collapse resumed at the 13th, where three more consecutive double bogeys bled six gross shots across holes 13 to 15, including the par-5 14th and the second-hardest hole on the course.

Jon Baker made him pay in real time, drawing level for the Green Jacket gross lead with a bogey at the 11th and again with a par at the 14th, right in the teeth of Williams's meltdown. Yet Williams held firm elsewhere on the card, and by the round's end he had somehow stretched his overall Green Jacket lead to four shots.

## Alex Baker's Troia debut turns into a two-act horror show, but the Wooden Spoon gap tightens anyway
<!-- storyline: d1 -->

Alex Baker's first visit to Troia turned into a round of two collapses. The trouble started at the 4th, where a triple bogey opened a stretch that bled 13 shots over six holes through to the 9th, including double bogeys at the 1st and 6th stroke-index holes. Baker finally halted the run with a birdie at the 10th, one of only two net gains all round, but the respite did not last. From the 13th to the 17th another 13 shots leaked away, with triple bogeys at the 13th, 14th and 15th — three holes running without a single net par, and the 15th among the hardest on the course. A triple bogey at the 13th also dropped Baker to the bottom of the TEG 16 Wooden Spoon race.

Yet the damage, brutal as it was, did not widen the gap Baker needed to close. The margin to the next-worst player in the Spoon race actually shrank from four shots to three, keeping Baker within touching distance despite the horror show. It was a small mercy from an otherwise miserable Troia debut, with the round standing as Baker's only Troia score to date.

## David Mullin leapfrogs Gregg Williams into outright second as Stuart Neumann's Trophy lead stretches to seven
<!-- storyline: race -->

At Troia in round 2 of the 2023 TEG Trophy, David Mullin moved past Gregg Williams into outright second, as Stuart Neumann's lead in the Trophy standings widened from five shots to seven. Williams, defending a Green Jacket lead that grew from three to four, endured a miserable stretch through the middle of his round. From the 8th to the 11th he went four holes without a net par, a run that included three straight double bogeys from the 9th through the 11th, bleeding six gross shots across those three holes alone. He then suffered a second drought from the 13th to the 15th, three more double bogeys in a row, the last of them coming on the 15th, one of the hardest holes on the course. The collapse did little to loosen his grip on the Jacket, but it was enough to see him overtaken by Mullin in the Trophy race, leaving Neumann clear at the top after the second round.
