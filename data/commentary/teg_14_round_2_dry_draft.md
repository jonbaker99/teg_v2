## OVERVIEW

David Mullin posted 41 Stableford points — best in the field — at Littlestone Golf Club, the 2nd TEG round at this venue. Gregg Williams was worst with 27 points. Mullin extended his Trophy lead from 3 to 9 and his Green Jacket lead from 5 to 12.

---

## THE ROUND

### David Mullin (41 Stableford points)

Mullin entered Round 2 with a 3-point Trophy lead from Royal Cinque Ports.

Mid-round, he ran into a troubled stretch at holes 4, 5, and 6: triple bogey at the par-4 4th (7 gross, one of the hardest on the course — stroke index 2, 0 Stableford points), double bogey at the par-5 5th (7 gross, 1 point), bogey at the par-3 6th (4 gross, 2 points). Six shots over par in three holes.

He answered with a birdie at the par-5 7th (4 gross, 4 Stableford points).

His closing holes included bogeys at H15 (one of the easiest, stroke index 17) and H16 (one of the hardest, stroke index 3), a double bogey at the par-3 17th (5 gross, 1 point), and a birdie at the par-5 18th (4 gross, 4 Stableford points) to close.

Mullin finished on 41 Stableford points — best in the field for the second consecutive round.

### Alex Baker (35 Stableford points)

Extending the pattern of back-nine blowups from Royal Cinque Ports in Round 1, Baker produced a front-nine implosion at Littlestone.

Holes 2 to 5: triple bogey at the par-4 2nd (7 gross, 1 Stableford point), double bogey at the par-4 3rd (6 gross, 1 point), triple bogey at the par-4 4th (7 gross, one of the hardest — stroke index 2, 1 point), quadruple bogey at the par-5 5th (9 gross, one of the easiest — stroke index 16, 0 points). Twelve shots over par in four holes, 3 Stableford points for the stretch.

Baker added a further quadruple bogey at the par-4 8th (8 gross, 0 Stableford points).

He recovered for the closing stretch. Holes 13 to 18: par at the par-4 13th (3 points), bogey at the par-3 14th (2 points), bogey at the par-4 15th (2 points), double bogey at the par-4 16th — one of the hardest, stroke index 3 (2 points), bogey at the par-3 17th (3 points), par at the par-5 18th (3 points). Fifteen Stableford points in six holes without a net-dropped shot.

Baker finished on 35 Stableford points — cumulative Trophy total 72, 9 behind Mullin.

### Jon Baker (29 Stableford points)

Jon Baker scored 29 Stableford points — the same as his Round 1 total — and remains last in the Trophy standings. He remains last in the Wooden Spoon race, though the gap to Williams narrowed from 4 to 2 points.

### Gregg Williams (27 Stableford points)

Williams posted 27 Stableford points — worst in the field this round. He is third in the Trophy on 60 points cumulative. His Wooden Spoon gap over Jon Baker narrowed to 2 points.

---

## RACE SHIFTS

**Trophy.** Mullin extended his lead from 3 to 9. Baker's front-nine disaster (12 shots over par across H2-5, plus a further quad at H8) cost him the points needed to challenge; Mullin's 41-point round, despite his own mid-round wobble, did the rest.

**Green Jacket.** Mullin's R2 gross of 14 over par — best in field — extended the Jacket lead from 5 to 12 shots over Jon Baker. With two rounds remaining, the Jacket lead is substantial.

**Wooden Spoon.** Jon Baker remains last. Williams's worse round (27 pts vs Baker's 29 pts) narrowed the Spoon gap from 4 to 2 points.
